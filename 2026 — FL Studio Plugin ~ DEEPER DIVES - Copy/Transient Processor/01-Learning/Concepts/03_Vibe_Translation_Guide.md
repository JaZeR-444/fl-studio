# 03: Vibe Translation Guide

## Vibe Targets in Transient Processor

### 1. Moody (Dark, Intimate, Distant)
- **The Lever:** **Negative Attack + Long Release.**
- **Setting Moves:**
  - Turn **Attack** to -6dB. This "pushes" the instrument back into the mix.
  - Turn **Release** to +3dB to emphasize the "room" or "atmosphere."
  - Set **Split Freq** high (500Hz) to keep the low-end muddy/warm.
- **What to listen for:** A sound that feels "soft" and "foggy," perfect for background lo-fi layers.
- **Pitfall:** Too much release can create a "wall of noise." Keep it subtle.

### 2. Upbeat (High-Energy, Snappy, Aggressive)
- **The Lever:** **Max Attack + Short Release + Drive.**
- **Setting Moves:**
  - Turn **Attack** to +8dB.
  - Turn **Release** to -40% to make the sound "stop" faster.
  - Turn up **Drive** to 15% for harmonic grit.
- **What to listen for:** A sound that "jumps" out of the speakers and feels physically impactful.
- **Pitfall:** Extreme attack can cause the "Limiter" on your master track to work too hard. Check your levels.

### 3. Psychedelic (Trippy, Pulsing, Sucking)
- **The Lever:** **Automated Release.**
- **Setting Moves:**
  - Right-click the **Release** knob and "Create Automation Clip."
  - Draw a "Sucking" shape (Release going from -100% to +100% over 1 bar).
  - Add a **Delay** *before* the Transient Processor.
- **What to listen for:** A sound that seems to "breathe" or "inhale" rhythmically.
- **Pitfall:** Don't automate the Attack too wildly; it can cause unpleasant "pops" in the audio.

### 4. Jazzy (Organic, Balanced, Real)
- **The Lever:** **Subtle Release Shaping.**
- **Setting Moves:**
  - Use Transient Processor on a live drum break.
  - Set **Attack** to +2dB (just to bring out the stick hit).
  - Set **Release** to -10% to clean up the "muddiness" between hits while keeping the ghost notes.
- **What to listen for:** A "cleaner" version of the original recording that still sounds 100% human.
- **Pitfall:** Avoid the **Drive** knob; keep the signal transparent for a classy jazz feel.

### 5. Vibey (Smooth, Lush, Modern R&B)
- **The Lever:** **Softened Attack + Mix Knob.**
- **Setting Moves:**
  - Set **Attack** to -3dB on keys or vocals.
  - Use **Mix** at 40%.
  - This removes the "harshness" from digital instruments, making them feel like they were recorded on tape.
- **What to listen for:** A "silky" texture where no element is "poking" the ear too hard.
- **Pitfall:** If you soften the attack too much, the track will lose its "rhythm." Only use this on melodic layers.
