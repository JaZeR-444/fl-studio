# Fruity Slicer: Vibe Translation Guide

## Purpose

Translate abstract vibes into concrete production levers for **Fruity Slicer** in Hip-Hop/R&B/Trap production.

**Key Principle:** Vibes aren't magic—they're the sum of deliberate technical decisions.

## The 5 Target Vibes

| Vibe | Core Character | Slicer Role |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| **Moody** | Dark, atmospheric, introspective | Slow, reverberant chops with space |
| **Upbeat** | Energetic, bouncy, forward | Fast, crisp chops with off-beat rhythm |
| **Psychedelic** | Warped, unstable, disorienting | Extreme automation, glitchy artifacts |
| **Jazzy** | Smooth, swung, sophisticated | Clean chops with heavy swing (58-66%) |
| **Vibey** | Smooth, groovy, textural | Medium chops with consistent feel |

## VIBE 1: MOODY 🌙

### Production Definition
**Moody =** Dark tonality + slow movement + space + reverberant depth + minor harmony + subdued energy

### Slicer Levers (10 Actionable Moves)

#### 1. Sample Selection
**Lever:** Choose inherently dark sources
- Minor key vocals or melodic loops
- Low-pitch instruments (bass, cello, low vocals)
- Degraded/lo-fi textures (vinyl crackle, tape hiss)
- Ambient pads or sustained tones

**What to Listen For:** Sample feels melancholic even before slicing

---

#### 2. Threshold Setting (Conservative)
**Lever:** 55-70% (fewer, longer slices)
- **Why:** Moody needs space, not density
- **Target:** 6-10 slices per 2-bar loop (sparse)

**What to Listen For:** Each slice has room to breathe

---

#### 3. Crossfade (Smooth)
**Lever:** 15-30 ms (medium-long)
- **Why:** Smooth transitions = less energy = more moody
- **Avoid:** Short crossfades (too percussive)

**What to Listen For:** No audible clicks or harsh cuts

---

#### 4. Time Stretch (Slower)
**Lever:** 0.85-0.95x (slight slowdown)
- **Why:** Slower = more contemplative
- **Extreme:** 0.7x for very moody sections

**What to Listen For:** Sample feels dragged, not rushed

---

#### 5. Pitch Shifting (Lower)
**Lever:** -2 to -5 semitones
- **Why:** Lower pitch = darker tonality
- **Stack With:** Reverb for depth

**What to Listen For:** Sample sits in low-mid range (200-800 Hz emphasis)

---

#### 6. MIDI Pattern (Sparse, Sustained)
**Lever:** Long notes, minimal density
- **Example:** 4 notes per bar (whole notes or half notes)
- **Avoid:** Fast 1/16th patterns

**Pattern Example (1 bar):**
```
Beat 1: Slice 2 (whole note, velocity 70)
Beat 3: Slice 5 (half note, velocity 65)
Beat 4.5: Slice 1 (quarter note, velocity 60)
```

**What to Listen For:** Pattern feels like breathing, not rhythmic

---

#### 7. Velocity Programming (Low, Consistent)
**Lever:** 55-75 velocity range (subdued)
- **Why:** Low velocity = less aggressive = more moody
- **Avoid:** High velocity (90+) or extreme dynamics

**What to Listen For:** Chops sit back in the mix (not in-your-face)

---

#### 8. FX Chain (Reverb + Darkness)
**Post-Slicer FX:**
- **Reverb:** Decay 2-4 seconds, 40-60% mix (spacious)
- **LPF:** Cut above 8-10 kHz (remove brightness)
- **Subtle Saturation:** Warmth without aggression

**What to Listen For:** Chops sound distant, atmospheric (not close/dry)

---

#### 9. Automation (Slow, Gradual)
**Lever:** Automate pitch or time-stretch slowly
- **Example:** Pitch -2 semitones → -5 semitones over 8 bars
- **Avoid:** Fast, jarring automation

**What to Listen For:** Vibe shifts subtly (listener doesn't notice exact moment)

---

#### 10. Stereo Placement (Wide, Ambient)
**Lever:** Pan slices L/R, add stereo reverb
- **Example:** Slice 1 = 30% L, Slice 2 = 30% R
- **Why:** Width = space = moody

**What to Listen For:** Chops surround listener (not mono/centered)

---

### What to Listen For: Moody Validation

✅ **Correct Moody Feel:**
- Dark tonality (minor, low-pitched)
- Slow, breathing rhythm
- Spacious (reverb, gaps in pattern)
- Smooth transitions (no harsh cuts)
- Subdued energy (not aggressive)
- Sits back in mix (not upfront)

❌ **Not Moody (Fix It):**
- Bright tonality → Lower pitch, cut highs
- Fast rhythm → Reduce MIDI density
- Dry/close sound → Add reverb
- Harsh cuts → Increase crossfade
- Aggressive energy → Lower velocities

---

### Moody Don'ts (2 Critical Mistakes)

❌ **DON'T: Use short crossfades (under 10 ms)**  
**Why:** Creates percussive, energetic cuts (opposite of moody)  
**Fix:** Increase to 15-30 ms

❌ **DON'T: Create dense MIDI patterns (1/16th notes)**  
**Why:** Density = energy = not moody  
**Fix:** Sparse patterns (4-8 notes per bar max)

---

## VIBE 2: UPBEAT ⚡

### Production Definition
**Upbeat =** Fast tempo + crisp transients + off-beat rhythm + bright tonality + forward energy + bounce

### Slicer Levers (10 Actionable Moves)

#### 1. Sample Selection
**Lever:** Choose bright, energetic sources
- High-pitched vocals or melodic loops
- Crisp transients (percussion, stabs)
- Short duration (1-2 bars ideal)
- Major key or bright harmonies

**What to Listen For:** Sample has inherent energy

---

#### 2. Threshold Setting (Aggressive)
**Lever:** 30-45% (many tight slices)
- **Why:** More slices = more bounce potential
- **Target:** 12-20 slices per 2-bar loop (dense)

**What to Listen For:** Waveform is heavily sliced (lots of markers)

---

#### 3. Crossfade (Crisp)
**Lever:** 3-8 ms (short)
- **Why:** Crisp transients = energetic
- **Avoid:** Long crossfades (too smooth)

**What to Listen For:** Each slice has a clear attack (punchy)

---

#### 4. Time Stretch (Faster)
**Lever:** 1.1-1.3x (speed up)
- **Why:** Faster = more energetic
- **Extreme:** 1.5x for very upbeat sections

**What to Listen For:** Sample feels rushed, exciting (not laid-back)

---

#### 5. Pitch Shifting (Higher)
**Lever:** +2 to +5 semitones
- **Why:** Higher pitch = brighter = more energetic
- **Stack With:** Compression for punch

**What to Listen For:** Sample sits in high-mid range (2-5 kHz emphasis)

---

#### 6. MIDI Pattern (Dense, Off-Beat)
**Lever:** Fast rhythm with off-beat emphasis
- **Example:** 16+ notes per bar (1/16th or 1/8th)
- **Critical:** Off-beat hits create bounce

**Pattern Example (1 bar):**
```
Beat 1: Slice 3 (velocity 110) [on-beat]
Beat 1.5: Slice 7 (velocity 90) [off-beat = bounce]
Beat 2: Slice 5 (velocity 105) [on-beat]
Beat 2.75: Slice 2 (velocity 85) [off-beat = bounce]
Beat 3: Slice 8 (velocity 110) [on-beat]
Beat 3.5: Slice 1 (velocity 95) [off-beat = bounce]
Beat 4: Slice 6 (velocity 105) [on-beat]
Beat 4.75: Slice 4 (velocity 90) [off-beat = bounce]
```

**What to Listen For:** Off-beat hits feel like a "rebound"

---

#### 7. Velocity Programming (High, Dynamic)
**Lever:** 85-115 velocity range (aggressive)
- **Why:** High velocity = more energy
- **Accent Pattern:** Main hits 105-115, off-beats 80-95

**What to Listen For:** Chops are loud and punchy (upfront in mix)

---

#### 8. FX Chain (Compression + Brightness)
**Post-Slicer FX:**
- **Compression:** 4:1 ratio, fast attack (5-10 ms), adds punch
- **HPF:** Cut below 100 Hz (focus on mids/highs)
- **Slight Saturation:** Adds brightness and excitement

**What to Listen For:** Chops are glued together but still punchy

---

#### 9. Automation (Fast, Energetic)
**Lever:** Automate pitch upward in builds
- **Example:** Pitch +2 semitones → +7 semitones over 4 bars
- **Result:** Energy builds into drop

**What to Listen For:** Obvious energy increase (listener feels anticipation)

---

#### 10. Stereo Placement (Wide Bounces)
**Lever:** Alternate L/R on off-beat slices
- **Example:** On-beats = center, off-beats = L/R alternating
- **Why:** Stereo bounce = more exciting

**What to Listen For:** Bounce pattern moves in stereo field

---

### What to Listen For: Upbeat Validation

✅ **Correct Upbeat Feel:**
- Bright tonality (high-pitched, crisp)
- Fast, bouncy rhythm (off-beat emphasis)
- Crisp transients (punchy attacks)
- Forward energy (in-your-face)
- Compressed and glued together
- Wide stereo bounce

❌ **Not Upbeat (Fix It):**
- Dark tonality → Raise pitch, boost highs
- Slow rhythm → Increase MIDI density
- Smooth transitions → Reduce crossfade
- Laid-back energy → Increase velocities
- Sounds mushed → Adjust compression attack

---

### Upbeat Don'ts (2 Critical Mistakes)

❌ **DON'T: Use long crossfades (over 15 ms)**  
**Why:** Smooths transients, kills energy  
**Fix:** Reduce to 3-8 ms

❌ **DON'T: Neglect off-beat emphasis**  
**Why:** No off-beats = no bounce = not upbeat  
**Fix:** Place slices on 1.5, 2.75, 3.5, 4.75 beats

## VIBE 3: PSYCHEDELIC 🌀

### Production Definition
**Psychedelic =** Warped tonality + unpredictable rhythm + artifacts/glitches + extreme automation + disorienting

### Slicer Levers (10 Actionable Moves)

#### 1. Sample Selection
**Lever:** Any source (will be destroyed)
- Doesn't matter—psychedelic processing transforms everything
- Complex sources (vocals, melodies) = more interesting destruction

**What to Listen For:** Original character (will disappear after processing)

---

#### 2. Threshold Setting (Extreme)
**Lever:** 10-25% (50+ micro-slices)
- **Why:** More slices = more glitch potential
- **Target:** 30-60 tiny slices per 2-bar loop

**What to Listen For:** Waveform is covered in slice markers (overwhelming)

---

#### 3. Crossfade (Zero = Glitchy)
**Lever:** 0 ms (intentional artifacts)
- **Why:** Clicks and pops = psychedelic character
- **Embrace:** Artifacts are desired, not mistakes

**What to Listen For:** Obvious clicks between slices (glitchy)

---

#### 4. Time Stretch (Extreme, Automated)
**Lever:** 0.3x to 3x (wild range)
- **Critical:** AUTOMATE constantly
- **Pattern:** 1.0x → 0.5x → 2.0x → 0.7x (erratic)

**What to Listen For:** Sample warps unpredictably (unstable feel)

---

#### 5. Pitch Shifting (Random, Extreme)
**Lever:** -12 to +12 semitones (full range)
- **Critical:** RANDOMIZE or automate chaotically
- **Pattern:** 0 → +7 → -5 → +12 → -8 (disorienting)

**What to Listen For:** Sample is unrecognizable (completely warped)

---

#### 6. MIDI Pattern (Chaotic, Stuttering)
**Lever:** Erratic rhythm with stutter bursts
- **Mix:** Sparse sections + dense stutter sections
- **Example:** 1/64th note bursts + whole rests

**Pattern Example (2 bars):**
```
Bar 1:
  Beats 1-1.5: Slice 3 (1/32nd × 16) [stutter]
  Beat 2: Slice 7 (single hit)
  Beats 2.5-3: Slice 1 (1/64th × 8) [rapid glitch]
  Beat 4: Rest
Bar 2:
  Beat 1: Slice 12 (whole note, sustain)
  Beats 2-2.25: Slice 5 (1/32nd × 8)
  Beat 3.5-4: Slice 9 (1/16th × 8)
```

**What to Listen For:** Unpredictable rhythm (surprises listener)

---

#### 7. Velocity Programming (Randomized)
**Lever:** 40-127 range (extreme dynamics)
- **Use:** FL Studio's randomize function
- **Why:** Unpredictable = psychedelic

**What to Listen For:** Volume jumps unexpectedly

---

#### 8. FX Chain (Crushed Reverb + Distortion)
**Post-Slicer FX:**
- **Bit-Crusher:** 8-bit or lower (degradation)
- **Reverb:** 80-100% wet, short decay (0.5-1 sec, unnatural)
- **Ping-Pong Delay:** 70-80% feedback (cascading chaos)
- **Distortion:** 50-70% drive (parallel mix)

**What to Listen For:** Sample sounds destroyed but controlled

---

#### 9. Automation (Everything, Constantly)
**Lever:** Automate ALL parameters
- **Time Stretch:** Bar-by-bar changes
- **Pitch:** Random automation every 1/4 note
- **Crossfade:** Toggle 0 ms ↔ 20 ms for texture shifts

**What to Listen For:** Nothing stays stable (constant change)

---

#### 10. Resample Cascade (Advanced)
**Lever:** Layer multiple generations
1. Create psychedelic pattern (Generation 1)
2. Render to audio
3. Load into NEW Slicer, apply MORE extreme settings
4. Render again (Generation 2)
5. Repeat (Generation 3)
6. Layer all 3 generations

**What to Listen For:** Impossibly complex, layered chaos

---

### What to Listen For: Psychedelic Validation

✅ **Correct Psychedelic Feel:**
- Obvious glitches and artifacts (clicks, pops)
- Warped, degraded tonality
- Unpredictable rhythm (surprises)
- Disorienting (unstable character)
- Controlled chaos (not total randomness)
- Heavy FX processing

❌ **Not Psychedelic (Fix It):**
- Too clean → Add bit-crushing, zero crossfade
- Too predictable → Randomize automation
- Too stable → Extreme time-stretch changes
- Too dry → Crushed reverb + delay

---

### Psychedelic Don'ts (2 Critical Mistakes)

❌ **DON'T: Avoid extremes (trying to stay "musical")**  
**Why:** Psychedelic requires commitment to chaos  
**Fix:** Push settings to extremes (0.3x time-stretch, ±12 pitch)

❌ **DON'T: Repeat same chaos pattern**  
**Why:** Gets boring after 8 bars  
**Fix:** Automate chaos amount (build/release cycles)

## VIBE 4: JAZZY 🎷

### Production Definition
**Jazzy =** Heavy swing (58-66%) + clean chops + vintage warmth + sophisticated harmony + relaxed tempo

### Slicer Levers (10 Actionable Moves)

#### 1. Sample Selection
**Lever:** Choose jazz-influenced sources
- Jazz samples (piano, bass, horns)
- Complex harmony (7th chords, extensions)
- Vintage recordings (vinyl, tape)
- Smooth melodic content

**What to Listen For:** Sample has harmonic sophistication

---

#### 2. Threshold Setting (Moderate, Clean)
**Lever:** 50-65% (clean detection)
- **Why:** Jazzy requires musicality, not chaos
- **Target:** 8-14 slices per 2-bar loop

**What to Listen For:** Slices align with musical phrases

---

#### 3. Crossfade (Medium-Smooth)
**Lever:** 10-15 ms
- **Why:** Smooth but not mushy
- **Balance:** Preserves transients but no harshness

**What to Listen For:** Natural transitions (not clicks, not over-smoothed)

---

#### 4. Time Stretch (Original or Slightly Slower)
**Lever:** 0.95-1.0x (respect original tempo)
- **Why:** Jazzy is laid-back, not rushed
- **Avoid:** Speeding up (kills jazzy vibe)

**What to Listen For:** Groove feels natural (not forced)

---

#### 5. Pitch Shifting (Minimal or None)
**Lever:** 0 to -2 semitones
- **Why:** Preserve original harmony
- **Slight Lower:** For warmth, but don't overdo

**What to Listen For:** Harmony stays intact (musical)

---

#### 6. MIDI Pattern (Swung, Musical)
**Lever:** Musical phrases with HEAVY swing
- **Critical:** Apply 58-66% swing (J Dilla territory)
- **Density:** Moderate (8-12 notes per bar)

**Pattern Example (1 bar):**
```
Beat 1: Slice 2 (quarter note, velocity 75)
Beat 1.67: Slice 5 (eighth note, velocity 70) [swung late]
Beat 2.5: Slice 3 (quarter note, velocity 78)
Beat 3: Slice 7 (quarter note, velocity 72)
Beat 3.83: Slice 1 (eighth note, velocity 68) [swung late]
```

**What to Listen For:** "Drunk" feel (laid-back, off-grid)

---

#### 7. Velocity Programming (Natural Dynamics)
**Lever:** 65-85 range (moderate, human)
- **Why:** Jazzy feels played, not programmed
- **Accents:** Subtle (5-10 velocity difference)

**What to Listen For:** Dynamics feel natural (breathing)

---

#### 8. FX Chain (Vintage Processing)
**Post-Slicer FX:**
- **Tape Saturation:** Subtle warmth (20-30% drive)
- **Vintage EQ:** Boost 200 Hz, 3 kHz (warmth + presence)
- **Light Compression:** 2:1 ratio, slow attack (30 ms)
- **LPF:** Cut above 12 kHz (bandwidth limiting)

**What to Listen For:** Warm, vintage character (not digital)

---

#### 9. Automation (Subtle, Musical)
**Lever:** Automate filter sweeps or reverb
- **Example:** LPF sweep in intro (500 Hz → 12 kHz)
- **Avoid:** Extreme or chaotic automation

**What to Listen For:** Automation is musical (supports vibe)

---

#### 10. Stereo Placement (Natural Width)
**Lever:** Moderate panning (not extreme)
- **Example:** Main elements center, textures 20-30% L/R
- **Why:** Jazzy is balanced, not exaggerated

**What to Listen For:** Natural stereo image (like a jazz ensemble)

---

### What to Listen For: Jazzy Validation

✅ **Correct Jazzy Feel:**
- Heavy swing (obvious laid-back feel)
- Clean, musical chops
- Vintage warmth (tape/vinyl character)
- Sophisticated harmony (not simple)
- Moderate tempo (not rushed)
- Natural dynamics

❌ **Not Jazzy (Fix It):**
- Robotic rhythm → Apply 60%+ swing
- Harsh/digital → Add vintage processing
- Simple harmony → Choose better sample
- Too fast → Slow down or reduce density

---

### Jazzy Don'ts (2 Critical Mistakes)

❌ **DON'T: Neglect swing (staying on-grid)**  
**Why:** No swing = no jazzy vibe  
**Fix:** Apply 58-66% swing in Piano Roll quantize

❌ **DON'T: Over-process (too many FX)**  
**Why:** Kills natural, organic feel  
**Fix:** Subtle vintage processing only

## VIBE 5: VIBEY 🌊

### Production Definition
**Vibey =** Smooth texture + consistent groove + moderate tempo + warm tonality + relaxed but engaging

### Slicer Levers (10 Actionable Moves)

#### 1. Sample Selection
**Lever:** Choose smooth, groovy sources
- Warm pads, smooth vocals, mellow melodies
- Medium complexity (not too simple, not chaotic)
- Consistent tonality (not jarring)

**What to Listen For:** Sample already has "vibe" quality

---

#### 2. Threshold Setting (Balanced)
**Lever:** 45-60% (moderate slicing)
- **Why:** Vibey is balanced (not sparse, not excessive)
- **Target:** 10-16 slices per 2-bar loop

**What to Listen For:** Clean slices, musical phrasing

---

#### 3. Crossfade (Medium)
**Lever:** 8-15 ms
- **Why:** Smooth without losing definition
- **Balance:** Natural transitions

**What to Listen For:** Seamless flow (not choppy, not mushed)

---

#### 4. Time Stretch (Original or Subtle)
**Lever:** 0.95-1.05x (minimal change)
- **Why:** Vibey respects original groove
- **Subtle Adjustments:** For tempo matching only

**What to Listen For:** Groove feels natural

---

#### 5. Pitch Shifting (Warmth Zone)
**Lever:** 0 to -3 semitones
- **Why:** Slight lowering adds warmth (vibey)
- **Avoid:** Higher pitch (too bright/energetic)

**What to Listen For:** Warm, full-bodied tone

---

#### 6. MIDI Pattern (Consistent Groove)
**Lever:** Steady rhythm with moderate density
- **Density:** 8-14 notes per bar
- **Consistency:** Repeating pattern (hypnotic)

**Pattern Example (1 bar):**
```
Beat 1: Slice 3 (velocity 75)
Beat 1.5: Slice 7 (velocity 70)
Beat 2: Slice 5 (velocity 78)
Beat 2.5: Slice 2 (velocity 72)
Beat 3: Slice 8 (velocity 75)
Beat 3.5: Slice 4 (velocity 73)
Beat 4: Slice 6 (velocity 77)
Beat 4.5: Slice 1 (velocity 71)
```

**What to Listen For:** Hypnotic, consistent groove (not varying wildly)

---

#### 7. Velocity Programming (Consistent, Subtle)
**Lever:** 70-85 range (narrow, smooth)
- **Why:** Consistency = vibey (not dynamic extremes)
- **Variation:** 5-8 velocity difference max

**What to Listen For:** Even, flowing dynamics

---

#### 8. FX Chain (Warm Depth)
**Post-Slicer FX:**
- **Reverb:** Medium decay (1.5-2.5 sec), 30-40% mix
- **Chorus/Phaser:** Subtle modulation (adds movement)
- **Tape Saturation:** Warmth (15-25% drive)
- **Light Compression:** Glue (3:1 ratio)

**What to Listen For:** Warm, spacious, slightly modulated

---

#### 9. Automation (Smooth, Gradual)
**Lever:** Slow filter or reverb automation
- **Example:** Reverb mix 30% → 50% over 8 bars
- **Avoid:** Fast or dramatic changes

**What to Listen For:** Vibe deepens gradually (subtle)

---

#### 10. Stereo Placement (Balanced Width)
**Lever:** Moderate stereo spread
- **Example:** Main chops center, textures 20-40% L/R
- **Add:** Stereo chorus for width

**What to Listen For:** Enveloping but not extreme

---

### What to Listen For: Vibey Validation

✅ **Correct Vibey Feel:**
- Smooth, warm tonality
- Consistent, hypnotic groove
- Moderate tempo (not slow, not fast)
- Spacious but controlled
- Relaxed but engaging (not boring)
- Warm FX processing

❌ **Not Vibey (Fix It):**
- Too harsh → Add warmth (lower pitch, saturation)
- Too varied → Consistency in pattern and velocity
- Too dry → Add reverb and chorus
- Too energetic → Reduce tempo and velocity

---

### Vibey Don'ts (2 Critical Mistakes)

❌ **DON'T: Use extreme settings (trying to be exciting)**  
**Why:** Vibey is about subtlety and consistency  
**Fix:** Keep all settings moderate (no extremes)

❌ **DON'T: Vary the pattern too much**  
**Why:** Vibey requires hypnotic repetition  
**Fix:** Lock in a 1-2 bar loop, repeat with minimal variation
