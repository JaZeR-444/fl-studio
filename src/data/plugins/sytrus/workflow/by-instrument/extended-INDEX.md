# Sytrus Extended By-Instrument Workflows

## Overview
Sytrus excels at creating complex, genre-defining sounds through its FM and subtractive synthesis capabilities. This extended guide provides comprehensive workflows for hip-hop, trap, and EDM production.

|-----------|---------|

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues
- **No Low-End**: Increase carrier level, check filter cutoff
- **Too Harsh**: Reduce FM, increase filter cutoff
- **No Definition**: Increase resonance slightly
- **Muddy Sound**: High-pass at 40Hz, reduce lows
- **Weak Attack**: Decrease envelope attack time

### Solutions
- **Frequency-Specific**: Adjust individual operator settings
- **Dynamic Control**: Modify FM and envelope parameters
- **Level Management**: Optimize gain staging
- **Processing Order**: Reorganize effects chain
