# Quick Reference: Kick Design Workflows

A step-by-step process for building sounds in BassDrum. [SRC: IL-MAN]

## 1. Initializing (The Clean Slate)
- Right-click the plugin header and select **Reset**.
- You will hear a basic, clean sub-kick.

## 2. Setting the Tone
- Adjust the **End Frequency** to match your song's key.
- Adjust the **Start Frequency** until you hear the desired amount of "chest punch."

## 3. Defining the Punch
- Tweak the **Pitch Decay**. 
- *Fast*: Creates a short "clicky" hit.
- *Slow*: Creates a "laser" or 808 sound.

## 4. Layering the Attack
- Go to the **SAMPLE** tab.
- Choose a click that complements your mix.
- Use the **High Pass Filter** to ensure the sample doesn't interfere with the sub-bass.

## 5. Adding the Grit
- Go to the **DRIVE** tab.
- Use "Soft" for R&B/Pop; use "Hard" or "Clip" for aggressive Trap.
- **Pro Tip**: Use the **Master Level** to ensure your new, distorted kick isn't red-lining the mixer.
