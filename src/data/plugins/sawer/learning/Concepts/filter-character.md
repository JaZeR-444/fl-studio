# Sawer Filter Character

## Summary
Sawer uses an analog-modeled filter that can be clean or gritty depending on resonance. The filter supports LP 12/24 dB, BP, and HP modes, with self-oscillation behavior at high resonance. The manual highlights its "bright and clean" behavior at low resonance and "gritty and grungy" behavior when resonance is introduced.

## What to Document
- LP12 vs LP24 slope behavior on identical patches.
- Resonance settings where the filter begins to self-oscillate.
- KBD Track and VEL Track influence on bright vs dark notes.

## Application Guidance
- Bass patches: LP24, moderate resonance, reduced unison width.
- Lead patches: LP12 or BP, higher resonance, sync on.
- Texture patches: higher resonance + noise blend.
