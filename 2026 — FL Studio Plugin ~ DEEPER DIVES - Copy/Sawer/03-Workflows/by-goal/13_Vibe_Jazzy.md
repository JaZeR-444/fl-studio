# Goal: Vibe - Jazzy

## Goal Statement
Create warm, sophisticated "Soviet Rhodes" keys for jazzy R&B.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Oscillator:** Saw 40%, Sub Level 20% (Subtle).
2. **Filter:** LP 12dB. Cutoff 45%. Resonance 0%.
3. **Envelopes:** Amp Release 600ms.
4. **Matrix:** Velocity -> Filter Cutoff (40%).
5. **Vibrato:** Slow, shallow LFO -> Pitch (1%).
6. **Acoustics:** Chorus ON, Reverb Mix 10%.

## What to Listen For
- The "mellow" tone when playing softly.
- The "analog warmth" of the 12dB filter.
- A sound that complements a real bass guitar.
