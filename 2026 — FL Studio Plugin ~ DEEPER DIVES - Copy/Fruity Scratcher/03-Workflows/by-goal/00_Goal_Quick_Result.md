# Fruity Scratcher — Goal: Quick Result

## Workflow Goal
Get a usable scratch effect in **under 5 minutes** for beginners or rapid production.

---

## Workflow 1: Classic Vocal Scratch (Boom-Bap Style)

### Time: 3 minutes

### Routing Context
- **Insert:** Scratcher on its own channel (not on another instrument)
- **Signal path:** Sample Scratcher → Mixer track → Master

### Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Load Scratcher**
   - Add new channel → Fruity Scratcher

2. **Load vocal sample**
   - Drag a short vocal stab into Scratcher ("Hey!", "Fresh!", "Yeah!")
   - Sample length: 0.5-2 seconds
   - **Quick sample sources:** Splice, Loopcloud, or record your own "Hey!"

3. **Set parameters (Universal Safe Starting Point)**
   - SPD: 1x (100%)
   - ACC: 75%
   - SEN: 55%
   - Hold: OFF
   - REV: OFF

4. **Test scratch**
   - Click and drag the platter with mouse
   - Forward-back-forward motion = baby scratch
   - Should feel responsive but controlled

5. **Add to mix**
   - Scratch on downbeats or between kick/snare hits
   - 1-2 scratches per 8 bars (don't overdo it)

### Starting Settings

| Parameter | Value | Why |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| SPD | 1x | Normal pitch (matches track key) |
| ACC | 75% | Balanced responsiveness |
| SEN | 55% | Moderate mouse sensitivity |
| Sample | Vocal stab, 1-2 sec | Classic boom-bap material |

### What to Listen For
- [ ] Clean transient at start of each scratch
- [ ] Pitch matches your track's key
- [ ] No digital artifacts or glitching
- [ ] Scratch feels rhythmic and on-beat

### Variations

**Variation 1: Faster Scratch**
- Raise SEN to 65% for quicker response

**Variation 2: Pitched Up**
- Set SPD to 1.2x for brighter, more energetic scratch

### Pitfalls + Fixes
- **Scratch feels twitchy:** Lower SEN to 45%
- **Scratch feels slow:** Raise ACC to 85%
- **Sample glitches:** Shorten sample to < 1 second

### Automation Ideas
1. **Pitch rise:** Automate SPD from 1x to 1.3x during scratch
2. **Volume swell:** Automate channel volume during scratch

**Result:** Boom-bap vocal scratch ready in 3 minutes.

## Workflow 2: Tape Stop Effect (Trap/EDM)

### Time: 4 minutes

### Routing Context
- **Insert:** Scratcher on drum bus or full beat channel
- **Signal path:** Beat/Drum Bus → Scratcher → Mixer track → Master

### Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Load Scratcher on drum bus**
   - Route drums to mixer track
   - Add Scratcher to that track's insert slot

2. **Load beat sample (or route live drums)**
   - Option A: Load a drum loop into Scratcher
   - Option B: Route live drums through Scratcher (use "hold" source)

3. **Set parameters for gradual tape stop**
   - SPD: 1x (will automate)
   - ACC: 30% (low = gradual stop)
   - SEN: N/A (not scratching manually)

4. **Create automation clip**
   - Right-click SPD knob → Create automation clip
   - Draw curve from 1x (100%) to 0x (0%) over 2 bars

5. **Adjust curve**
   - Use curved automation (not straight line)
   - Should decelerate gradually, not linearly

6. **Test playback**
   - Play through automation
   - Stop should feel smooth and natural

### Starting Settings

| Parameter | Value | Why |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| SPD | 1x → 0x (automated) | Decelerate to silence |
| ACC | 30% | Gradual, realistic stop |
| Duration | 2 bars (4-8 beats) | Classic tape stop length |

### What to Listen For
- [ ] Smooth pitch drop (not stepped)
- [ ] Reaches silence exactly at target downbeat
- [ ] Feels "heavy" and emotional (not bouncy)
- [ ] Low frequencies dominate as it slows

### Variations

**Variation 1: Quick Tape Stop (Pre-Drop)**
- ACC = 90%
- Duration = 1 beat
- Creates aggressive "brake" effect

**Variation 2: Reverse Start After Stop**
- After reaching 0x, automate SPD from 0x to -1x (reverse)
- Creates backspin into next section

### Pitfalls + Fixes
- **Stop sounds too fast:** Lower ACC to 20-25%
- **Stop sounds too slow:** Raise ACC to 50-60%
- **Doesn't reach silence:** Ensure automation ends at exactly 0x (0%)

### Automation Ideas
1. **Dynamic ACC:** Automate ACC from 30% to 10% during stop (gets slower as it goes)
2. **Filter combo:** Pair with Parametric EQ 2, automate low-pass closing during stop

**Result:** Trap/EDM tape stop ready in 4 minutes.

## Workflow 3: Pitch-Down Vocal Effect (R&B/Moody)

### Time: 2 minutes

### Routing Context
- **Insert:** Scratcher on vocal ad-lib channel
- **Signal path:** Vocal sample → Scratcher → Reverb 2 (insert) → Mixer track → Master

### Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Load Scratcher with vocal ad-lib**
   - Sample: "Yeah," "Ooh," or sung phrase (2-4 sec)

2. **Set parameters for pitch-down**
   - SPD: 0.7x (30% slower = deeper pitch)
   - ACC: 65%
   - SEN: 55%

3. **Add reverb**
   - Insert Fruity Reverb 2 after Scratcher
   - Mix: 40-50%
   - Decay: 2-3 seconds

4. **Manual or automated scratch**
   - Option A: Manually scratch on hook/ad-lib sections
   - Option B: Automate SPD to drop pitch at end of phrase

5. **Place in mix**
   - Use sparingly (1-2 times per song)
   - Creates moody, emotional moment

### Starting Settings

| Parameter | Value | Why |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| SPD | 0.7x | Pitch down for dark, heavy feel |
| ACC | 65% | Moderate response |
| FX | Reverb 2 (40% mix) | Depth and space |

### What to Listen For
- [ ] Vocal sounds deeper, more haunting
- [ ] Reverb adds emotional depth
- [ ] Fits in minor-key context
- [ ] Not overused (maintains impact)

### Variations

**Variation 1: Extreme Pitch-Down**
- SPD = 0.4x to 0.5x (very deep, almost bass-like)

**Variation 2: Automated Pitch Drop**
- Automate SPD from 1x to 0.6x over last word of phrase

### Pitfalls + Fixes
- **Too dark/muddy:** Raise SPD to 0.8x
- **Sounds harsh:** Add Parametric EQ 2, cut 2-4 kHz

### Automation Ideas
1. **Gradual drop:** Automate SPD from 1x to 0.5x during a vocal phrase
2. **Wobble:** Subtle LFO on SPD (0.65x to 0.75x) for unstable feel

**Result:** Moody pitch-down vocal effect ready in 2 minutes.

## Workflow 4: Reverse Atmosphere (Vibey/Psychedelic)

### Time: 3 minutes

### Routing Context
- **Insert:** Scratcher on its own channel or pad bus
- **Signal path:** Sample → Scratcher → Reverb 2 → Delay 3 → Mixer track → Master

### Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Load Scratcher with sustained sound**
   - Sample: Pad, vocal hum, ambient texture (5-10 sec)

2. **Set parameters for reverse**
   - SPD: -0.7x (reverse at 70% speed)
   - ACC: 45%
   - SEN: N/A (not scratching, just playing reversed)

3. **Add FX chain**
   - Insert 1: Fruity Reverb 2 (60% mix, 4-sec decay)
   - Insert 2: Fruity Delay 3 (ping-pong, 30% mix)

4. **Let it play**
   - No manual scratching—just reverse playback
   - Creates eerie, ghostly background texture

5. **Place in mix**
   - Low in the mix (background element)
   - Use in intros, verses, or breaks

### Starting Settings

| Parameter | Value | Why |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| SPD | -0.7x | Slow reverse for haunting feel |
| ACC | 45% | Smooth reverse playback |
| FX | Reverb (60%) + Delay (30%) | Creates space and depth |

### What to Listen For
- [ ] Smooth reverse texture (not jarring)
- [ ] Blends into background
- [ ] Creates "floating" or "ghostly" atmosphere
- [ ] Doesn't distract from main elements

### Variations

**Variation 1: Slow-Motion Reverse**
- SPD = -0.3x (very slow, "drowning" effect)

**Variation 2: Reverse Swell**
- Automate SPD from 0x to -1x over 4 beats (reverse build-up)

### Pitfalls + Fixes
- **Too loud/distracting:** Lower channel volume to -12 to -18 dB
- **Sounds dry:** Increase reverb mix to 70-80%

### Automation Ideas
1. **Fading reverse:** Automate SPD from -1x to -0.2x over 8 bars (slows down gradually)
2. **Filter sweep:** Automate low-pass filter opening during reverse

**Result:** Vibey reverse atmosphere ready in 3 minutes.

## Workflow 5: Syncopated Hi-Hat Cuts (Upbeat/Trap)

### Time: 5 minutes

### Routing Context
- **Insert:** Scratcher on hi-hat sample channel
- **Signal path:** Hi-hat sample → Scratcher → Mixer track → Master

### Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Load hi-hat or short percussion sample**
   - Sample: Closed hi-hat, open hi-hat, or shaker (< 0.5 sec)

2. **Set parameters for tight cuts**
   - SPD: 1x
   - ACC: 90%
   - SEN: 75%

3. **Scratch on off-beats**
   - Between kick and snare hits
   - Creates syncopation and rhythmic interest

4. **Record performance**
   - Route Scratcher channel to mixer track
   - Arm for recording
   - Record scratch performance
   - Result: Audio clip of scratch pattern

5. **Loop or repeat**
   - Use recorded clip throughout track

### Starting Settings

| Parameter | Value | Why |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| SPD | 1x | Normal speed for clarity |
| ACC | 90% | High responsiveness for tight cuts |
| SEN | 75% | Sensitive for rapid scratching |
| Sample | Hi-hat, < 0.5 sec | Percussive, rhythmic |

### What to Listen For
- [ ] Scratches are tight and percussive
- [ ] Adds syncopation (not on-beat)
- [ ] Complements drums, doesn't clash
- [ ] Creates rhythmic interest

### Variations

**Variation 1: Transformer Scratch**
- Use Hold button to cut rhythm (tap Hold in time)

**Variation 2: Pitched-Up Hi-Hat**
- SPD = 1.3x for brighter, more energetic cuts

### Pitfalls + Fixes
- **Scratches drift off-beat:** Practice with metronome, re-record
- **Too aggressive:** Lower SEN to 65%

### Automation Ideas
1. **Build-up:** Increase scratch density before drop
2. **Filter sweep:** Automate high-pass filter during scratches

**Result:** Upbeat syncopated hi-hat cuts ready in 5 minutes.
