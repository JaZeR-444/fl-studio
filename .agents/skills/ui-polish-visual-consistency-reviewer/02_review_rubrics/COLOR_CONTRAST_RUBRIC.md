# Color, Contrast, Borders & Shadows Rubric

Use this rubric to evaluate color usage, contrast, and depth.

## 1. Accent Color Usage
- **Focus:** Is there a single, primary accent color?
- **Competition:** Are multiple bright colors fighting for attention? (Recommend keeping one primary brand color and making the rest neutral).
- **Interactive State:** Do interactive elements clearly utilize the accent color?

## 2. Contrast & Readability
- **Text:** Does text have sufficient contrast against its background? (Recommend checking WCAG guidelines mentally—light gray text on white backgrounds often fails).
- **Dark Mode:** In dark mode, are pure blacks (`#000000`) avoided for large backgrounds? (Recommend very dark grays like `bg-gray-900` or `bg-slate-950`).

## 3. Background Separations
- **Alternating Sections:** Are subtle background color shifts (e.g., white to very light gray) used to separate long scrolling sections?
- **Card Contrast:** Do cards stand out from the page background? (Either via shadow, border, or a slight background color difference).

## 4. Gradients & Glows
- **Subtlety:** Are gradients smooth and professional? Avoid harsh, banded gradients.
- **Glow Effects:** If used, are they subtle and constrained to specific accents or focal points?
