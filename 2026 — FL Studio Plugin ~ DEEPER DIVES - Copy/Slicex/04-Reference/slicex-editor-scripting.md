# Slicex Editor Scripting: Pascal Magic

Slicex (and Edison) includes a built-in Pascal-based scripting engine for automated wave manipulation. This is the "hidden power" of the plugin. [SRC: IL-ED-01]

## Why use Scripts?
Scripts can perform complex tasks on 100+ slices instantly that would take hours to do manually.

## Essential Scripts for Hip-Hop Producers:

### 1. "Randomize Velocity"
- **Function:** Slightly varies the gain of every slice.
- **Production Value:** Instantly "humanizes" a perfect electronic drum loop.

### 2. "Reverse All Regions"
- **Function:** Flips every slice in place.
- **Production Value:** Great for creating "Reverse-Chop" melodies for **Moody** R&B.

### 3. "Export Regions to Files"
- **Function:** Saves every slice as an individual `.wav` file.
- **Production Value:** Essential for building your own "One-Shot" drum kits from classic breaks.

## How to use Scripts:
1. Click the **Wrench/Tools** icon in the editor.
2. Navigate to the **Run Script** menu.
3. Choose from the factory presets or load an external `.txt` script.

## Research Tip:
Advanced users can write their own scripts to create "custom slicing" logic (e.g., "Slice every 1/8th note exactly").
