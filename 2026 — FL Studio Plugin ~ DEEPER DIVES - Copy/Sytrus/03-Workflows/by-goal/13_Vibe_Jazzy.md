# Goal: Vibe - Jazzy

## Goal Statement
Create warm, sophisticated FM electric piano keys for jazzy R&B chords.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Carrier (OP 1):** Sine wave, Ratio 1.000.
2. **Modulator (OP 2):** Sine wave, Ratio 1.000.
3. **Matrix:** Use the **Mod** tab to map **Velocity** to OP 2 -> OP 1 amount.
4. **Saturation:** Route OP 1 to **Filter 1** (LP Vanilla).
5. **Chorus:** Use the FX tab Chorus (Fat) at 20% Mix.
6. **Dynamics:** Ensure the note **Release** is long enough (1s) to let the jazzy extensions ring out.

## What to Listen For
- A "mellow" tone when playing soft, and a "growly" tone when playing hard.
- A "classic" sophisticated feel that avoids 80s "cheese."
