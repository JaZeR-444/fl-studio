# FX/Samples Analysis with Fruity Spectroman

## FX Frequency Considerations

**Purpose:** Add texture, interest, transitions  
**Frequency:** Variable (depends on FX type)  
**Balance:** Should enhance, not distract

---

## FX Analysis Workflow

### Riser/Build FX
1. Check where riser sits spectrally
2. Often 500 Hz - 12 kHz (sweeping upward)
3. Should build excitement without clouding mix

**Spectroman:** Visible frequency sweep

---

### Impact/Transition FX
1. Check for excessive low-end
2. High-pass if needed (leave room for bass)
3. Should add punch without muddying

**Spectroman:** Transient spike visible, controlled

---

### Textural FX (Vinyl Crackle, etc.)
1. Usually high-frequency content (5-15 kHz)
2. Should be subtle in spectrum
3. Adds character without dominating

**Spectroman:** Minimal presence, adds texture

---

## FX Checklist
- [ ] Doesn't muddy low-end
- [ ] Appropriate spectral placement
- [ ] Enhances, doesn't distract
- [ ] Level balanced with mix
