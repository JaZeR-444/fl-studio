## Moody Vibe Presets (Quick Copy)

### 1. Dark Tape Stop
- **SPD:** 1x → 0x (automate over 6-8 beats)
- **ACC:** 25%
- **SEN:** N/A
- **Sample:** Full beat, 808, or dark pad
- **FX:** Fruity Reverb 2 (50% mix, 4-second decay)
- **Use:** Transitions into moody verses/bridges

### 2. Haunting Reverse Vocal
- **SPD:** -0.7x (static)
- **ACC:** 45%
- **Sample:** Vocal phrase, 3-5 seconds
- **FX:** Fruity Reverb 2 (60% mix, long decay)
- **Use:** Background atmospheric texture

### 3. Pitch-Down Scratch
- **SPD:** 0.6x to 0.8x
- **ACC:** 65%
- **SEN:** 55%
- **Sample:** Vocal stab ("Yeah," "Ooh"), 1-2 sec
- **Use:** Dark vocal chops, minimal scratching

### 4. Minimal Texture
- **SPD:** 0.85x ↔ 0.95x (slow wobble automation)
- **ACC:** 35%
- **SEN:** 45%
- **Sample:** Pad, ambient texture, jazz loop
- **Use:** Background texture, sparse arrangements

### 5. Reverse Swell
- **SPD:** 0x → -1x (automate over 4 beats)
- **ACC:** 40%
- **Sample:** Cymbal crash, vocal ending
- **Use:** Tension build into moody section

## Upbeat Vibe Presets (Quick Copy)

### 1. Rapid Vocal Cuts
- **SPD:** 1.1x (static, 10% faster)
- **ACC:** 90%
- **SEN:** 70%
- **Sample:** Bright, energetic vocal (0.5-1.5 sec)
- **FX:** Parametric EQ 2 (boost 3-5 kHz for presence)
- **Use:** Hype sections, upbeat hooks

### 2. Quick Tape Stop
- **SPD:** 1x → 0x (automate over 1 beat)
- **ACC:** 90%
- **Sample:** Drum loop or full beat
- **FX:** Limiter (prevent clipping)
- **Use:** Pre-drop build-ups

### 3. Syncopated Scratch
- **SPD:** 1x
- **ACC:** 85%
- **SEN:** 75%
- **Sample:** Drum hit or vocal (< 1 sec)
- **Use:** Rhythmic complexity, off-beat scratches

### 4. Pitch Riser
- **SPD:** 1x → 1.5x (automate over 2 beats)
- **ACC:** 80%
- **Sample:** Riser, vocal, synth
- **Use:** Energy build before drop

## Psychedelic Vibe Presets (Quick Copy)

### 1. Warped Pitch Bend
- **SPD:** 0.5x ↔ 1.5x (curved automation, not linear)
- **ACC:** 50%
- **Sample:** Vocal or synth (2-5 sec, sustained)
- **FX CHAIN (CRITICAL):**
  - Fruity Reverb 2 (50% mix, 3-5 sec decay)
  - Fruity Delay 3 (ping-pong, 30% mix)
  - Fruity Phaser (slow rate, 70% depth)
- **Use:** Trippy FX, experimental sections

### 2. Glitch Reverse
- **SPD:** Rapid jumps between -1x, -0.5x, 0x (random automation)
- **ACC:** 80%
- **Sample:** Drum loop or complex vocal
- **Use:** Broken tape, glitchy psychedelic

### 3. Feedback Loop Scratch [UNVERIFIED]
- **SPD:** 0.8x to 1.2x (subtle variation)
- **ACC:** 60%
- **SEN:** 55%
- **Routing:** Scratcher out → Send (reverb/delay) → back to Scratcher in
- **Use:** Self-feeding experimental texture

## Jazzy Vibe Presets (Quick Copy)

### 1. Lazy Tape Stop
- **SPD:** 1x → 0.7x (automate over 4 beats, DON'T reach 0x)
- **ACC:** 30%
- **Sample:** Jazz loop or full beat
- **Use:** Smooth transitions, laid-back feel

### 2. Swung Vocal Scratch
- **SPD:** 0.95x to 1.05x (subtle variation)
- **ACC:** 60%
- **SEN:** 55%
- **Sample:** Warm vocal (1-2 sec)
- **FX:** Parametric EQ 2 (boost 200-500 Hz) + Effector (vinyl)
- **Technique:** Swung timing (not straight 16ths)
- **Use:** Syncopated vocal chops

### 3. Texture Warble
- **SPD:** 0.92x ↔ 1.08x (slow sine wave automation)
- **ACC:** 35%
- **Sample:** Jazz loop or piano (4-8 bars)
- **FX:** Effector (vinyl mode)
- **Use:** Lo-fi jazzy wobble, vintage texture

## Vibey Vibe Presets (Quick Copy)

### 1. Sustained Reverse Texture
- **SPD:** -0.7x (static reverse)
- **ACC:** 45%
- **Sample:** Pad, vocal hum, or ambient (5-10 sec)
- **FX:** Fruity Reverb 2 (60% mix, long decay) + Delay 3 (30% mix)
- **Use:** Floating atmospheric background

### 2. Slow Pitch Drift
- **SPD:** 0.95x → 1.05x (automate over 8 bars, slow curve)
- **ACC:** 40%
- **Sample:** Pad or synth (4-8 sec)
- **Use:** Breathing texture, subtle movement

### 3. Minimal Vocal Flutter
- **SPD:** 1x (mostly) with occasional 0.9x dips (1/8-note duration)
- **ACC:** 55%
- **Sample:** Vocal phrase (2-4 sec)
- **Use:** Subtle vibey character, organic feel

## FX Chain Presets

### Psychedelic FX Chain
```
Scratcher (SPD varies) → 
Fruity Reverb 2 (50%, 3-5 sec) → 
Fruity Delay 3 (ping-pong, 30%) → 
Fruity Phaser (slow, 70%) → 
Fruity Limiter (-3 dB)
```

### Lo-Fi/Jazzy FX Chain
```
Scratcher (SPD wobble) → 
Parametric EQ 2 (low-pass 6 kHz, boost 300 Hz) → 
Effector (vinyl mode, 30%) → 
Fruity Reeverb (small room, 25%) → 
Soft Clipper (gentle)
```

### Moody FX Chain
```
Scratcher (SPD slow/reverse) → 
Parametric EQ 2 (low-pass 5 kHz, boost 200-400 Hz) → 
Fruity Reverb 2 (60%, 4-6 sec, high damping) → 
Fruity Delay 3 (1/8 note, 20%, filtered) → 
Fruity Limiter (-6 dB)
```

### Upbeat FX Chain
```
Scratcher (SPD fast) → 
Parametric EQ 2 (boost 3-5 kHz) → 
Fruity Stereo Enhancer (30-40% width) → 
Fruity Limiter (0 dB, prevent clipping)
```

## Save These As Channel States

**Workflow:**
1. Set up Scratcher + FX chain as described above
2. Right-click channel → Save channel state as...
3. Name: `Scratcher - [Vibe] - [Preset Name].fst`
4. Save to: `FL Studio\Data\Patches\Channel presets\Fruity Scratcher\Vibes\`

**Example file names:**
- `Scratcher - Moody - Dark Tape Stop.fst`
- `Scratcher - Upbeat - Rapid Vocal Cuts.fst`
- `Scratcher - Psychedelic - Warped Pitch Bend.fst`
- `Scratcher - Jazzy - Lazy Tape Stop.fst`
- `Scratcher - Vibey - Sustained Reverse.fst`
