# Goal: Vibe - Moody

## Goal Statement
Create a "soft," "distant," and "receding" texture for emotional background layers.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Placement:** Use on an acoustic piano or soft Rhodes.
2. **Attack:** Turn the **Attack** knob to -6dB. This "blunts" the hammers/fingers of the instrument.
3. **Release:** Turn the **Release** knob up to +5dB. This emphasizes the room noise and "air" around the notes.
4. **Mix:** Set to 100%.
5. **Result:** The instrument feels like it's being played behind a curtain or in another room.

## What to Listen For
- A "muffled" but "lush" tone.
- A feeling of intimacy where the background textures are louder than the hits themselves.
