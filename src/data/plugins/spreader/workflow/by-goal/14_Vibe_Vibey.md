# Goal: Vibe - Vibey

## Goal Statement
Create a smooth, wide, and "expensive" R&B atmosphere.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Width:** 65%.
2. **Low Bypass:** 350Hz.
3. **Mono Switch:** ON.
4. **Processing:** Add a **Chorus** AFTER Spreader.
5. **Result:** The Spreader creates the wide canvas, and the Chorus adds the "shimmer."

## Mini-Recipe: "Silk Width"
- Width at 50%.
- Separation at 50%.
- Used on a Rhodes or Pad.
- *Result:* A silky, wide sound that sits perfectly behind a centered lead vocal.
