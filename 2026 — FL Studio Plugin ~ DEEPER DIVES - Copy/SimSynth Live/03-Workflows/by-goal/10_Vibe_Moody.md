# Goal: Vibe - Moody

## Goal Statement
Create a warm, dark, and intimate analog texture that provides a "lonely" atmosphere.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Oscillator:** Osc 1 (Triangle, Warm).
2. **Filter:** LP Mix 100%. Cutoff at 150Hz. Resonance 0%.
3. **Envelopes:** Attack 50ms, Release 1s.
4. **Matrix:** LFO -> Pitch (Amount 1%, Speed 1.5Hz). This adds the "tired" feel.
5. **Acoustics:** Chorus Mix 20% (Subtle).

## What to Listen For
- A "felt" or "soft" sound that doesn't have any sharp edges.
- A subtle pitch drift that makes it feel like an old recording.
