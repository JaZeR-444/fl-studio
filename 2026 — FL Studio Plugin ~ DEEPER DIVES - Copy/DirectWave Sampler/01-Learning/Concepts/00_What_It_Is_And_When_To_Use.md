# What It Is And When To Use: DirectWave Sampler

## Purpose & Identity
DirectWave is FL Studio's **professional multi-sampler**. Its primary identity is a virtual instrument builder. Unlike the simple Fruity Sampler, DirectWave can manage hundreds of samples at once, mapping them to different keys and velocities. It is also a "Plugin Sampler," capable of automatically sampling your other VSTs to turn them into lightweight DirectWave banks. [SRC: IL-MAN]

## 60-Second Mental Model
Think of it as a "Library Creator." 
- If the standard sampler is a single book, DirectWave is the entire library. 
- You can layer a soft piano sample for light playing and a loud piano sample for hard playing (**Velocity Layering**).
- You can assign different flute notes to different keys so the pitch sounds natural (**Key Mapping**).

## Hip-Hop / R&B Context
- **Custom VST Ripping:** Sampling a heavy Kontakt library or Serum patch so you can use it without lagging your session (Upbeat).
- **Intimate R&B Pianos:** Using multi-sampled banks with high velocity sensitivity to capture the "feel" of a real player (Jazzy/Vibey).
- **Vocal Textures:** Mapping different vocal ad-libs across the keyboard to play them as a "Melodic Chop" kit (Melodic).

## When To Use
- When you want to build a **realistic instrument** from multiple audio files.
- When you need to **save CPU** by sampling a complex plugin chain into a single instrument.
- When you are working with **Soundfonts (.sf2)** or other professional sample bank formats.

## When NOT To Use
- **Simple One-Shots:** Use the standard **Sampler** or **Slicex** for single kicks or vocal chops.
- **CPU Savings (Simple):** Just "Consolidating" a track in the playlist is faster if you don't need to play the sound via MIDI. [SRC: REPUTABLE]
