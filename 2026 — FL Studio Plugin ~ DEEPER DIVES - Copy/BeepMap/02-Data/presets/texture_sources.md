# Texture Source Library

Recommended image types for specific sonic textures in BeepMap:

- **Clouds/Smoke:** Soft, washing atmospheric noise. Great for pads.
- **Architecture/Cities:** Sharp, dissonant, rhythmic patterns due to straight lines and grids.
- **Fractals:** Complex, evolving, mathematically related harmonics that feel "organic" yet synthesized.
- **QR Codes:** Perfect for digital glitch rhythms and randomized bit-crushed textures.
- **Star Fields:** Tinkling, bell-like high-frequency clusters with a lot of "air".
- **Nature (Trees/Leaves):** Dense, jittery, mid-range noise that mimics field recordings.
