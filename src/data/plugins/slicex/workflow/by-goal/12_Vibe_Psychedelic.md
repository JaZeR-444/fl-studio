# Goal: Vibe - Psychedelic

## Goal Statement
Transform a standard loop into a trippy, evolving soundscape that warps the listener's sense of time.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Deck Layering:** Load the loop in **Deck A** and its **Reversed version** in **Deck B**.
2. **Crossfade Modulation:** Map a slow **LFO** to the **Deck Crossfade** slider.
3. **Granular Slices:** Manually slice very small 1/64th note regions. Repeat them in the Piano Roll.
4. **Articulator:** Route **LFO** to **Pan** and **Filter Cutoff**.
5. **Effects:** Use a **Ping-Pong Delay** (External) with high feedback.

## What to Listen For
- A sound that "morphs" between forward and reverse.
- "Bubbling" filter movement that pans across the stereo field.
- "Stutter" effects from the granular slices.
