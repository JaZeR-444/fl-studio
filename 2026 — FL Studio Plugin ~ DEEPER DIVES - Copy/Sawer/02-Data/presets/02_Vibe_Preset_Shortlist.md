# Vibe Preset Shortlist

## 1. Moody (Dark & Gritty)
- **Bunker Bass:** LP 24dB, Cutoff 15%, Noise 10%, Sub Phase 180°.
- **Cold War Pad:** Slow attack, high Unison detune, Filter LP 12dB at 400Hz.
- **Lost Satellite:** Scrape noise, high Reverb, LFO modulating Pitch (Slow).
- **Noir Keys:** Low Cutoff, high Damping, Chorus Mix 40%.
- **Deep Growl:** High Resonance BP filter, slow Filter Env sweep.

## 2. Upbeat (Aggressive & Snappy)
- **Tear Lead:** Hard Sync enabled, LFO -> Slave Pitch (Fast).
- **Trap Thump:** Max Sub, fast Amp Decay, Filter Env +50%.
- **Sync Stab:** Short Decay, high Resonance, Hard Sync ON.
- **Acid Saw:** LP 24dB, Max Resonance, fast Filter LFO.
- **Turbo Arp:** 1/16 Arp speed, Sync enabled, Noise 5%.

## 3. Psychedelic (Trippy & Moving)
- **Warped Vinyl:** LFO -> Master Pitch (2% depth), Unison 8 voices.
- **Spiral Filter:** LFO -> Filter Cutoff (Sine), Phaser Mix 50%.
- **Ghost in the Machine:** Noise mixed with Sync lead, high Delay feedback.
- **Phase Blur:** Max Unison Pan, high Phaser speed.
- **Morphing Pad:** LFO -> Sub Level, slowly blending sub-frequencies.

## 4. Jazzy (Warm & Analog)
- **Smooth Polivoks:** LP 12dB, Cutoff 50%, Res 0%, Attack 40ms.
- **Vintage Rhodes:** Amp Release 500ms, Velocity -> Filter ON.
- **Soft Brass:** 2 Voices Unison, slow Filter Env attack.
- **Warm Organ:** BP Filter at 1kHz, high Chorus.
- **Soulful Bass:** Sub oscillator only, Filter Cutoff at 200Hz.

## 5. Vibey (Lush & Modern)
- **R&B Cloud:** 8 Voices Unison, Max Pan, Reverb Size 100%.
- **Liquid Keys:** High Phaser depth (Slow), Delay Mix 20%.
- **Silk Lead:** Mono Legato, high Glide, Soft Attack.
- **Late Night Pad:** Dark LP filter, high Delay feedback, slow LFO -> Filter.
- **Dream Pluck:** Fast Amp Decay, high Reverb Mix, low Damping.
