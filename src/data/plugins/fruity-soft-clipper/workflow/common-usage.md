# Common Workflows: Fruity Soft Clipper

## 1. Transparent Master Limiting
**Goal:** Increase overall track loudness without "pumping" artifacts.

1.  Place **Fruity Soft Clipper** as the last slot on your **Master** mixer track.
2.  Leave **Threshold** at default (maximum).
3.  Slowly increase **Post Gain** to boost the overall volume.
    *   *Why?* The Soft Clipper handles the peaks gently, allowing you to push the volume higher than a standard hard limiter without audible distortion.

## 2. Saturating Drums (The "Glue")
**Goal:** Make a drum bus sound cohesive and "punchy".

1.  Route your Kick, Snare, and Hats to a single **Bus** track.
2.  Add **Fruity Soft Clipper**.
3.  Lower the **Threshold** slightly (create headroom).
4.  Push the drum levels *into* the clipper using the faders or an EQ before it.
    *   *Result:* The peaks of the kick and snare will "squash" together pleasantly, creating a cohesive "glue" effect tailored for Hip Hop and EDM.
