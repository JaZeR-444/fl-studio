# Genre Style Board: Sawer

## Hip-Hop
- **Technique:** "Soviet Sub." Use the Sub oscillator with the 24dB LP filter. The Polivoks character adds a natural saturation that makes the bass audible on small speakers without needing extra distortion plugins.
- **Reference Idea:** Think of the gritty, analog basslines in classic Dr. Dre or modern Travis Scott tracks.

## Rap
- **Technique:** "Toxic Sync Lead." Use Hard Sync with a fast pitch LFO. It creates a "reedy," aggressive tone that sits perfectly over loud 808s.
- **Reference Idea:** High-pitched West Coast G-Funk style leads with a Soviet twist.

## R&B / Pop-Hip-Hop
- **Technique:** "Vibey Analog Clouds." 8-voice Unison with maximum detune and chorus. It creates a "wash" of sound that feels nostalgic and expensive.
- **Reference Idea:** The lush, detuned synth pads in modern PBR&B (PartyNextDoor, The Weeknd).

## Key Technique References
- **Analog Drift:** LFO (1%) -> Master Pitch. [SRC: HW-HIST]
- **Soviet Filter Growl:** Resonance > 30% on LP 24dB. [SRC: IL-MAN-02]
- **Sub-Bass Punch:** Fixed Sub Phase at 180°. [SRC: REPUTABLE-03]
