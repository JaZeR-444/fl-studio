# Industrial Background Noise

**Context:** Creating the "room tone" of a factory or spaceship engine room.

### The "Grainy" Texture
1.  **Image:** Load a photo of "Concrete" or "Rust" (high frequency, random noise detail).
2.  **Mode:** Enable **Grainy**.
3.  **Controls:**
    -   **Freq:** Low.
    -   **Length:** Extremely Slow (e.g., 10 seconds).
    -   **Widen:** Max.
4.  **Processing:**
    -   Add **Fruity Flanger** (Slow rate).
    -   Add **Fruity Love Philter** (Low Pass LFO).
5.  **Result:** The texture of the concrete photo is translated into a grinding, shifting, granular drone that never perfectly repeats.
