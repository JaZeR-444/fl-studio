# Vocals - Fruity Slicer 2 Workflows

**Instrument:** Vocals (phrases, acapellas, ad-libs, hooks)  
**Primary Use:** Chop vocals into playable elements, create hooks, rhythmic vocal effects

---

## Workflow 1: Vocal Chop Hook Creation

### Goal
Transform vocal phrase into melodic/rhythmic hook

### Time: 10-15 minutes

---

### Step-by-Step

**1. Source Selection (2 min)**
- Load short vocal phrase (1-4 seconds)
- Best sources:
  - Acapella hooks
  - Vocal ad-libs ("Yeah," "Ooh," "Huh")
  - Sung phrases (melodic)
  - Spoken words

**2. Manual Syllable Slicing (5 min)**
- Don't use Auto-Slice (not rhythmic)
- Manually place slice markers at each syllable:
  - "Check - it - out" = 3 slices
  - "Yeah - yeah - yeah" = 3 slices
  - "O - oh - baby" = 3 slices
- Zoom in, place precisely at syllable start
- Enable zero-crossing snap

**3. Map to Keyboard (1 min)**
- Slices auto-map chromatically:
  - C5 = Slice 1 (first syllable)
  - C#5 = Slice 2 (second syllable)
  - D5 = Slice 3 (third syllable)
- Test each slice playback

**4. Create Melodic/Rhythmic Pattern (5 min)**

**Option A: Melodic Hook**
- Play slices at different pitches
- Example: "Yeah" sample
  - C5 (root pitch)
  - E5 (+4 semitones, major third)
  - G5 (+7 semitones, fifth)
  - Creates chord from single word

**Option B: Rhythmic Hook**
- Repeat slice on rhythmic grid
- Example: "Check" on every snare hit
- Example: "Yeah-yeah-yeah" rapid fire (1/16 notes)

**5. Process Vocal Chops (2-3 min)**
- **Reverb:** 30-50% send (space and depth)
- **Delay:** Dotted eighth or quarter (musical echo)
- **Auto-Tune (optional):** Tight correction for modern sound
- **Saturation:** Analog warmth, character

---

### Settings

**Slice Division:** Manual (syllable-based)  
**Stretch Mode:** Disabled (preserve pitch)  
**Processing:** Reverb 40%, delay 30%, optional auto-tune

## Workflow 2: Trap Vocal Stutter Effect

### Goal
Create modern trap vocal stutter/glitch effect

### Time: 7-10 minutes

---

### Step-by-Step

**1. Load Vocal Sample (1 min)**
- Single word or short phrase
- Common: "Yeah," "Skrrt," "Uh," ad-libs

**2. Slice Single Syllable (2 min)**
- Manual slice: isolate single syllable
- Example: "Yeah" = one slice
- Or: "Yuh" from longer phrase

**3. Create Stutter Pattern (3 min)**
- Piano Roll: rapid repetition
  ```
  16th note stutter:
  Beat 1.0: C5
  Beat 1.25: C5
  Beat 1.5: C5
  Beat 1.75: C5
  (Rapid fire "Yeah-yeah-yeah-yeah")
  ```
- Common on snare hits or before drops

**4. Velocity Variation (1 min)**
- Not all at 100%
- Pattern:
  - 1st hit: 100%
  - 2nd hit: 85%
  - 3rd hit: 75%
  - 4th hit: 90%
- Creates groove, not robotic

**5. Extreme Processing (2-3 min)**
- **Heavy reverb:** 50-70% send
- **Ping-pong delay:** Synced (1/8 or 1/16)
- **Pitch shift (optional):** -12 or +12 semitones (octave drama)
- **Distortion:** Grit and aggression

---

### Settings

**Slice Division:** Manual (single syllable)  
**Pattern:** 1/16 or 1/32 rapid fire  
**Velocity:** Varied (75-100%)  
**Processing:** Heavy reverb/delay, optional pitch shift

## Workflow 3: Soul Sample Vocal Flip

### Goal
Chop classic soul vocal, rearrange for boom bap hook

### Time: 15-20 minutes

---

### Step-by-Step

**1. Load Soul Acapella (2 min)**
- 2-8 bar soul/R&B vocal
- Look for expressive phrases, runs, ad-libs

**2. Strategic Phrase Slicing (5 min)**
- Slice at musical phrase boundaries (not syllables)
- Example: "I love you baby" 
  - Slice 1: "I love you"
  - Slice 2: "baby"
- Or slice individual words for more flexibility
- Mark emotional peaks (high notes, runs)

**3. Identify Hook Slices (3 min)**
- Play each slice, identify:
  - **Anchor slices:** Main hook, memorable
  - **Support slices:** Fills, transitions
  - **Accent slices:** One-shots, emphasis
- Note which slices are most musical

**4. Rearrange and Flip (5 min)**
- Techniques:
  - **Reverse order:** "baby" → "I love you" (flip phrase)
  - **Stutter:** "I-I-I love you baby"
  - **Pitch shift:** Original + pitched down (-3 semitones) layered
  - **Rhythmic:** "baby" on every snare hit

**5. Boom Bap Processing (3-5 min)**
- **Tape saturation:** Vintage warmth
- **Vinyl crackle layer:** Nostalgic character
- **Short plate reverb:** 25-35% (not washy)
- **Sidechain to kick (optional):** Vocal ducks on kick hits (classic)

---

### Settings

**Slice Division:** Manual (phrase-based)  
**BPM:** Match original or disable stretch  
**Processing:** Tape saturation, vinyl layer, vintage reverb

## Workflow 4: Acapella Build-Up/Breakdown

### Goal
Use vocal slices for transition/build-up sections

### Time: 5-7 minutes

---

### Step-by-Step

**1. Load Acapella Phrase (1 min)**
- 1-2 bar vocal phrase

**2. Slice for Texture (2 min)**
- Auto-Slice @ 1/8 or 1/16
- Not for musical content, for texture

**3. Granular/Glitch Pattern (2 min)**
- Rapid, chaotic playback:
  ```
  Random slices at 1/32 intervals
  Builds tension
  ```
- Or: Slice stutter increasing in speed:
  ```
  Bar 1: Quarter notes
  Bar 2: Eighth notes
  Bar 3: 16th notes
  Bar 4: 32nd notes (drops)
  ```

**4. Automation (1-2 min)**
- **Volume crescendo:** Quiet → loud (builds energy)
- **Reverb automation:** Dry → 80% wet (wash out before drop)
- **Pitch automation:** Slow pitch rise (+1 octave over 4 bars)

---

### Settings

**Slice Division:** 1/16 or 1/32 (texture)  
**Automation:** Volume, reverb, pitch (all increasing)  
**Use:** 4-8 bars before drop

---

## Workflow 5: Call-and-Response Vocal Arrangement

### Goal
Create conversational vocal pattern between two samples

### Time: 10 minutes

---

### Step-by-Step

**1. Load Two Vocal Samples (1 min)**
- Sample A: Question or phrase
- Sample B: Answer or contrasting phrase
- Example: "Where you at?" (A) / "Right here" (B)

**2. Slice Both (3 min)**
- Manual slicing, isolate key phrases
- Sample A → C5, C#5, D5
- Sample B → E5, F5, F#5

**3. Arrange Call-and-Response (4 min)**
- Pattern:
  ```
  Bar 1: Sample A (beats 1-2)
  Bar 1: Sample B (beats 3-4)
  Bar 2: Sample A (beats 1-2)
  Bar 2: Sample B (beats 3-4)
  (Conversation)
  ```

**4. Process Differently (2 min)**
- **Sample A (Call):** Dry, upfront (reverb 15%)
- **Sample B (Response):** Wet, distant (reverb 50%, delay)
- Creates spatial separation (call close, response far)

---

### Settings

**Slice Division:** Manual  
**Processing:** A = dry/close, B = wet/distant

## Vocal Slicing Tips

### For Melodic Vocals
- Slice at syllable boundaries
- Disable time-stretch (preserve pitch)
- Play chromatically for new melodies

### For Rhythmic Vocals
- Slice at word boundaries
- Enable time-stretch if tempo-based
- Use as percussion (trigger on snare, kick)

### For Ad-Libs
- Single slice per ad-lib
- Scatter throughout arrangement
- Minimal processing (natural feel)

---

## Common Vocal Mistakes

**Mistake 1: Auto-Slicing Vocals**
- Vocals aren't rhythmic like drums
- Fix: Manual slicing at syllables/phrases

**Mistake 2: Too Much Reverb**
- Washes out vocal, loses clarity
- Fix: 25-40% max, high-pass reverb send

**Mistake 3: Ignoring Pitch Preservation**
- Time-stretch changes pitch unintentionally
- Fix: Disable stretch for melodic content

**Mistake 4: No Dynamics**
- All slices same velocity = robotic
- Fix: Vary 70-100%, emphasize key slices

---

## The Bottom Line

**Vocal slicing in Fruity Slicer 2: Manual slice at syllable boundaries (don't auto-slice) → Map to keyboard → Play melodically (pitch shift) or rhythmically (stutter) → Process with reverb (30-40%), delay, optional auto-tune.** Soul samples slice by phrase, rearrange for hooks. Trap ad-libs slice single syllables, rapid stutter (1/16 notes). Disable time-stretch to preserve pitch. Vary velocities for dynamics. Use call-and-response with different processing (A dry, B wet).

**Quick Vocal Win:** "Yeah" ad-lib → Manual slice → Stutter 1/16 notes on snare → Reverb 50% → Instant trap vocal hook.
