# Mixing Rules for BassDrum

## Rule 1: Sidechain Priority
- Because BassDrum generates pure, heavy sub-frequencies, it *must* be the sidechain trigger for your bass synth.
- **Workflow:** Route BassDrum to a dedicated mixer track and use "Limiter Sidechain" on your bassline.

## Rule 2: EQ Carving
- Do not boost the low-end of BassDrum with an external EQ.
- **Rule:** If the sub is too quiet, increase the **Main Osc Level** internally. Internal synthesis is cleaner than post-processing gain boosts.

## Rule 3: Phase Consistency
- Check the kick in **Mono**.
- If the kick disappears or sounds "flangy" in mono, your Slave Osc or Sample is out of phase. Turn the **Phase** knob until it is rock solid in the center.
