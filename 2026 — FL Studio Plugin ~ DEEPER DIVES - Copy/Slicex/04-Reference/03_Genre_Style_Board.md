# Genre Style Board: Slicex

## Hip-Hop
- **Technique:** "The Soul Flip." Manually chop a soul sample by region (M). Use the Tune tool in the editor to shift it to the key of your beat. Apply a 24dB LP filter to make it "dusty."
- **Reference Idea:** Think of J Dilla or Kanye West style soul-chopping where the sample becomes a completely new instrument.

## Rap
- **Technique:** "Deck B 808 Layering." Load a drum break in Deck A and a hard 808 kick in Deck B. Sync them to the same trigger note. This gives you the "texture" of old school with the "power" of new school.
- **Reference Idea:** The heavy, textured drum production of modern trap artists like 21 Savage or Travis Scott.

## R&B / Pop-Hip-Hop
- **Technique:** "Vocal Swell Chops." Slice a vocal run. Use Articulator 1 to set a 100ms Attack and 500ms Release. This turns vocal hits into smooth, "vibey" pads.
- **Reference Idea:** Modern R&B vocal textures (SZA, Summer Walker) where the vocals are used as instruments.

## Key Technique References
- **Choke Groups:** Cut 1 / Cut By 1. [SRC: REPUTABLE-05]
- **Articulator Modulation:** LFO -> Filter Cutoff. [SRC: IL-MAN-04]
- **Manual "Micro-Chops":** Zooming in to avoid clicks. [SRC: REPUTABLE-05]
