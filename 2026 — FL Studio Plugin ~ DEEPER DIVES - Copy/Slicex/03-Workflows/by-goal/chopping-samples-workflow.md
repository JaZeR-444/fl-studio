# Goal: Chopping Samples Workflow

## Goal Statement
Slicing a melodic sample (Soul, Jazz, or Vocal) and re-arranging it into a new, original melody for Hip-Hop production.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Import:** Drag your sample into **Deck A**.
2. **Tempo Sync:** Go to the Editor menu -> **Regions -> Detect Score BPM**. This aligns Slicex to your project tempo.
3. **Manual Slicing:**
   - Zoom in.
   - Click where you want to chop and hit `M`.
   - Aim for the starts of piano chords, vocal words, or horn stabs.
4. **Articulator:** Select all regions (`Ctrl+A`). Assign them to **Articulator 1**.
5. **Envelopes:** In Articulator 1, set the **Volume Attack** to 15ms and **Release** to 100ms. This prevents clicks and adds a "smooth" transition between chops.
6. **Dump:** Click "Dump to Piano Roll" and start re-arranging.

## Starting Settings
- **Filter Mode:** LP (High Cut at 5kHz for a lo-fi feel).
- **Attack:** 15ms.
- **Release:** 100ms.

## Variations
- **Variation 1 (Pitched):** Use the **Master Pitch** to shift the whole melody up or down an octave.
- **Variation 2 (Glitchy):** Set one slice to **Loop** mode and use it as a rhythmic repeat.
