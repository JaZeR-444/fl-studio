# Fruity Scratcher — Genre Style Board: Hip-Hop & R&B

## Reference Track Analysis

This style board provides concrete examples of scratch usage in hip-hop/R&B production across different eras and subgenres. Use these as reference points for your own scratch applications.

---

## Boom-Bap / Golden Age Hip-Hop

### DJ Premier (Gang Starr)

**Track:** "Mass Appeal" (1994)  
**Scratch Style:** Classic vocal stabs on the 2 and 4  
**Sample:** "Hey!" vocal cuts  
**Technique:** Baby scratch, chirp scratch  
**Scratcher Settings (Estimated):**
- SPD: 1x
- ACC: 75-85% (direct-drive feel)
- SEN: 55-65%
- Density: 2-4 scratches per 8 bars (sparse, intentional)

**Key Characteristics:**
- Dry, punchy scratches (no reverb)
- Scratches reinforce backbeat
- Minimal, not flashy
- Classic DJ Premier "crunch" sound

**Production Notes:**
- Scratches placed in gaps between rap vocals
- No scratching during verses (respects the rapper)
- Hook/outro = scratch solo opportunities

## Trap / Modern Hip-Hop

### Metro Boomin

**Track:** "Mask Off" (Future, 2017)  
**Scratch Style:** Tape stop on hi-hats before drops  
**Technique:** Quick tape stop  
**Scratcher Settings (Estimated):**
- SPD: 1x → 0x (1 beat duration)
- ACC: 85-95% (quick, aggressive)
- Sample: Hi-hat loop or full beat

**Key Characteristics:**
- Aggressive, digital tape stop (not vintage)
- Used sparingly (1-2 times per track)
- Pre-drop tension builder

**Production Notes:**
- Modern trap uses tape stops more than scratching
- No classic turntablism—FX only
- High ACC for tight, modern feel

---

### Travis Scott

**Track:** "SICKO MODE" (2018)  
**Scratch Style:** Reverse swells, pitch drops on transitions  
**Technique:** Reverse automation, psychedelic FX  
**Scratcher Settings (Estimated):**
- SPD: 0x → -1x (reverse swell), or 1x → 0.5x (pitch drop)
- ACC: 40-60% (smooth, not abrupt)
- Heavy reverb + delay

**Key Characteristics:**
- Psychedelic, disorienting scratch FX
- Not traditional turntablism
- Atmospheric, vibe-focused

---

### 21 Savage / Metro Boomin

**Track:** "X" (2016)  
**Scratch Style:** Moody tape stop on 808s  
**Technique:** Gradual tape stop (dark vibe)  
**Scratcher Settings (Estimated):**
- SPD: 1x → 0x (4-6 beats)
- ACC: 25-35% (gradual, heavy)
- Sample: 808 bass loop

**Key Characteristics:**
- Dark, moody deceleration
- Low ACC for emotional weight
- Fits minor-key, introspective trap

## R&B / Pop-Hip-Hop

### The Weeknd

**Track:** "The Hills" (2015)  
**Scratch Style:** Reverse vocal textures, pitch drops  
**Technique:** Reverse playback, automated pitch manipulation  
**Scratcher Settings (Estimated):**
- SPD: -0.6x to -0.8x (reverse) or 1x → 0.6x (pitch drop)
- ACC: 40-55% (smooth)
- Heavy reverb (60% mix, 4-sec decay)

**Key Characteristics:**
- Atmospheric, not rhythmic
- Reverse vocal textures low in mix
- Creates haunting, moody vibe

---

### PARTYNEXTDOOR

**Track:** "Come and See Me" (2016)  
**Scratch Style:** Minimal ad-lib pitch drops  
**Technique:** Automated SPD drop on "yeah," "ooh" ad-libs  
**Scratcher Settings (Estimated):**
- SPD: 1x → 0.7x (2-beat drop on vocal ending)
- ACC: 55-65%
- Reverb: 40-50% mix

**Key Characteristics:**
- Subtle, emotional pitch drops
- R&B smoothness (not aggressive)
- Fits with melodic production

---

### Jhené Aiko

**Track:** "Sativa" (2017)  
**Scratch Style:** Vibey reverse textures, slow pitch drift  
**Technique:** Reverse vocal hums, gentle automation  
**Scratcher Settings (Estimated):**
- SPD: -0.7x (reverse) or 0.95x → 1.05x (slow drift)
- ACC: 35-45% (loose, vibey)
- Reverb + delay

**Key Characteristics:**
- Floating, dreamy atmosphere
- No percussive scratching
- Blends into soundscape

## Lo-Fi / Chillhop

### Nujabes

**Track:** "Feather" (2005)  
**Scratch Style:** Jazzy texture wobble, lazy tape stops  
**Technique:** Subtle pitch variation, vinyl warble  
**Scratcher Settings (Estimated):**
- SPD: 0.92x ↔ 1.08x (slow sine automation)
- ACC: 30-40% (loose, analog)
- Effector (vinyl mode)

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm, vintage feel
- Almost imperceptible pitch wobble
- Lo-fi aesthetic (dust, crackle)

---

- ACC: 50-70% (moderate swing)
- SEN: 50-60%

**Key Characteristics:**
- Human timing (not robotic)
- Swung, laid-back groove
- Scratches fit jazz/soul aesthetic

---

## Scratch Placement Patterns

### Boom-Bap Placement
- **Intro:** Minimal scratches or DJ drops
- **Verse:** No scratching (respect rapper's space)
- **Hook:** 2-4 scratches on 2 and 4 (with snare)
- **Bridge:** Scratch solo or transition scratches
- **Outro:** Extended scratch solo

**Reference:** DJ Premier, Pete Rock, 9th Wonder

---

### Trap Placement
- **Intro:** Reverse swell or tape stop
- **Verse:** Minimal to none
- **Pre-Drop:** Quick tape stop (1 beat)
- **Hook:** Occasional vocal cuts (sparse)
- **Bridge:** Tape stop transition

**Reference:** Metro Boomin, Southside, TM88

---

### R&B Placement
- **Intro:** Reverse texture or slow pitch drift
- **Verse:** Minimal (background atmosphere)
- **Hook:** Pitch drop on ad-libs
- **Bridge:** Emotional pitch manipulation
- **Outro:** Reverse fade or slow warble

**Reference:** The Weeknd, PARTYNEXTDOOR, Jhené Aiko

---

### Lo-Fi Placement
- **Throughout:** Constant subtle pitch wobble
- **Transitions:** Lazy tape stops (don't reach 0x)
- **Breaks:** Swung vocal chops

**Reference:** Nujabes, J Dilla, Joji

## Mix Integration: Scratch Levels

### Boom-Bap
- Scratches: -6 to -3 dB (prominent but not overpowering)
- EQ: Boost 3-5 kHz for presence
- Compression: Minimal (preserve transients)

### Trap
- Tape stops: -3 to 0 dB (loud, impactful)
- Vocal cuts: -9 to -6 dB (accent, not focal)
- EQ: Boost 4-6 kHz, high-pass 100 Hz

### R&B
- Pitch drops: -6 to -3 dB (audible but smooth)
- Reverse textures: -15 to -12 dB (background atmosphere)
- Heavy reverb/delay

### Lo-Fi
- Texture wobble: -12 to -9 dB (subtle background)
- Swung scratches: -9 to -6 dB (complementary)
- EQ: Low-pass 6 kHz, boost 200-500 Hz

## Subgenre Decision Tree

```
What's your subgenre?

BOOM-BAP
├─ Study: DJ Premier, Pete Rock
├─ Scratch on: 2 and 4 (with snare)
├─ Settings: ACC 75-85%, dry signal
└─ Avoid: Over-scratching, reverb

TRAP
├─ Study: Metro Boomin, Travis Scott
├─ Use: Tape stops (not turntablism)
├─ Settings: ACC 25-35% (gradual) or 85-95% (quick)
└─ Avoid: Classic scratch techniques

R&B / POP-HIP-HOP
├─ Study: The Weeknd, PARTYNEXTDOOR
├─ Use: Pitch drops, reverse textures
├─ Settings: ACC 35-55%, heavy reverb
└─ Avoid: Aggressive scratching

LO-FI / CHILLHOP
├─ Study: Nujabes, J Dilla, Joji
├─ Use: Texture wobble, lazy tape stops
├─ Settings: ACC 30-50%, Effector (vinyl)
└─ Avoid: Tight timing, bright samples

EXPERIMENTAL
├─ Study: Flying Lotus, Death Grips
├─ Use: Wide SPD range, heavy FX
├─ Settings: ACC varies, creative automation
└─ Avoid: Traditional rules
```
