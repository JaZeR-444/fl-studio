# 03: Vibe Translation Guide

## Vibe Targets in Spreader

### 1. Moody (Dark, Intimate, Deep)
- **The Lever:** **Low Width + High Low-Bypass.**
- **Setting Moves:**
  - Keep **Width** at a modest 25-35%.
  - Set **Low Bypass** to 400Hz. This keeps the "warmth" in mono while the "dark" textures spread out.
- **What to listen for:** A sound that feels "deep" and "centered" but has a subtle atmosphere around it.
- **Pitfall:** Too much width makes moody sounds feel "plastic" or "synthetic." Keep it subtle.

### 2. Upbeat (High-Energy, Huge, Modern)
- **The Lever:** **Max Width + Mono Bypass.**
- **Setting Moves:**
  - Turn **Width** to 80-100%.
  - Set **Low Bypass** to 100Hz and enable the **Mono** switch.
  - Use on percussive loops or synth leads.
- **What to listen for:** An "explosive" stereo image that fills the speakers and creates energy.
- **Pitfall:** Ensure the snare doesn't lose its "center." If it does, reduce the **Separation**.

### 3. Psychedelic (Trippy, Moving, Fluid)
- **The Lever:** **Automated Width.**
- **Setting Moves:**
  - Right-click the **Width** knob and "Create Automation Clip."
  - Draw a rhythmic "pulse" shape (triangles or sine waves).
  - Add an external **Delay** or **Phaser** *before* Spreader.
- **What to listen for:** A sound that seems to "breathe" in and out of the speakers rhythmically.
- **Pitfall:** Don't automate too fast (no "vibrato" speeds); slow, fluid movements work best for psychedelic vibes.

### 4. Jazzy (Organic, Wide, Sophisticated)
- **The Lever:** **Separation + High Cut (External).**
- **Setting Moves:**
  - Use Spreader on a mono piano or sax.
  - Adjust **Separation** to the "Merged" side to keep the instrument's "wood" in the center.
  - Use a moderate **Width** (40%).
- **What to listen for:** A "lush" and "expensive" sound that mimics a stereo microphone recording.
- **Pitfall:** Avoid 100% width on organic instruments; it can make them sound "phasey" and fake.

### 5. Vibey (Smooth, "Ear-Candy", Modern R&B)
- **The Lever:** **The "Flower" Vectorscope Shape.**
- **Setting Moves:**
  - Aim for a "round" flower shape on the vectorscope.
  - Use on background vocal harmonies or "glitter" synths.
  - Set **Width** to 60% and **Low Bypass** to 300Hz.
- **What to listen for:** A "smooth" and "silky" texture that floats perfectly around the lead vocal.
- **Pitfall:** If the "flower" is leaning to one side, use the mixer pan to balance it before it hits Spreader.
