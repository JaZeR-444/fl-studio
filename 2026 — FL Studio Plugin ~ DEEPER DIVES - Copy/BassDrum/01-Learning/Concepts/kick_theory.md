# Kick Synthesis Concepts

## The Frequency Sweep (The "Laser")
A kick drum is psychoacoustically perceived as a "hit" because of the rapid drop in frequency.
- **Peak:** Provides the "click" and transient impact.
- **Base:** Provides the "body" and tonal weight.
- If the slide is too slow, it sounds like a "Laser" SFX. If it's too fast, it loses its punch.

## Phase Coherence
When layering a synthesized sub with a sampled top, the waveforms must "push" and "pull" at the same time.
- If they are out of phase, the low end will disappear (Phase Cancellation).
- BassDrum's **Phase** knob on the slave and **Delay** on the sample are the tools used to achieve **Coherent Summation**.
