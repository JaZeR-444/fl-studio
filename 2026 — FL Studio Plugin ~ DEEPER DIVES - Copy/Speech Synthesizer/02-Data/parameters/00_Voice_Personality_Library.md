# Voice Personality Library

## Built-in Voices
Each voice in Speech Synthesizer has a unique formant structure that dictates its "personality." [SRC: IL-MAN-06]

| Voice | Sonic Character | Best Use Case | Vibe Mapping |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Classic** | The standard 90s "computer" voice. | Producer tags, narrations. | All |
| **Giant** | Deep, resonant, and slow. | Scary intros, "Demon" voices. | Moody |
| **Child** | High-pitched, fast, and thin. | Hyper-pop, high-energy tags. | Upbeat |
| **Robot** | Highly metallic, buzzing formants. | Sci-fi textures, Vocoder-ish leads. | Psychedelic |
| **Old Man** | Raspy, unstable, and lower mid-heavy. | Gritty lo-fi intros. | Moody/Jazzy |
| **Whisper** | (Special Mode) No tonal pitch. | Intimate R&B backgrounds. | Vibey/Moody |

## Personality Mixing Tips
- **The "Pitch-Shifted Child":** Take the "Child" voice and pitch it DOWN by 1 octave. It creates an unsettling, "uncanny valley" effect.
- **The "BPM Syncing":** To make a voice fit your beat perfectly, generate a phrase and use the **Time Stretch** (Pro Transient) in the FL Sampler to align it to the grid.
- **Formant Filtering:** Run the "Robot" voice through a **Fruity Love Philter** with a formant preset to make it "talk" even more clearly.
