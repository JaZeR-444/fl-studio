# Goal: Vibe - Psychedelic

## Goal Statement
Create a "breathing" or "warping" effect that pulls and pushes the listener's attention.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Effect:** Add a heavy **Delay** BEFORE Transient Processor.
2. **Automation:** Create an automation clip for the **Release** knob.
3. **The Curve:** Draw a shape that goes from -15dB to +15dB every half-bar.
4. **Result:** The delay echoes will seem to "suck in" and then "explode" outward rhythmically.
5. **Mix:** Set to 60% for a "blend" of normal and warped time.

## What to Listen For
- A "pumping" effect that is more surgical than sidechaining.
- A sense of "disorientation" as the tails of the sound change volume rapidly.
