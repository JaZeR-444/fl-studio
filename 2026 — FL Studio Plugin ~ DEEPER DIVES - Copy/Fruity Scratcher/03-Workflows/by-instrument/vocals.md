# Fruity Scratcher — by-instrument: Vocals

## Vocal Scratching in Hip-Hop/R&B Production

Vocal scratching is the **most common use case** for Fruity Scratcher in modern hip-hop/R&B production. This guide covers techniques, sample selection, and vibe-specific vocal scratch workflows.

---

## Vocal Sample Selection

### Best Vocal Samples for Scratching

**Short stabs (0.5-2 seconds):**
- "Hey!", "Yeah!", "Fresh!", "Check it!", "Yo!"
- Classic hip-hop DJ drops
- Ad-libs from soul/funk records

**Sung phrases (2-4 seconds):**
- Melodic "ooh" or "ahh" vocals
- R&B ad-libs
- Gospel/soul vocal runs

**Spoken word (1-3 seconds):**
- Producer tags
- Voice-over snippets
- Motivational phrases

**What to avoid:**
- Full lead vocal phrases (too long, too complex)
- Overly processed vocals (heavy autotune, reverb)
- Copyrighted acapellas without clearance

## Mini-Recipe 1: Boom-Bap Vocal Scratch

**Use case:** Classic hip-hop vocal cuts on hooks or breaks.

### Parameters
- **SPD:** 1x (normal pitch)
- **ACC:** 80% (direct-drive responsiveness)
- **SEN:** 60% (balanced control)
- **Sample:** Dry vocal stab ("Hey!", "Fresh!"), 0.5-1.5 sec

### Step-by-Step
1. Load Scratcher with vocal stab
2. Set parameters as above
3. Scratch on downbeats or between kick/snare
4. Use baby scratch technique (forward-back-forward)
5. Keep density low: 2-4 scratches per 8 bars

### What to Listen For
- Clean transient at scratch start
- Pitch matches track key
- Scratches fit rhythmically
- Not over-scratched (listener fatigue)

### Automation Ideas
1. Automate SPD from 1x to 1.2x during scratch (pitch rise)
2. Automate channel volume for dynamic scratches

### Vibe Mapping
- **Upbeat:** SPD = 1.1x to 1.3x (brighter, more energetic)
- **Moody:** Use sparingly, darker vocal samples

## Mini-Recipe 2: R&B Ad-Lib Pitch Drop

**Use case:** Emotional pitch drops on vocal endings in R&B/pop-hip-hop.

### Parameters
- **SPD:** 1x → 0.6x (automated pitch drop)
- **ACC:** 55% (smooth transition)
- **Sample:** Sung ad-lib ("Yeah," "Ooh"), 2-4 sec

### Step-by-Step
1. Load sung vocal phrase
2. Automate SPD from 1x to 0.6x over last syllable (1-2 beats)
3. Add Fruity Reverb 2 (40% mix, 2-sec decay)
4. Place at end of vocal phrases in hook or bridge

### What to Listen For
- Smooth pitch drop (not stepped)
- Emotional "sinking" effect
- Reverb adds depth
- Fits song's minor-key vibe

### Automation Ideas
1. Automate ACC from 60% to 30% during drop (gets slower)
2. Pair with Parametric EQ 2 low-pass automation (filter closes as pitch drops)

### Vibe Mapping
- **Moody:** Use on minor-key vocals, drop to 0.5x-0.6x
- **Vibey:** Subtle drops (1x → 0.85x), minimal

## Mini-Recipe 3: Trap Vocal Cut (Quick Scratches)

**Use case:** Syncopated vocal cuts in trap hooks.

### Parameters
- **SPD:** 1x
- **ACC:** 90% (tight, responsive)
- **SEN:** 70% (high sensitivity for quick cuts)
- **Sample:** Short vocal ("Yeah," "Aye"), 0.5-1 sec

### Step-by-Step
1. Load short vocal sample
2. Set parameters for tight cuts
3. Scratch in off-beat positions (between hi-hat rolls)
4. Use transformer scratch (Hold button for rhythmic cuts)
5. Record performance to audio clip

### What to Listen For
- Tight, percussive cuts
- Syncopated timing
- Complements trap drums
- Not overused (maintain impact)

### Automation Ideas
1. Build scratch density before drop (1 cut → 4 cuts in 4 bars)
2. Automate SPD to 1.2x for brighter cuts in final bar

### Vibe Mapping
- **Upbeat:** Rapid cuts, high SPD (1.1x-1.3x)
- **Moody:** Use sparingly, darker samples

## Mini-Recipe 4: Lo-Fi Vocal Warble

**Use case:** Vintage vinyl texture on vocal loops (lo-fi hip-hop, chillhop).

### Parameters
- **SPD:** 0.92x ↔ 1.08x (slow sine wave automation)
- **ACC:** 35% (loose, analog feel)
- **Sample:** Jazz/soul vocal loop, 4-8 bars

### Step-by-Step
1. Load sustained vocal loop (sung or hummed)
2. Automate SPD with slow sine wave (0.92x to 1.08x over 2-4 bars)
3. Add Effector (vinyl mode, 30% mix)
4. Add Parametric EQ 2: Boost 200-500 Hz, cut 8 kHz+
5. Layer low in mix as background texture

### What to Listen For
- Subtle pitch wobble (barely noticeable)
- Warm, vintage character
- Blends into background
- Creates "dusty" lo-fi vibe

### Automation Ideas
1. Automate Effector mix from 20% to 50% during intro
2. Pair with tape stop at end of loop

### Vibe Mapping
- **Jazzy:** Perfect fit—use warm, swung vocal loops
- **Vibey:** Slow wobble (0.95x to 1.05x), minimal

## Parameter Moves for Vocal Scratching

### SPD Moves

**Pitch up (energetic):**
- SPD = 1.1x to 1.3x
- Use for upbeat, hype sections
- Brightens vocal, adds excitement

**Pitch down (dark/heavy):**
- SPD = 0.6x to 0.8x
- Use for moody, introspective sections
- Deepens vocal, adds weight

**Pitch bend (creative):**
- Automate SPD 0.5x ↔ 1.5x with curves
- Requires FX chain (reverb, delay)
- Psychedelic vibe

**Tape stop:**
- Automate SPD 1x → 0x
- ACC = 25-35% (gradual) or 85-95% (quick)
- Transition effect

---

### ACC Moves

**Tight control (modern):**
- ACC = 80-95%
- Direct-drive turntable feel
- Boom-bap, trap, modern hip-hop

**Loose control (vintage):**
- ACC = 30-50%
- Belt-drive turntable feel
- Lo-fi, jazzy, chillhop

**Dynamic ACC (experimental):**
- Automate ACC during scratching [UNVERIFIED]
- Creates evolving scratch character

---

### SEN Moves

**Responsive scratching:**
- SEN = 65-80%
- For expressive, rapid scratching
- Upbeat, trap, performance-oriented

**Controlled scratching:**
- SEN = 45-60%
- For precise, minimal scratching
- Moody, jazzy, vibey

**Static (automation-driven):**
- SEN = N/A (not manually scratching)
- For automated tape effects

## Vibe Mapping: Vocal Scratching

### Moody Vocal Scratching
- **Sample:** Dark, minor-key vocals
- **SPD:** 0.6x to 0.8x (pitch down)
- **ACC:** 30-50% (slow, heavy)
- **Technique:** Minimal scratching, slow pitch drops, reverse textures
- **FX:** Reverb 2 (60% mix, long decay), low-pass EQ

### Upbeat Vocal Scratching
- **Sample:** Bright, energetic vocals
- **SPD:** 1.1x to 1.3x (pitch up)
- **ACC:** 85-95% (tight, responsive)
- **Technique:** Rapid cuts, syncopated scratches, quick tape stops
- **FX:** EQ boost 3-5 kHz (presence), limiter

### Psychedelic Vocal Scratching
- **Sample:** Sustained vocals, synths
- **SPD:** 0.5x ↔ 1.5x (wide pitch bends with curved automation)
- **ACC:** 40-60% (smooth bends)
- **Technique:** Warped pitch automation, reverse glitches
- **FX CHAIN (CRITICAL):** Reverb 2 (50%) + Delay 3 (30%) + Phaser

### Jazzy Vocal Scratching
- **Sample:** Warm, vintage jazz vocals
- **SPD:** 0.95x ↔ 1.05x (subtle wobble)
- **ACC:** 50-70% (moderate, swung feel)
- **Technique:** Swung timing (not grid-locked), lazy tape stops
- **FX:** Effector (vinyl), Parametric EQ 2 (warmth)

### Vibey Vocal Scratching
- **Sample:** Smooth ad-libs, pads
- **SPD:** 0.95x → 1.05x (slow pitch drift)
- **ACC:** 35-50% (smooth, loose)
- **Technique:** Minimal scratching, sustained reverse textures
- **FX:** Reverb 2 (60%, long decay), Delay 3 (30%)

## Vocal Scratch Placement in Arrangement

### Intro
- **Reverse vocal textures** (vibey, psychedelic)
- **Subtle pitch wobble** (jazzy, lo-fi)
- **Build anticipation** with reverse swells

### Verse
- **Minimal scratching** (1-2 cuts per 8 bars)
- **Background textures** (reverse vocals low in mix)
- **Don't distract** from rap vocals

### Hook/Chorus
- **Vocal cuts on downbeats** or between kick/snare
- **Boom-bap scratches** (classic hip-hop)
- **Trap vocal cuts** (syncopated, tight)

### Bridge
- **Tape stops** (transitions into new sections)
- **Pitch drops** (emotional R&B ad-libs)
- **Creative effects** (psychedelic pitch bends)

### Outro
- **Reverse textures fading out**
- **Lazy tape stops** (don't reach full stop)
- **Lo-fi wobble** (vintage outro vibe)

## Vocal Scratch Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Vocal scratches sound harsh/brittle  
**Solution:** Add Parametric EQ 2, low-pass at 6-8 kHz, cut 2-4 kHz if harsh

**Problem:** Scratches clash with lead vocals  
**Solution:** Use sidechain compression (duck scratches when lead vocals play)

**Problem:** Scratches disappear in mono  
**Solution:** Center scratches, avoid wide stereo enhancement, check mono fold-down

**Problem:** Vocal sample has too much reverb  
**Solution:** Use dry sample, add reverb in FL Studio for control

**Problem:** Scratch timing drifts off-beat  
**Solution:** Enable metronome, practice with click, quantize recorded scratches
