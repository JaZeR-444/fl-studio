# Goal: Vibe - Jazzy

## Goal Statement
Create warm, sophisticated acoustic piano or electric bass for jazzy R&B chords.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Instrument:** Load a high-quality "Rhodes" or "Acoustic Piano" (e.g., from SGM-V2.0).
2. **Resampling:** **Extreme HQ**.
3. **LFO:** Set Freq to 3Hz, Amp to 1.5% (Very subtle drift).
4. **Chorus:** Enable internal Chorus for that "expensive" Rhodes sheen.
5. **Velocity:** In the Piano Roll, ensure your chords have varied velocities (lower for the middle notes, higher for the top melodic note).

## What to Listen For
- The "wood" of the piano and the "warmth" of the electric keys.
- A professional, "classy" sound that doesn't feel like a cheap MIDI file.
