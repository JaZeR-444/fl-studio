# Genre Style Board: Transient Processor

## Hip-Hop
- **Technique:** "Dry Break Flip." Use a soul drum break. Set Release to -100%. This removes all the "room" from the break, leaving only the "staccato" hits. Layer your own high-end reverb.
- **Reference Idea:** Think of the tight, dry drum textures in early Pharrell or current Kanye West (Donda era) productions.

## Rap
- **Technique:** "Aggressive Snare Crack." Boost Attack to +8dB. Turn Drive to 15%. This creates a "toxic" and "loud" snare that defines the rhythm of a modern Trap track.
- **Reference Idea:** The sharp, piercing snares in Lex Luger or Southside productions.

## R&B / Pop-Hip-Hop
- **Technique:** "Silk Vocal Softening." Reduce Attack by -3dB on the lead vocal bus. This removes the "harsh" consonants without needing a de-esser, making the vocal feel smooth and professional.
- **Reference Idea:** The silky, intimate vocal presence in modern R&B like Summer Walker or SZA.
