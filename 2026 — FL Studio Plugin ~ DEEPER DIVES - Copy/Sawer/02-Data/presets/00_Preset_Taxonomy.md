# Preset Taxonomy

## Bank Structure
Sawer's factory presets are categorized by their role in a vintage or electronic production. [SRC: IL-MAN-02]

### Categories
1. **Bass:** Aggressive, snappy, and deep. (Good for Upbeat/Moody)
2. **Lead:** Hard sync, singing, and tearing sounds. (Good for Upbeat/Psychedelic)
3. **Pads:** Lush, detuned, and evolving textures. (Good for Vibey/Moody)
4. **Arp:** Rhythmic sequences and noise-based hits. (Good for Upbeat)
5. **Keys:** Analog electric pianos and organ-like tones. (Good for Jazzy/Lo-Fi)

## Vibe Tagging Strategy
When organizing custom Sawer patches:
- `[VINT]` - For authentic 80s Polivoks sounds.
- `[GRIT]` - For highly resonant/distorted "stinky" patches.
- `[CLEAN]` - For jazzy/sophisticated keys.
- `[DRFT]` - For patches with significant pitch LFO/drift.

## Auditioning Tips
- **The "Res" Test:** Turn the Resonance up to 50%. If the sound starts to "scream" in a musical way, it's a good Polivoks-style patch.
- **The "Sub" Test:** Mute the main Saw wave. If the Sub-oscillator sounds solid and "round," the patch is well-built for modern bass.
