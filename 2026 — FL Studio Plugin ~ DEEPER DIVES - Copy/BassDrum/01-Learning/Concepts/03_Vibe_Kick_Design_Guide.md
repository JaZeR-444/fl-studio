# Vibe Kick Design Guide: BassDrum

Translating professional drum synthesis into specific Hip-Hop and R&B aesthetic targets.

---

## 1. MOODY
*Darkness, weight, and "heavy" muffled thumps.*
- **The Lever**: **End Freq Priority + Zero Drive + Low Pass Sample**.
- **Move**: Set End Freq to 35Hz. Turn off all distortion. Use a "Dark" sample layer.
- **What to Listen For**: A "soft" heavy punch that feels like a weight being dropped on carpet. It provides the sub-foundation without being distracting.
- **Don't Do This**: High "Start" frequencies (too aggressive).

## 2. UPBEAT
*Energy, sharp transients, and driving "knock".*
- **The Lever**: **High Start Freq + Fast Pitch Decay + Hard Drive**.
- **Move**: Set Start Freq to 800Hz. Pitch Decay at 15% (Tight). Use "Hard" Drive at 40%.
- **What to Listen For**: A sharp, percussive "knock" that hits the chest. The fast decay ensures the kick stays out of the way of the following notes.
- **Don't Do This**: Long sustain/tail (it slows the energy down).

## 3. PSYCHEDELIC
*Disorientation, shifting textures, and "trippy" resonant tails.*
- **The Lever**: **Automated Feedback + Sample Layer Sweeps**.
- **Move**: Link the **Drive Amount** to an LFO. Set the **Offset** in the Sample tab to modulate.
- **What to Listen For**: A kick that seems to "morph" or "scream" during the transition, adding a hallucinogenic grit to the low end.
- **Don't Do This**: Fixed, static settings.

## 4. JAZZY
*Warmth, organic focus, and "natural" woodiness.*
- **The Lever**: **Medium Start Freq + Sine Wave + Low Gain**.
- **Move**: Set Start Freq to 200Hz. Use only the Base Sine oscillator. Keep Res at zero. 
- **What to Listen For**: A "rounded" tone that emphasizes the body of the drum. It feels "human" and "wooden," mimicking a felt-covered beater.
- **Don't Do This**: Digital noise or clicks (too artificial).

## 5. VIBEY
*Modern luxury, expensive sheen, and smooth motion.*
- **The Lever**: **Tube Drive + Wide Sample Filter + 10k Shelf**.
- **Move**: Use "Tube" drive at 20%. Keep the Sample layer bright but subtle. Use a fast Pitch Decay.
- **What to Listen For**: A wide, high-budget sheen that fits perfectly into modern R&B. It should feel powerful but "silky."
- **Don't Do This**: Over-saturating the sub (ruins the professional focus).
