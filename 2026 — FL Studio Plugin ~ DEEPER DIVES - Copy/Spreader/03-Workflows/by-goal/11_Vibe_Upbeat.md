# Goal: Vibe - Upbeat

## Goal Statement
Create an explosive, massive stereo image for high-energy synth leads or percussion.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Width:** 100%.
2. **Separation:** Drag toward "Separated." This aggressively pushes sound into the side speakers.
3. **Low Bypass:** 100Hz with **Mono** ON. This ensures the kick/snare energy doesn't weaken.
4. **Vectorscope:** You should see a large "flower" or "circle."
5. **Result:** A sound that feels like it's jumping out of the speakers at the listener.

## What to Listen For
- Maximum spatial impact.
- An increase in "energy" and "excitement" in the track.
