# Goal: The Perfect Mono Vocal Widen

## Goal Statement
Take a mono-recorded lead vocal and give it a "stereo" presence that sounds expensive and professional.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Width:** Set **Width** to 35%. Subtle is better for leads.
2. **Separation:** Drag the **Separation** slider slightly toward "Merged." This ensures the "body" of the voice doesn't get pushed too far to the sides.
3. **Low Bypass:** Set **Low Bypass** to 300Hz. This keeps the chest-resonance of the vocal in mono.
4. **Vectorscope:** You should see a "narrow leaf" shape. It should not be a full "flower."
5. **Level:** Increase **Gain** by +1dB to compensate for the stereo expansion.

## What to Listen For
- The vocal should feel "larger" but still sound like it's coming from the center.
- There should be no "hollowness" or "robotic" phase artifacts.

## Variations
- **The Whisper Widener:** Set Width to 100% for whispered ad-libs to make them feel like they are "outside" the head.
- **The Harmony Spread:** Route all backing harmonies to a bus and use Spreader at 60% Width.
