# Parameter Cheat Sheet: BassDrum

The essential modifiers for the professional kick engine. [SRC: IL-MAN]

---

## 🕒 BASE Section (The Core)
- **Start Freq**: The initial pitch. Controls the "Knock."
- **End Freq**: The final pitch. Controls the "Sub."
- **Pitch Decay**: How fast the pitch falls from Start to End.
- **Amp Decay**: How long the volume lasts.

## 🎨 SAMPLE Section (The Transient)
- **Sample Selection**: Choose the internal click/noise layer.
- **Drive (Sample)**: Saturation for the noise layer only.
- **Filter (HP/LP)**: Shapes the tone of the click.
- **Offset**: Delay the click relative to the sub.

## 🔥 DRIVE Section (The Grit)
- **Type**: Soft, Hard, Clip, Tube.
- **Amount**: Intensity of the saturation.
- **Master Level**: Final gain adjustment.

## 📐 Global Modifiers
- **Phase**: Adjusts the starting phase of the sine oscillator.
- **Smoothing**: Prevents digital "pops" during parameter automation.
