# Goal: Vibe - Psychedelic

## Goal Statement
Create a warping, shifting stereo field that makes the listener feel like the room is moving.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Width Automation:** Create an automation clip for **Width**.
2. **The Wave:** Draw a slow, oscillating Sine wave from 0% to 100%.
3. **Separation Automation:** Create a second automation clip for **Separation**.
4. **The Counter-Wave:** Draw a faster Triangle wave.
5. **Result:** The sound will expand and contract while also shifting its balance between the center and sides.

## What to Listen For
- A "breathing" effect.
- A sense of "disorientation" that adds to the psychedelic experience.
