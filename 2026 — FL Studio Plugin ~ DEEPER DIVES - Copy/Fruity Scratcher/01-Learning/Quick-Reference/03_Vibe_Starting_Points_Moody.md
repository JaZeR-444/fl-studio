# Fruity Scratcher - Vibe Starting Points: Moody

## Overview

"**Moody**" in hip-hop/R&B/pop-hip-hop production translates to:
- Dark, introspective, melancholic sonic character
- Slow tempos or half-time feels (60-90 BPM or 140 BPM trap at half-time)
- Minor keys, low-end emphasis, subdued highs
- Sparse rhythmic movement, sustained textures
- Reverb/delay for depth and isolation

**Scratcher's role in moody production:**
- Slow tape stops for dramatic, sad transitions
- Reverse effects for eerie, unsettling textures
- Pitch-down vocal scratches for haunting atmosphere
- Minimal, intentional scratching (not aggressive turntablism)

## Moody Setting #1: Dark Tape Stop

**When to use:** Transitioning into a moody verse or bridge.

### Parameters

| Parameter | Value | Reasoning |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| **SPD** | 1x → 0x (automate) | Gradual deceleration into silence [SRC: IL-MAN] |
| **ACC** | 20-30% | Very low = slow, haunting stop |
| **SEN** | N/A | Automation-driven |
| **Duration** | 4-8 beats | Long, drawn-out stop |
| **Sample** | Full beat, pad, or bass | Low-frequency content reinforces darkness |

### Setup

1. Insert Scratcher on drum bus or melodic element
2. Create automation clip on SPD
3. Automate from 1x to 0x over 2 bars (8 beats at 70 BPM)
4. Set ACC to 25% for maximum slowdown curve

### What to Listen For

- [ ] Pitch drops smoothly and gradually (not stepped)
- [ ] Low frequencies dominate as it slows (highs fade first)
- [ ] Reaches silence exactly at the start of the moody section
- [ ] Creates emotional "weight" (feels heavy, not playful)

### Variations

**Variation 1: Reverse Entry**
- After the stop (SPD = 0x), automate SPD from 0x → -0.5x
- Creates eerie reverse swell into the moody section

**Variation 2: Pitch Down Only (No Full Stop)**
- Automate SPD from 1x → 0.6x (slow, but still playing)
- Sustain at 0.6x for moody, "drugged" feel

### Pitfalls

- **Don't:** Use high ACC (> 40%)—creates "bouncy" stop, breaks mood
- **Don't:** Stop too quickly (< 2 beats)—loses emotional impact
- **Don't:** Use bright/harsh samples—kills moody vibe

### Automation Ideas

1. **Dynamic stop:** Automate ACC from 30% → 10% during the stop (gets slower as it decelerates)
2. **Filter combo:** Pair with Parametric EQ 2, automate low-pass filter closing during stop

## Moody Setting #2: Haunting Reverse Vocal

**When to use:** Creating atmospheric background textures or intros.

### Parameters

| Parameter | Value | Reasoning |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| **SPD** | -0.5x to -1x | Reverse playback at slow to normal speed [SRC: IL-MAN] |
| **ACC** | 40-50% | Moderate response for smooth reverse |
| **SEN** | N/A | Static reverse playback |
| **Sample** | Vocal phrase (3-5 sec) | Sung phrases or ad-libs work best |

### Setup

1. Load a vocal sample (preferably minor-key or melancholic)
2. Set SPD to -0.7x (reverse at 70% speed)
3. No scratching—let it play reversed continuously
4. Layer with reverb (Fruity Reeverb 2, 30-50% mix)

### What to Listen For

- [ ] Eerie, backwards vocal texture
- [ ] Pitch is slightly lower than original (moody depth)
- [ ] Blends into background (not aggressive or distracting)
- [ ] Creates "haunted" or "ghostly" atmosphere

### Variations

**Variation 1: Slow-Motion Reverse**
- SPD = -0.3x (very slow reverse)
- Creates "drowning" or "underwater" vocal effect

**Variation 2: Reverse Scratch**
- Set SPD to -1x, manually scratch with mouse
- Creates unsettling, glitchy reverse cuts

### Pitfalls

- **Don't:** Use bright, energetic vocals—breaks moody vibe
- **Don't:** Set SPD too fast (< -1x)—loses haunting quality
- **Don't:** Skip reverb/delay—dry reverse vocals sound too harsh

### Automation Ideas

1. **Fading reverse:** Automate SPD from -1x → -0.2x over 8 bars (slows down gradually)
2. **Reverse rise:** Automate SPD from 0x → -1x (reverse "swell" into a section)

## Moody Setting #3: Pitch-Down Vocal Scratch

**When to use:** Moody vocal chops in verses or hooks.

### Parameters

| Parameter | Value | Reasoning |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| **SPD** | 0.6x to 0.8x | Lower pitch for dark, heavy feel |
| **ACC** | 60-70% | Moderate response for controlled scratching |
| **SEN** | 50-60% | Balanced control |
| **Sample** | Vocal stab (1-2 sec) | "Yeah," "Ooh," "Hey" pitched down |

### Setup

1. Load a short vocal sample
2. Set SPD to 0.7x (30% slower = deeper pitch)
3. Scratch minimally—1-2 scratches per 4 bars
4. Use on downbeats or in empty spaces (not rapid scratching)

### What to Listen For

- [ ] Vocal sounds deeper, more masculine/haunting
- [ ] Scratching is slow and intentional (not frantic)
- [ ] Fits in minor-key context
- [ ] Creates tension, not energy

### Variations

**Variation 1: Extreme Pitch-Down**
- SPD = 0.4x to 0.5x (very deep, almost bass-like vocal)
- Use sparingly for dramatic impact

**Variation 2: Pitch-Down + Hold**
- Scratch forward at 0.7x, press Hold to freeze at peak
- Creates "stuck" vocal effect (moody stutter)

### Pitfalls

- **Don't:** Scratch rapidly—breaks moody mood
- **Don't:** Use SPD > 0.9x—not enough pitch shift for moody character
- **Don't:** Overuse—moody scratching is about restraint

### Automation Ideas

1. **Gradual pitch drop:** Automate SPD from 1x → 0.5x during a scratch
2. **Pitch wobble:** Subtle LFO on SPD (0.65x to 0.75x) for unstable, anxious feel

## Moody Setting #4: Minimal Texture Scratch

**When to use:** Background atmosphere in sparse arrangements.

### Parameters

| Parameter | Value | Reasoning |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| **SPD** | 0.8x to 1.1x | Subtle pitch variation |
| **ACC** | 30-40% | Slow, loose response |
| **SEN** | 40-50% | Gentle control |
| **Sample** | Pad, ambient texture, or jazz loop | Sustained, non-percussive |

### Setup

1. Load a sustained sound (not a transient-heavy sample)
2. Set SPD to 0.9x (slightly slower than original)
3. Slowly drag platter back and forth (no aggressive scratching)
4. Create gentle pitch wobbles—human imperfection

### What to Listen For

- [ ] Subtle pitch movement (barely noticeable)
- [ ] Warm, analog character
- [ ] Blends into the background (not a focal point)
- [ ] Creates "broken" or "warped" texture

### Variations

**Variation 1: Automate Slow Wobble**
- Automate SPD with slow LFO curve (0.8x to 1x over 2 bars)
- Creates "tape warble" effect

**Variation 2: Reverse Texture**
- SPD = -0.9x (reverse at slightly slower speed)
- Pair with heavy reverb for ghostly pad

### Pitfalls

- **Don't:** Use percussive samples—too jarring for moody background
- **Don't:** Scratch aggressively—breaks subtle texture
- **Don't:** Set SEN too high—creates unintentional jumps

### Automation Ideas

1. **Breathing texture:** Automate SPD in slow sine wave (0.85x to 0.95x)
2. **Detuned pad:** SPD at 0.93x (slightly flat) for dissonant, moody character

## Moody Setting #5: Long Reverse Swell

**When to use:** Building tension into a moody drop or verse.

### Parameters

| Parameter | Value | Reasoning |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| **SPD** | 0x → -1x (automate) | Reverse swell from silence |
| **ACC** | 35-45% | Gradual acceleration |
| **SEN** | N/A | Automation-driven |
| **Duration** | 4-8 beats | Long build |
| **Sample** | Cymbal, crash, or vocal | Transient-rich for impact |

### Setup

1. Load a sample with a strong ending (e.g., vocal phrase, cymbal crash)
2. Automate SPD from 0x to -1x over 2 bars
3. Set ACC to 40% for smooth ramp-up
4. The sample will "swell" in reverse, ending at the downbeat

### What to Listen For

- [ ] Reverse playback gradually speeds up
- [ ] Creates anticipation and tension
- [ ] Peaks exactly at the target downbeat
- [ ] Sounds "pulling" into the next section

### Variations

**Variation 1: Slow Reverse Swell**
- SPD = 0x → -0.5x (slower reverse, less intense)

**Variation 2: Reverse + Filter**
- Pair with automated low-pass filter opening during swell

### Pitfalls

- **Don't:** Use short samples—reverse swell needs material to "reveal"
- **Don't:** Rush the swell (< 2 beats)—loses tension-building effect
- **Don't:** Use high ACC—creates sudden, non-moody start

### Automation Ideas

1. **Accelerating swell:** Automate ACC from 30% → 60% during the swell (speeds up as it goes)
2. **Pitch bend swell:** Automate SPD from 0x → -1.2x (slight pitch overshoot at end)

## Moody Workflow Checklist

### Sample Selection (Critical for Moody)
- [ ] Use minor-key or atonal samples
- [ ] Prefer low-frequency content (bass, male vocals, sub pads)
- [ ] Avoid bright, energetic samples
- [ ] Choose dry samples (add reverb/delay in FL Studio for control)

### Parameter Guidelines
- [ ] ACC: 20-50% (slow, heavy response)
- [ ] SPD: Favor < 1x (pitch down) or reverse playback
- [ ] SEN: 40-60% (controlled, not twitchy)
- [ ] Avoid rapid scratching—moody is about restraint

### Mix Context
- [ ] Low-pass filter at 5-8 kHz (cut brightness)
- [ ] Reverb with 2-4 second decay (depth and space)
- [ ] Pair with dark chords (minor 7th, diminished, suspended)
- [ ] Use in sparse arrangements (not dense, aggressive beats)

### What to Avoid
- ❌ High ACC (> 60%)—too responsive, breaks moody drag
- ❌ Bright/harsh samples—kills dark vibe
- ❌ Rapid scratching—too energetic for moody
- ❌ Dry signal—moody needs reverb/delay for depth

## Genre-Specific Moody Applications

### Moody Hip-Hop/Trap
- Long tape stops (4-8 beats) before verses
- Pitch-down vocal scratches on minor-key hooks
- Reverse swells into 808 drops
- Minimal scratching (1-2 cuts per 8 bars)

**Example artists/styles:** Drake ("Marvin's Room"), Travis Scott (moody intros), 21 Savage

### Moody R&B
- Slow reverse vocal textures on verses
- Pitch-down ad-lib scratches ("Yeah" at 0.6x SPD)
- Tape stops on bridges (emotional transitions)
- Subtle texture scratching on pads

**Example artists/styles:** The Weeknd, Jhené Aiko, PARTYNEXTDOOR

### Lo-Fi/Moody Chillhop
- Constant slow tape warble (SPD = 0.85x to 0.95x wobble)
- Reverse jazz loop scratches
- Minimal, intentional scratching for "human" imperfection
- Heavy reverb on all scratched elements

**Example artists/styles:** Nujabes, Joji, moody lo-fi playlists
