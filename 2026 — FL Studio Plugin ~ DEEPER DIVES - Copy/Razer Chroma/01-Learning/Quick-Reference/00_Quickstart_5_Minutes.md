# Quickstart: 5 Minutes to Light Show

1.  **Install:** Ensure Razer Synapse 3 is installed and running.
2.  **Load:** Drag **Razer Chroma** plugin onto the **Master** Mixer track (Slot 10).
3.  **Check:** Look at the bottom of the plugin window. It should say "Connected".
4.  **Effect:** Click the dropdown, select **Audio Level**.
5.  **Play:** Play your song.
6.  **Witness:** Your Razer keyboard should now be bouncing green/yellow/red bars along with the music volume.
