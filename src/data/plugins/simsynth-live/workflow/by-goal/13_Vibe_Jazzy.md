# Goal: Vibe - Jazzy

## Goal Statement
Create warm, sophisticated analog keys or electric bass for jazzy hip-hop chords.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Oscillator:** Osc 1 (Sine), Osc 2 (Square). Balance them 50/50.
2. **Filter:** LP Mix 100%. Cutoff 300Hz (Mellow).
3. **Envelopes:** Attack 40ms, Release 500ms.
4. **Vibrato:** LFO -> Pitch (Amount 1%, Speed 3Hz).
5. **Chorus:** Mix 15% to add just a tiny bit of "shimmer."

## What to Listen For
- A "round" tone that fits perfectly between a kick and a snare.
- A clean, articulate sound that emphasizes the harmony of your chords.
