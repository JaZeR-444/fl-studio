# Quick Reference: Image to Sound Workflows

A step-by-step process for generating audio from visuals in BeepMap. [SRC: IL-MAN]

## 1. Importing the Source
- **Action**: Open your File Explorer.
- **Move**: Drag a JPEG or BMP file directly onto the BeepMap window.
- **Goal**: You will see the image appear in the preview box.

## 2. Setting the "Speed of Time"
- **Move**: Adjust the **Length (ms)** knob.
- **Strategy**: 
  - For **Drums/SFX**: Set to 100-300ms.
  - For **Pads/Atmospheres**: Set to 2000-5000ms.

## 3. Defining the Musicality
- **Move**: Ensure the **Log** (Logarithmic) switch is active.
- **Action**: Play a note on your keyboard. 
- **Goal**: If the sound is too high-pitched, lower the **Freq (Hz)** knob.

## 4. Refining the Texture (Grain)
- **Move**: Tweak the **Grain** knob.
- **Listen For**: The moment the sound becomes "solid" vs "stepped."
- **Pro Tip**: Higher grain can add a "cool" digital artifacts texture to lo-fi beats.

## 5. Adding Space (External)
- BeepMap sounds very "dry" and digital. 
- **Move**: Route it to a mixer track with **Fruity Convolver** or **Delay 3**.
- **Result**: The image-sound becomes a vast, immersive environment.
