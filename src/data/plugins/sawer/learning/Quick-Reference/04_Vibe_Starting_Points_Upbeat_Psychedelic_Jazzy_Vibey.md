# 04: Vibe Starting Points - Upbeat, Psychedelic, Jazzy, Vibey

## 1. Upbeat (High-Energy Leads)
- **Moves:** `Sync: ON`, `Resonance: 50%`, `Mono Mode: ON`, `Chorus: OFF`.
- **Key Move:** Automate the Slave Oscillator pitch for "screaming" sync sweeps.

## 2. Psychedelic (Trippy Movement)
- **Moves:** `LFO -> Pan: 50%`, `LFO -> Filter: 30%`, `Phaser: ON (Mix 50%)`, `Unison Detune: 25%`.
- **Key Move:** Use a random LFO waveform for unpredictable "glitchy" filtering.

## 3. Jazzy (Warm Keys)
- **Moves:** `Attack: 30ms`, `LP Filter: 40%`, `Unison: 2 Voices`, `Velocity -> Filter: 50%`.
- **Key Move:** Keep the Resonance at 0% to avoid any "aggressive" Soviet grit.

## 4. Vibey (Lush R&B Pads)
- **Moves:** `Unison: 8 Voices`, `Pan: 100%`, `Reverb: Mix 30% (Large)`, `Decay/Release: 1.5s`.
- **Key Move:** Detune the Unison just enough to hear the "chorusing" but not enough to lose the pitch.

## Automation Idea (Upbeat)
- **Arp Speed:** Automate the Arpeggiator speed from 1/16 to 1/32 during the final bar of a phrase for a "roll" effect.
