# Goal: Vibe - Upbeat

## Goal Statement
Create a high-pitched, fast "chipmunk" vocal tag for energetic pop-trap.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Voice:** Select **Child**.
2. **Pitch:** +12 semitones (1 Octave).
3. **Rate:** 130% (Fast).
4. **Render:** Hit OK.
5. **Mixer FX:**
   - **Fruity Limiter (Comp):** Flatten the dynamics to make it "pop."
   - **Soundgoodizer:** Preset A for instant "sheen."

## What to Listen For
- A bright, "kawaii" or "hyper-pop" energy.
- Crisp transients that cut through a loud 808 and snare.
