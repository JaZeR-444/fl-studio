# 02: Best Settings Starting Points

## Aggressive Trap Bass
- **Osc:** Saw 70%, Sub 100%.
- **Filter:** LP 24dB, Cutoff 15%, Res 20%.
- **Env:** Filter Env Amount 40%, Decay 400ms.
- **Mode:** Mono.

## 80s Synthwave Lead
- **Osc:** Saw 100%, Noise 5%.
- **Unison:** 4 voices, Detune 15%, Pan 100%.
- **Filter:** LP 12dB, Cutoff 60%.
- **FX:** Chorus and Delay enabled.

## Lo-Fi Rhodes / Keys
- **Osc:** Saw 50%, Sub 20%.
- **Filter:** LP 12dB, Cutoff 40%, Res 10%.
- **Env:** Amp Release 300ms.
- **Mod:** LFO -> Pitch (1% depth, 3Hz speed).

## Cinematic Brass Stab
- **Osc:** Saw 100%, Sub 50%.
- **Filter:** LP 24dB, Cutoff 30%.
- **Env:** Amp Attack 50ms, Filter Env Amount 60%, Decay 1s.
- **Unison:** 8 voices, Detune 5% (Tight).
