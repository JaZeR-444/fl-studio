# Fruity Slicer 2 - Vibe Translation Guide

## Production Levers: From Adjectives to Actions

This guide translates the 5 vibe targets into **concrete Fruity Slicer 2 moves**. Each vibe section includes 6-10 production levers you can pull, what to listen for, and 2 critical "don't do this" pitfalls.

---

## 1. MOODY

### Vibe Definition (Production Terms)
- **Emotional quality**: Introspective, melancholic, dark, contemplative
- **Sonic characteristics**: Filtered highs, slow movement, reverberant space, minor tonality
- **Mix context**: Spacious, understated, low energy but emotionally heavy

### Production Levers (Fruity Slicer 2)

#### Lever 1: Reversed Vocal Slices
**Action:** Reverse slices 3, 5, 7 (vowel sounds).  
**Result:** "Sucked in" effect creates tension before downbeat.  
**Settings:** Right-click slice → Reverse. Place reversed slice 1 beat before main vocal.  
**Listen For:** Smooth reverse tail vs. abrupt cutoff (add Release envelope if needed).  
[SRC: SAMPLING]

#### Lever 2: Low-Pass Filter Envelope (Dark Tone)
**Action:** Apply filter envelope to all slices: Cutoff 200Hz → 800Hz over 500ms.  
**Result:** Slices start muffled, open slightly, stay dark.  
**Settings:** Filter envelope: Attack 0ms, Decay 500ms, Sustain 30%, Release 200ms.  
**Listen For:** Vowels should sound "underwater," consonants barely audible.  
[SRC: IL-MAN]

#### Lever 3: Slow Time Stretch (60-80 BPM Feel)
**Action:** Time stretch sample to 60-75% original tempo.  
**Result:** Slowed, syrupy movement (even in 140 BPM project).  
**Settings:** Use "Pro" algorithm for transparent slow-down.  
**Listen For:** Pitch artifacts (should stay natural sounding, not robotic).  
[SRC: IL-MAN]

#### Lever 4: Pitch Down (-3 to -7 Semitones)
**Action:** Global pitch slider -5 semitones.  
**Result:** Darker, deeper tonality (minor key vibe even from major sample).  
**Settings:** Pitch -5st, then chromatic mode for playable minor melodies.  
**Listen For:** Detuned feel creates melancholy; too low (<-7st) sounds comical.  
[SRC: HIPHOP]

#### Lever 5: Long Release Envelopes (Trailing Echoes)
**Action:** Volume envelope: Attack 10ms, Release 800-1200ms.  
**Result:** Slices fade out slowly, creating ghostly trails.  
**Settings:** Apply to sustained slices (pads, vocals), not transients (kicks).  
**Listen For:** Overlapping slices create reverb-like wash.  
[SRC: IL-KB]

#### Lever 6: Minor Key Slice Selection
**Action:** If using chromatic mode, select slices that imply minor intervals (b3, b7).  
**Result:** Instant sad/dark vibe.  
**Settings:** Play slices at C, Eb, G, Bb (Cm7 chord).  
**Listen For:** Avoid major 3rd (E) — sounds too happy.  
[SRC: Music Theory]

#### Lever 7: Sparse Slice Triggering (Less Is More)
**Action:** Delete every other slice marker (halve slice count).  
**Result:** Slowed, breathing rhythm; gaps create tension.  
**Settings:** Manual delete: keep beats 1 & 3, remove 2 & 4.  
**Listen For:** Silence between hits should feel intentional, not empty.  
[SRC: HIPHOP]

#### Lever 8: Mono Low-End Slices (Centered Weight)
**Action:** Pan envelope: Center (0%) for bass/kick slices.  
**Result:** Moody low-end stays focused, grounded.  
**Settings:** Kicks/bass center, only ambient slices pan slightly.  
**Listen For:** Bass should anchor the track, not wander.  
[SRC: Mixing]

### What To Listen For (Moody)
- **Tonality**: Predominantly minor or suspended (avoid bright major intervals)
- **Movement**: Slow, deliberate (no rushed transients)
- **Space**: Reverberant, distant (long release envelopes create space)
- **Frequency Balance**: Dark (filtered highs, emphasized low-mids 200-500Hz)

### DON'T Do This (Moody Pitfalls)

#### Pitfall 1: Upbeat Slice Patterns
**Mistake:** Fast, syncopated slice triggering (16th note hi-hats).  
**Why It Fails:** Creates energy, contradicts introspective vibe.  
**Fix:** Halve trigger rate (1/8 notes max), add silence between slices.

#### Pitfall 2: Bright, Wide Stereo Slices
**Mistake:** High-pass filter + extreme stereo width on all slices.  
**Why It Fails:** Brightness = cheerful, width = expansive/uplifting. Moody is focused/dark.  
**Fix:** Low-pass filter, narrow stereo field, center low-end.

## 2. UPBEAT

### Vibe Definition (Production Terms)
- **Emotional quality**: Energetic, optimistic, driving, motivational
- **Sonic characteristics**: Bright highs, rhythmic punch, forward momentum, major tonality
- **Mix context**: Dense, energetic, high-impact transients

### Production Levers (Fruity Slicer 2)

#### Lever 1: Fast Hi-Hat Slices (Rhythmic Density)
**Action:** Auto-slice hi-hats at 90-100% sensitivity.  
**Result:** Captures every transient → dense 16th/32nd note rolls.  
**Settings:** Slice sensitivity 95%, map to C5-C6 range.  
**Listen For:** Clean separation between hits (no overlapping tails).  
[SRC: HIPHOP]

#### Lever 2: Punchy Kick Slices (Transient Emphasis)
**Action:** Volume envelope: Attack 0ms, Decay 50ms, Sustain 0%, Release 20ms.  
**Result:** Kicks punch hard, decay fast (no mud).  
**Settings:** Apply to kick slices only.  
**Listen For:** Kick should hit chest, not linger.  
[SRC: IL-MAN]

#### Lever 3: High-Pass Filter Envelope (Brightness)
**Action:** Filter envelope: Start 500Hz, end 8kHz over 100ms.  
**Result:** Slices start bright, stay bright (air and sparkle).  
**Settings:** High-pass mode, fast attack envelope.  
**Listen For:** Hi-hats, snares should sizzle; avoid thin bass.  
[SRC: Mixing]

#### Lever 4: Upward Pitch Envelope (+2 to +5 Semitones)
**Action:** Pitch envelope: 0st → +3st over 50ms on snare slices.  
**Result:** Pitch rise creates uplifting energy (opposite of tape stop).  
**Settings:** Apply to fills/transitions only (not every hit).  
**Listen For:** Subtle rise feels exciting, not cartoonish.  
[SRC: IL-KB]

#### Lever 5: Staccato Release Envelopes (Bouncy)
**Action:** Volume envelope: Release 30-50ms (short decay).  
**Result:** Slices don't overlap → clean, bouncy feel.  
**Settings:** Apply to all percussive slices.  
**Listen For:** Each hit distinct, no wash.  
[SRC: IL-MAN]

#### Lever 6: Major Key Slice Selection
**Action:** If chromatic mode, select slices implying major intervals (3, 5, 7).  
**Result:** Happy, uplifting tonality.  
**Settings:** Play slices at C, E, G, B (Cmaj7).  
**Listen For:** Avoid minor 3rd (Eb) — sounds sad.  
[SRC: Music Theory]

#### Lever 7: Stereo Width on Non-Bass Slices
**Action:** Pan envelope: Hats alternate L→R, snares stay center.  
**Result:** Wide stereo field creates space and energy.  
**Settings:** Pan envelope ±50% on hats, 0% on kicks/snares.  
**Listen For:** Mono check — should not thin out bass.  
[SRC: Mixing]

#### Lever 8: Time Stretch to Faster Tempo (110-140 BPM)
**Action:** Stretch sample to 120-130% original tempo.  
**Result:** Faster movement = more energy.  
**Settings:** Use "Pro" algorithm to avoid artifacts.  
**Listen For:** Tempo should feel driving, not rushed.  
[SRC: IL-MAN]

#### Lever 9: Chromatic Triggering in Upper Octaves
**Action:** Play chromatic slices at C5-C6 (higher register).  
**Result:** Higher pitch = brighter, more energetic.  
**Settings:** Chromatic mode, play melodies in upper 2 octaves.  
**Listen For:** Pitch accuracy (should stay in tune).  
[SRC: IL-MAN]

### What To Listen For (Upbeat)
- **Tonality**: Major or dominant (avoid minor/diminished)
- **Movement**: Fast, rhythmic (16th notes common)
- **Transients**: Clean, punchy (short release envelopes)
- **Frequency Balance**: Bright (emphasis on 3-8kHz for air)

### DON'T Do This (Upbeat Pitfalls)

#### Pitfall 1: Slow, Droning Slices
**Mistake:** Long release envelopes (>500ms) on all slices.  
**Why It Fails:** Creates wash, loses rhythmic definition.  
**Fix:** Short release (<100ms), clean separation between hits.

#### Pitfall 2: Dark, Filtered Tone
**Mistake:** Low-pass filter <1kHz on all slices.  
**Why It Fails:** Darkness = moody, not upbeat.  
**Fix:** High-pass or bypass filter, emphasize 3-8kHz range.

## 3. PSYCHEDELIC

### Vibe Definition (Production Terms)
- **Emotional quality**: Surreal, disorienting, hypnotic, mind-bending
- **Sonic characteristics**: Pitch warping, stereo swirl, glitch artifacts, unpredictable movement
- **Mix context**: Wide stereo, evolving textures, intentional "wrongness"

### Production Levers (Fruity Slicer 2)

#### Lever 1: Pitch Envelope Dives (-12 to -24 Semitones)
**Action:** Pitch envelope: 0st → -18st over 300ms.  
**Result:** Tape-stop-style pitch dive (psychedelic wobble).  
**Settings:** Apply to random slices (3, 7, 11) for unpredictability.  
**Listen For:** Smooth glide vs. stepped artifacts (use Pro algorithm).  
[SRC: IL-KB]

#### Lever 2: Randomized Slice Order
**Action:** Drag-and-drop slices into random order (not sequential).  
**Result:** Glitchy, unexpected rhythmic patterns.  
**Settings:** Original order: 1-2-3-4 → New order: 3-1-4-2.  
**Listen For:** Intentional chaos, not just mess.  
[SRC: SAMPLING]

#### Lever 3: Extreme Pan Envelope (L→R→L Swirl)
**Action:** Pan envelope: L100% → R100% → L100% over 200ms.  
**Result:** Slice swirls around stereo field.  
**Settings:** Apply to ambient slices, not bass (keeps low-end centered).  
**Listen For:** Disorienting movement, headphone "around-head" effect.  
[SRC: Mixing]

#### Lever 4: Reverse + Forward Layering
**Action:** Slice 1 = forward, Slice 2 = reversed version of Slice 1, trigger both.  
**Result:** Palindrome effect (forward-reverse-forward).  
**Settings:** Duplicate slice, reverse one copy, trigger in sequence.  
**Listen For:** Mirror-image symmetry.  
[SRC: SAMPLING]

#### Lever 5: Glitch Time Stretch (>200% or <50%)
**Action:** Extreme time stretch (300% or 30%).  
**Result:** Intentional artifacts (robotic, glitchy).  
**Settings:** Use "Fast" algorithm for MORE artifacts (embrace the weirdness).  
**Listen For:** Digital stutter, pitch warbles.  
[SRC: IL-MAN]

#### Lever 6: Filter Envelope Sweeps (Wah-Wah)
**Action:** Filter envelope: 200Hz → 8kHz → 200Hz over 500ms.  
**Result:** Psychedelic wah-wah sweep.  
**Settings:** Bandpass filter mode for vowel-like resonance.  
**Listen For:** Vocal "talking" effect on non-vocal slices.  
[SRC: IL-MAN]

#### Lever 7: Delay Envelope (Stuttering Timing)
**Action:** Delay envelope: 0ms → 50ms → 0ms (per slice).  
**Result:** Slice timing shifts unpredictably.  
**Settings:** Apply to hi-hat slices for glitchy feel.  
**Listen For:** Off-grid hits create disorientation.  
[SRC: IL-KB]

#### Lever 8: Chromatic Mode with Microtonal Detuning
**Action:** Global pitch +50 cents (between semitones).  
**Result:** Detuned, "out of tune" psychedelic vibe.  
**Settings:** Pitch slider +0.5st, chromatic mode enabled.  
**Listen For:** Unsettling dissonance.  
[SRC: Music Theory]

### What To Listen For (Psychedelic)
- **Pitch**: Warping, unstable, detuned
- **Stereo**: Wide, swirling, disorienting
- **Rhythm**: Unpredictable, glitchy, off-grid
- **Artifacts**: Embrace them (robotic voices, digital stutter)

### DON'T Do This (Psychedelic Pitfalls)

#### Pitfall 1: Predictable Slice Patterns
**Mistake:** Sequential slice triggering (1-2-3-4 loop).  
**Why It Fails:** Psychedelic needs unpredictability.  
**Fix:** Randomize slice order, trigger non-sequentially.

#### Pitfall 2: Centered, Narrow Stereo
**Mistake:** All slices panned center, no width.  
**Why It Fails:** Psychedelic thrives on spatial disorientation.  
**Fix:** Extreme pan envelopes, stereo swirl effects.

## 4. JAZZY

### Vibe Definition (Production Terms)
- **Emotional quality**: Sophisticated, relaxed, swing-based, warm
- **Sonic characteristics**: Swing timing, vinyl warmth, brushed textures, live-feeling imperfections
- **Mix context**: Warm low-mids, soft highs, organic timing (not quantized)

### Production Levers (Fruity Slicer 2)

#### Lever 1: Swing Quantize (Triplet Feel)
**Action:** Trigger slices on swing grid (not straight 16ths).  
**Result:** Laid-back, bouncy jazz groove.  
**Settings:** Piano roll: Swing 66% (triplet feel).  
**Listen For:** "Laid back" timing, not robotic.  
[SRC: HIPHOP]

#### Lever 2: Warm Filter Envelope (Smooth Highs)
**Action:** Filter envelope: Low-pass 3kHz, soft rolloff.  
**Result:** Slices sound warm, not brittle.  
**Settings:** Filter resonance 10-20% for gentle bump.  
**Listen For:** Vinyl-like warmth, no harsh transients.  
[SRC: Mixing]

#### Lever 3: Brush-Like Slice Selection (Soft Transients)
**Action:** Select slices with soft attack (brush hits, soft vocals).  
**Result:** Gentle, non-aggressive vibe.  
**Settings:** Avoid hard kick/snare slices; favor ambient tails.  
**Listen For:** Soft "shh" instead of hard "crack."  
[SRC: SAMPLING]

#### Lever 4: Subtle Pan Envelope (Vinyl Wobble)
**Action:** Pan envelope: ±5% slow sine wave.  
**Result:** Subtle stereo drift (like vinyl playback variations).  
**Settings:** LFO rate 0.1-0.5 Hz on pan envelope.  
**Listen For:** Barely noticeable drift, adds "live" feel.  
[SRC: Mixing]

#### Lever 5: Pitch Down Slightly (-1 to -3 Semitones)
**Action:** Global pitch -2st.  
**Result:** Warmer, jazzier tonality (less bright).  
**Settings:** Chromatic mode, play in lower octaves (C3-C4).  
**Listen For:** Darker, more mature sound.  
[SRC: Music Theory]

#### Lever 6: Long, Natural Release Envelopes
**Action:** Volume envelope: Release 300-600ms.  
**Result:** Slices decay naturally (like real instruments).  
**Settings:** Apply to all slices except hard transients.  
**Listen For:** Slices fade smoothly, not abruptly.  
[SRC: IL-MAN]

#### Lever 7: Layer Vinyl Crackle (External FX)
**Action:** Add Effector → Vinyl preset after Slicer 2.  
**Result:** Authentic jazz vinyl texture.  
**Settings:** Crackle amount 20-40%.  
**Listen For:** Subtle noise, not overpowering.  
[SRC: HIPHOP, Lo-Fi Production]

#### Lever 8: Imperfect Timing (Manual Nudge)
**Action:** Manually shift some slices ±5-10ms off-grid.  
**Result:** Human timing (not quantized).  
**Settings:** Piano roll: Shift individual notes slightly.  
**Listen For:** "Live" feel, not sloppy.  
[SRC: SAMPLING]

### What To Listen For (Jazzy)
- **Timing**: Swing/triplet feel, slight imperfections
- **Tone**: Warm, filtered highs, emphasized low-mids
- **Texture**: Vinyl crackle, soft transients
- **Movement**: Relaxed, laid-back (not rushed)

### DON'T Do This (Jazzy Pitfalls)

#### Pitfall 1: Hard Quantization (Straight 16ths)
**Mistake:** All slices locked to grid, no swing.  
**Why It Fails:** Jazz needs swing timing.  
**Fix:** Apply swing quantize or manual timing shifts.

#### Pitfall 2: Bright, Harsh Highs
**Mistake:** High-pass filter, boosted 8kHz+.  
**Why It Fails:** Jazz is warm, not bright/digital.  
**Fix:** Low-pass <3kHz, soft rolloff.

## 5. VIBEY

### Vibe Definition (Production Terms)
- **Emotional quality**: Atmospheric, textured, layered, immersive
- **Sonic characteristics**: Filtered ambience, subtle movement, reverberant space, non-intrusive
- **Mix context**: Background texture, supporting role (not lead), spacious

### Production Levers (Fruity Slicer 2)

#### Lever 1: Filtered Atmospheric Slices
**Action:** Select sustained/ambient slices (pads, vocal tails).  
**Result:** Texture layer, not rhythmic element.  
**Settings:** Auto-slice at 20-30% sensitivity (captures only sustained notes).  
**Listen For:** Long, evolving tones.  
[SRC: SAMPLING]

#### Lever 2: Long Release + Reverb Layering
**Action:** Volume envelope: Release 800-1500ms + external reverb.  
**Result:** Slices blend into reverberant wash.  
**Settings:** LuxeVerb or Fruity Reeverb 2, Wet 40-60%.  
**Listen For:** Slices should merge, not stay distinct.  
[SRC: Mixing]

#### Lever 3: Subtle Delay Envelope (Rhythmic Texture)
**Action:** Delay envelope: 0ms → 20ms → 0ms per slice.  
**Result:** Micro-timing shifts create "floating" feel.  
**Settings:** Apply to pad/vocal slices.  
**Listen For:** Slight drift, not obvious delay.  
[SRC: IL-KB]

#### Lever 4: Low-Pass Filter (Subdued Presence)
**Action:** Filter envelope: Cutoff 1.5-2.5kHz.  
**Result:** Slices sit in background, don't dominate.  
**Settings:** Static filter (no sweep), gentle rolloff.  
**Listen For:** Muffled, distant texture.  
[SRC: Mixing]

#### Lever 5: Wide Stereo Field (Non-Bass Slices)
**Action:** Pan envelope: Slices drift ±50-80%.  
**Result:** Enveloping stereo image.  
**Settings:** Slow pan movement (LFO 0.2 Hz).  
**Listen For:** Headphone "around-head" width.  
[SRC: Mixing]

#### Lever 6: Layered Vocal Chops (Commit + Re-Slice)
**Action:** Slice vocals → commit to audio → load into new Slicer → slice again.  
**Result:** Double-layered texture (chops of chops).  
**Settings:** 1st pass: syllables. 2nd pass: consonants.  
**Listen For:** Dense vocal texture bed.  
[SRC: SAMPLING]

#### Lever 7: Slow Time Stretch (Ambient Extension)
**Action:** Stretch sample to 50-70% original tempo.  
**Result:** Slowed, ambient vibe (even in uptempo track).  
**Settings:** Pro algorithm for transparency.  
**Listen For:** Syrupy, suspended time feel.  
[SRC: IL-MAN]

#### Lever 8: Pitch Down for Warmth (-2 to -4 Semitones)
**Action:** Global pitch -3st.  
**Result:** Warmer, less intrusive tonality.  
**Settings:** Chromatic mode, play in mid-range (C4).  
**Listen For:** Darker without being muddy.  
[SRC: Music Theory]

### What To Listen For (Vibey)
- **Presence**: Background, supporting (not foreground)
- **Space**: Reverberant, layered (long tails)
- **Movement**: Slow, subtle (not dramatic)
- **Tone**: Warm, filtered (not bright)

### DON'T Do This (Vibey Pitfalls)

#### Pitfall 1: Aggressive Transients
**Mistake:** Short release (<50ms), punchy volume envelope.  
**Why It Fails:** Vibey is smooth, not punchy.  
**Fix:** Long release (>500ms), soft attack.

#### Pitfall 2: Dry, Unprocessed Slices
**Mistake:** No reverb, no delay, no filtering.  
**Why It Fails:** Vibey needs spatial depth.  
**Fix:** Layer reverb + delay, long release envelopes.
