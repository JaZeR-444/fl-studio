# Genre Style Board: SoundFont Player

## Hip-Hop
- **Technique:** "Boombap Grand." Use a high-quality Grand Piano SF2 (like SGM). Set resampling to **Crunchy**. High-pass at 200Hz. This creates the "sampled from a record" piano sound without needing a real record.
- **Reference Idea:** Think of the warm piano melodies in J Dilla, Pete Rock, or early Kanye West tracks.

## Rap
- **Technique:** "Cinematic Trap Strings." Layer 3 instances of "Cello" or "Violin Section" SoundFonts. Detune them slightly and pan them wide. This creates the "epic" backdrop for aggressive rap lyrics.
- **Reference Idea:** The orchestral, minor-key melodies in 2010s trap (Lex Luger, Metro Boomin).

## R&B / Pop-Hip-Hop
- **Technique:** "Lush R&B Rhodes." Use a Rhodes SF2 with internal Chorus enabled. Set a long Release. Layer it with a soft Sine wave in 3x Osc.
- **Reference Idea:** The smooth, "vibey" instrumentation in modern R&B like Bryson Tiller or H.E.R.
