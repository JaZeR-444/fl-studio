# Goal: Vibe - Psychedelic

## Goal Statement
Transform an acoustic SoundFont into a trippy, warping soundscape.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Instrument:** Load a "Sitar" or "Ethnic Flute" SoundFont.
2. **Modulation:** Enable LFO. Set **Freq** to 10Hz (Fast) and **Amp** to 20% (Deep).
3. **Pitch:** Automate the **Master Pitch** to "glide" between notes unnaturally.
4. **External FX:** Add `Fruity Flanger` and `Fruity Love Philter` with a "Random" LFO preset.
5. **Resampling:** Use **Crunchy** to add strange digital artifacts during pitch sweeps.

## Automation Idea
- **LFO Freq:** Automate from 1Hz (Slow) to 20Hz (Fast) over 4 bars to create a "vibrating" tension.
