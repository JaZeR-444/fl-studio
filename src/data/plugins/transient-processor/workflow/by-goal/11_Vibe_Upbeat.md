# Goal: Vibe - Upbeat

## Goal Statement
Create an aggressive, "exploding" transient for high-energy Trap and Rap tracks.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Placement:** Use on your main Drum Bus (Kick and Snare).
2. **Attack:** Boost **Attack** to +9dB (Aggressive).
3. **Drive:** Turn **Drive** to 25%. This adds a "crunch" to the snap.
4. **Mix:** Use **Parallel Mix** at 30%.
5. **Result:** This adds a "layer" of pure energy to the drums that makes the whole track feel more "exciting."

## What to Listen For
- Maximum impact.
- The drums should sound "stiff" and "heavy."
- A feeling of the track "jumping" on every hit.
