# Slicex - 00 START HERE

## Purpose
Slicex slices loops into regions and provides per-slice articulation, routing, and layered playback with Deck A and B. It is designed for detailed loop editing and performance.

## 10-Minute Orientation
1. Load a loop in Deck A and run Auto Slice.
2. Audition slices from the keyboard.
3. Assign a slice to an articulator and shape its envelope.
4. Set OUT for per-slice mixer routing.
5. Load a second loop in Deck B to layer with Deck A.

## Listening Tests
- Auto-slice modes on the same loop.
- Articulator filter envelope vs no envelope.
- CUT/CUT BY for hats and stabs.

## Pitfalls
- OUT is an offset from the Slicex mixer track, not an absolute track number.
- If slices stop early, check articulator envelope and note length.

## 🎯 What is Slicex?

Slicex is the ultimate beat-slicing tool in FL Studio. It takes a drum loop (or any sample), uses transient detection to slice it into individual hits, and maps them to MIDI notes. Unlike Fruity Slicer, Slicex features **Deck A** and **Deck B** for layering, a full **Wave Editor** (Edison built-in), and independent articulation (Filter, Envelope) for *every single slice*.

**Key Capabilities:**
- **Auto-Slicing:** Intelligent transient detection.
- **Wave Editor:** Full editing capabilities (Fade, Normalize, EQ) per slice.
- **Dual Decks:** Load two loops and layer/crossfade them.
- **Articulations:** 8 Articulation slots (Filter/Envelopes) assignable to slices.
- **Piano Roll Dump:** Instantly dumps the groove to MIDI.
- **Mixer Routing:** Route individual slices to different mixer tracks effortlessly.

# What It Is And When To Use: Slicex

## Purpose & Identity
Slicex is FL Studio's powerhouse **beat-slicing sampler**. Its primary identity is a professional "Chop & Re-arrange" tool. It takes any loop or sample, automatically detects the hits (transients), and maps them to your MIDI keyboard. Unlike simpler samplers, Slicex features **dual decks (A/B)** and independent synthesis (filters/envelopes) for every single slice. [SRC: IL-MAN]

## 60-Second Mental Model
Think of it as a "Precision Surgical Sampler." You load a sample (like a piece of wood), and Slicex cuts it into building blocks (slices). You can then rebuild those blocks into a completely new structure (a new drum beat or melody). Each block can be painted a different color (filtered) or shaped differently (enveloped) without affecting the others.

## Hip-Hop / R&B Context
- **The "Boom Bap" Flip:** Sampling a soulful 70s record, auto-slicing by beat, and replaying the chops in a new order (Jazzy/Moody).
- **Vocal Melodies:** Chopping a single vocal phrase and playing it as a lead instrument with "Legato" slides (Melodic/Vibey).
- **Layered Breaks:** Loading a gritty acoustic break in Deck A and a clean electronic kick in Deck B to create a "Hybrid" drum kit (Upbeat).

## When To Use
- When you want to **re-arrange a drum loop** while keeping its original groove.
- When you are **sampling records** and need to find the perfect "chops."
- When you want **per-slice control** (e.g., reversing only the snare in a loop).

## When NOT To Use
- **One-Shot Drums:** Use the standard **Sampler** or **FPC** if you just want to play a single Kick or Snare sample.
- **Quick Simple Slicing:** Use **Fruity Slicer** if you don't need deep synthesis or layering. [SRC: REPUTABLE]

# 01: UI Map and Signal Flow

## UI Sections Tour
Slicex is essentially two Edison-style editors combined with a synthesis engine. [SRC: IL-MAN-04]

### 1. The Decks (A & B)
- **Deck Toggle:** Switch between the two sample slots.
- **Waveform Display:** View and edit markers.
- **Auto-Slice:** The "Magic" wand to detect transients.

### 2. The Region Editor (Slice Properties)
- **Cut/Cut By:** Used for "choking" sounds (e.g., Open Hat cutting off Closed Hat).
- **Out Offset:** Route the selected slice to a specific Mixer offset (e.g., Output +1).
- **Articulator Assignment:** Choose which of the 8 articulation slots controls this slice.

### 3. Articulators (The Synthesis)
- **MOD / ENV / LFO:** 8 independent slots featuring Filter, Volume, Pitch, and Pan controls.
- **Graph Editor:** Draw custom shapes for envelopes and LFOs.

### 4. The Master Panel
- **Master Vol / Pitch:** Global controls.
- **Freeze:** Locks markers to prevent accidental movement.

## Signal Flow
`Sample (Deck A or B) -> Region Selection -> Articulator Processing (Filter/Env/LFO) -> Mixer Offset (Routing) -> Master Global -> Output`

## Things Beginners Misunderstand
- **Piano Roll Dump:** Slicex doesn't play the loop automatically. You must "Dump to Piano Roll" to see the MIDI trigger notes.
- **The "Cut" Numbers:** "Cut 1" means "I belong to group 1." "Cut By 1" means "I am silenced by anyone in group 1."
- **Two Decks = Two Samples:** You can layer sounds by having Deck A and Deck B mapped to the same MIDI note.

# 02: Core Techniques and Best Practices

## 1. Manual "Micro-Chops"
Auto-slice often misses the very beginning of a transient. Always zoom in and move your markers a few milliseconds **before** the waveform starts to avoid "clicking." [SRC: REPUTABLE-05]

## 2. Layering via Deck B
1. Load an acoustic drum break into **Deck A**.
2. Load a heavy 808/Trap kit into **Deck B**.
3. Align the markers.
4. Now, every time you hit a "Kick" note, you get the organic texture of Deck A and the power of Deck B.

## 3. The "Infinite" Vocal Lead
1. Slice a vocal "Ah" or "Oh."
2. Set the Region mode to **Loop**.
3. Use **Articulator 1** to apply a slow Volume Envelope (Release).
- *Result:* The vocal slice will loop indefinitely as long as you hold the key, turning it into a playable instrument.

## 4. Choke Groups (The MPC Way)
To prevent messy overlapping drums:
1. Select all Hi-Hat slices.
2. Set their **Cut** to 1 and **Cut By** to 1.
3. Now, the Closed Hat will instantly stop the Open Hat sound, creating a tight groove.

## 5. Reverse Chops for "Vibe"
Select a melodic slice (like a piano chord). Go to the editor menu and select **Tools -> Reverse**. Route this slice to a separate mixer track with heavy reverb. This is the "secret sauce" for **Psychedelic** and **Moody** textures.

## 6. Velocity-Sensitive Filtering
In the Articulator section:
1. Select **Filter Cutoff**.
2. Select the **MOD** tab.
3. Map **Velocity** to Cutoff with an upward slope.
- *Result:* Playing the pads harder makes the sample brighter, adding "human" expression to your beats.

## Common Pitfalls + Fixes
- **Pitfall:** "Clicking" at the end of a slice.
  - **Fix:** Add a tiny **Release** (5-10ms) in the Articulator Volume Envelope.
- **Pitfall:** Sample is out of key.
  - **Fix:** Use the **Master Pitch** knob or the "Tune" tool in the Wave Editor.
- **Pitfall:** MIDI notes are all the same.
  - **Fix:** Ensure you haven't accidental enabled "Single Note" mode in the settings.

# Vibe Translation Guide: Slicex

Slicex translates vibes through **Rhythmic Re-arrangement** and **Slice Articulation**.

## 1. Jazzy (Soulful, Organic, Human)
- **Concept:** "The MPC Flip."
- **Levers:**
  - **Slicing:** Slice by "Beat" or "Bar" for melodic chunks.
  - **Articulation:** Low-pass filter (Articulator 1) at 1kHz.
  - **Groove:** Keep the "original" timing of the chops to preserve the drummer's feel.
- **Listen For:** Smooth, musical transitions between sample chunks that feel like a live performance.

## 2. Upbeat (Kinetic, Aggressive, Fast)
- **Concept:** "The Jungle Chop."
- **Levers:**
  - **Slicing:** Dull Auto-slice (to get more hits).
  - **Speed:** High-speed patterns in the Piano Roll.
  - **Envelope:** Very short decay on the volume envelope.
- **Listen For:** Sharp, rapid-fire percussion that drives the energy of the track.

## 3. Melodic (Polished, Glossy, Catchy)
- **Concept:** "The Vocal Synth."
- **Levers:**
  - **Target:** Vocal phrase.
  - **Settings:** Enable **Mono/Legato** in the master panel.
  - **Smoothing:** Increase the Attack time in the volume envelope slightly.
- **Listen For:** A fluid, singing character where the chops glide into each other.

## 4. Psychedelic (Warped, Backwards, Surreal)
- **Concept:** "The Reverse Flip."
- **Levers:**
  - **Action:** Select specific slices and hit **Tools -> Reverse**.
  - **Pitch:** Use the Pitch Envelope to create "dive bomb" effects on individual slices.
- **Listen For:** Backwards-breathing textures that move in and out of the beat.

## 5. Moody (Dark, Damp, Isolated)
- **Concept:** "The Filtered Loop."
- **Levers:**
  - **Filter:** 24dB Low Pass @ 500Hz.
  - **Mix:** Lower the dry signal to 0.
- **Listen For:** A submerged, muffled loop that creates a dark "underwater" foundation for the track. [SRC: REPUTABLE]
- **Don't Do This:** Don't use bright high-end; keep the slices dark and heavy.

# Slicex vs. Fruity Slicer: When to Level Up

## Fruity Slicer (The Speed Demon)
- **Best For:** Quick chops, 1-knob time stretching, and basic drum rearranging.
- **Pros:** Extremely fast, very low CPU, simple interface.
- **Cons:** No per-slice filtering, no layering, limited wave editing.
- **The "Vibe":** Clean, functional, and "no-nonsense."

## Slicex (The Surgeon)
- **Best For:** Professional sampling, layering loops, sound design, and complex articulations.
- **Pros:** Edison-level editing, 8 Articulators, Deck A/B layering, independent mixer routing per slice.
- **Cons:** Higher learning curve, more complex UI.
- **The "Vibe":** Creative, experimental, and "limitless."

## When to Level Up to Slicex:
1. **Layering:** You want to add an 808 layer to a breakbeat.
2. **Filtering:** You want the Snare to be bright but the Hi-Hats to be dark.
3. **Routing:** You want to EQ your Kick and Snare separately in the Mixer.
4. **Vocal Chops:** You need to loop or sustain vocal regions for melodies. [SRC: IL-MAN-04]

## Summary Table
| Feature | Fruity Slicer | Slicex |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Slicing Engine** | Basic Transient | Advanced Transient/Manual |
| **Editors** | 1 | 2 (Deck A/B) |
| **Wave Editing** | None | Full (Edison Engine) |
| **Articulators** | Global only | 8 Independent Slots |
| **Mixer Outputs** | 1 | Unlimited (via Offset) |

# Slicex Keyboard Shortcuts

## Navigation & Selection
- **Ctrl + Left Click:** Select a slice/region.
- **Double Click (on marker):** Rename slice.
- **Mouse Wheel:** Zoom in/out of the waveform.
- **Page Up / Page Down:** Select Next/Previous slice.

## Editing (In Wave Editor)
- **Ctrl + E:** Auto-slice (using default settings).
- **Alt + S:** Open "Dull Auto-slice" (Good for noisy loops).
- **Ctrl + Z:** Undo.
- **Ctrl + R:** Reverse the selected region.
- **Ctrl + N:** Normalize the selected region.
- **Alt + L:** Open Region/Loop properties.

## Piano Roll / Workflow
- **Shift + D:** Dump to Piano Roll (Quick).
- **Drag & Drop (Dump Icon):** Manual dump to specific track.
- **Ctrl + F:** Find marker by name.

## Articulator Shortcuts
- **Right Click (on graph):** Reset graph or copy/paste state.
- **Alt + Left Click (on node):** Reset node to default.
- **Shift + Click (on node):** Snap node to grid. [SRC: IL-MAN-04]

# Genre Rules: Hip-Hop & R&B (Slicex)

## 1. Zero-Crossing Rule
- **Rule:** No clicks in the chops.
- **Application:** Use the **Snap to zero crossing** tool (the "S" magnet icon) when moving markers. This ensures that slices start and end at silence, preventing digital pops. [SRC: REPUTABLE]

## 2. Drum Routing discipline
- **Rule:** Mix the elements separately.
- **Application:** Use the **OUT** offset in the Region Properties. Send your Kicks to Mixer Offset 1, Snares to 2, and Hats to 3. This gives you "Multi-out" control over a single loop.

## 3. Choke Group Logic (The Open Hat Rule)
- **Rule:** The Closed Hat must kill the Open Hat.
- **Application:** Set both Hi-Hat slices to the same **CUT** number (e.g., 1). This mimics how a real hi-hat works, creating a tighter, more professional "Vibey" rhythm.

## 4. Sample Key Alignment
- **Rule:** Chops must match the song's key.
- **Application:** Use the master **Pitch** knob in Slicex to tune the entire loop to your project. Use per-region **Fine Tune** for individual melodic chops that are slightly out of pitch.

## 5. Legato for Vocal Chops
- **Rule:** Vocal melodies should glide.
- **Application:** In the Master Panel, set the play mode to **MONO** and increase the **Portamento** time. This allows your vocal chops to "slide" between notes, which is a staple of modern R&B. [SRC: IL-MAN]

# Articulator Routing Logic: The Slicex Brain

Slicex features 8 independent Articulation slots. Each slice can be assigned to one slot, allowing for complex per-hit processing. [SRC: IL-MAN-04]

## The Logic Flow:
`MIDI Note -> Slice Marker -> Assigned Articulator (1-8) -> Envelope/LFO -> Mixer Offset`

## High-Value Routing Strategies:

### 1. The "Dynamic Snare" (Velocity to Filter)
- **Goal:** Make the snare brighter the harder you hit it.
- **Setup:** Assign all Snare slices to **Articulator 2**. In Articulator 2, go to the **MOD** tab and select **Filter Cutoff**. Draw a diagonal line from bottom-left to top-right.
- **Vibe:** This adds "Jazz" and "Human" feel to static drum breaks.

### 2. The "Sub-Bass Protector" (Filter Cutoff)
- **Goal:** Keep the Kick punchy but filter the mid-range "mud" from the rest of the loop.
- **Setup:** Assign the Kick to **Articulator 1** (No Filter). Assign all other slices to **Articulator 3** (LP Filter at 500Hz).
- **Vibe:** Essential for **Trap** and **Boom Bap** to ensure the 808 has its own space.

### 3. The "Stereo Percussion" (LFO to Pan)
- **Goal:** Make hi-hats move across the stereo field.
- **Setup:** Assign Hi-Hats to **Articulator 4**. Enable the **LFO** and route it to **Pan**.
- **Vibe:** Adds "Psychedelic" and "Vibey" interest to simple loops.

## Articulator Limits:
- **Max Slots:** 8.
- **Tip:** If you need more than 8 different sounds, use **Deck B** or a second instance of Slicex.
