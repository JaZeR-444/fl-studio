## Step-by-Step Setup

### Step 1: Extreme Slicing
**Settings:**
- **Threshold:** 15-25% (extreme, 30+ tiny slices)
- **Why:** More slices = more glitch potential

**Expected Result:** Waveform covered in slice markers (almost overwhelming)

---

### Step 2: Psychedelic Settings
**Crossfade:** 0 ms (intentional clicks/artifacts)  
**Time Stretch:** Automate wildly (0.5x → 2x)  
**Pitch:** Automate randomly (-12 to +12 semitones)

**Why These Settings:**
- Zero Crossfade = glitchy clicks (desired)
- Extreme Time-Stretch = warped, unstable
- Random Pitch = disorienting

---

### Step 3: Chaotic MIDI Pattern
**Pattern Style:** Erratic, unpredictable, stuttering

**Example Pattern (2 bars):**
```
Bar 1:
- Beats 1-1.5: Slice 3 (1/32nd notes × 16) [stutter]
- Beat 2: Slice 7 (single hit)
- Beats 2.5-3: Slice 1 (1/64th notes × 8) [rapid glitch]
- Beat 4: Rest

Bar 2:
- Beat 1: Slice 12 (whole note, sustain)
- Beats 2-2.25: Slice 5 (1/32nd notes × 8)
- Beat 3: Slice 2 (single hit)
- Beats 3.5-4: Slice 9 (1/16th notes × 8)
```

**Key:** Unpredictable rhythm, mix of stutter and space

---

### Step 4: Automation (Critical for Psychedelic)
**Automate ALL parameters:**

**Time Stretch Automation (Bar-by-Bar):**
```
Bar 1: 1.0x
Bar 2: 0.7x (slow, warped)
Bar 3: 1.5x (fast, manic)
Bar 4: 0.5x (extremely slow, degraded)
```

**Pitch Automation (Random):**
- Use FL Studio's randomizer
- Range: -12 to +12 semitones
- Steps: Every 1/4 note

**Crossfade Automation (Texture Shift):**
- Verse: 0 ms (glitchy)
- Chorus: 20 ms (smooth moment)
- Bridge: 0 ms (back to chaos)

---

### Step 5: FX Chain (Psychedelic Depth)
**Reverb (Crushed):**
- Decay: 0.5-1 second (short, unnatural)
- Pre-Delay: 0 ms
- Mix: 80-100% (very wet, drowning in reverb)
- Crush: Add bit-crusher before reverb

**Delay (Ping-Pong):**
- Time: 1/8 note dotted
- Feedback: 70-80% (cascading echoes)
- Stereo: 100% width (wild stereo)

**Distortion:**
- Type: Hard clipping or bit-crushing
- Drive: 50-70%
- Mix: 30-50% (parallel distortion)

## Pitfalls & Fixes

### Pitfall 1: "Too chaotic, unmusical"
**Cause:** Complete randomness with no structure  
**Fix:** Add rhythmic anchor (kick on 1 and 3, consistent)

### Pitfall 2: "Sounds like mistakes, not intentional"
**Cause:** Not committing to psychedelic aesthetic  
**Fix:** Push further (more extreme = more intentional)

### Pitfall 3: "Gets boring after 8 bars"
**Cause:** Same chaos pattern repeated  
**Fix:** Automate chaos amount (build/release cycles)

---

## Automation Ideas

### Automation 1: Chaos Build
- Intro: Time Stretch 1.0x, Pitch 0 (normal)
- Verse: Time Stretch 0.9x, Pitch -2 (slight weird)
- Chorus: Time Stretch 0.5x to 2x (automation), Pitch ±12 (extreme)

### Automation 2: Glitch Density
- Verse: Sparse pattern (8 notes per bar)
- Chorus: Dense stutter (64 notes per bar, 1/64th)

## Advanced Technique: Resample Cascade
**Goal:** Layer multiple psychedelic generations

**Workflow:**
1. Create psychedelic chop pattern (Generation 1)
2. Render to audio (8 bars)
3. Load rendered audio into NEW Slicer instance
4. Apply even more extreme settings (Generation 2)
5. Render again
6. Load into THIRD Slicer (Generation 3)
7. Layer all 3 generations (panned L/C/R)

**Result:** Impossibly complex psychedelic texture
