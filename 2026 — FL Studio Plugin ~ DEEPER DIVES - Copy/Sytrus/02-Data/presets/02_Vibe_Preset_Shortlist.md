# Vibe Preset Shortlist

## 1. Moody (Dark & Intimate)
- **Mango Sub:** Operator 1 (Dull Harmonics) -> Filter 1 (Mango mode, High Drive).
- **Lonely Tine:** FM piano with high Damping, slow Pitch LFO, Reverb Mix 30%.
- **Dusty Additive:** 3-partial additive pad, LP Vanilla filter at 300Hz.
- **Noir Lead:** Monophonic lead, low Cutoff, high Glide, 1 voice.
- **Hidden Room:** Ethereal pad with Reverb Size 100% and Reverb Mix 50%.

## 2. Upbeat (High-Definition & Snappy)
- **Glass Bell:** OP 2 (Ratio 4.000) -> OP 1 (Ratio 1.000) at 50%. Fast Decay.
- **Trap Snap:** Single operator Saw, Filter 1 (LP 24dB) with Snap envelope.
- **Laser Synth:** 9 voices Unison, Detune 2%, high FM feedback.
- **Digital Brass:** Additive engine building a bright saw, Filter Env +70%.
- **Cold Chime:** Ratio 8.000 modulator, 0ms Attack, high high-pass.

## 3. Psychedelic (Trippy & Moving)
- **Feedback Glitch:** Serial FM (OP 3->2->1) with all operators feeding back.
- **Spiral Morph:** X-Y Controller mapping to FM amounts and Filter Res.
- **RM Horror:** Ring Modulation (Blue knob) between ratio 1.500 oscillators.
- **Bubbling Pad:** LFO -> Filter Cutoff (Square wave) with Unison Detune 30%.
- **Space Warp:** Global Pitch LFO set to 10Hz (Fast) and 10% Depth.

## 4. Jazzy (Warm & Sophisticated)
- **Classic DX Rhodes:** FM Ratio 1.000/1.000, Velocity -> OP 2 Volume.
- **Warm Soul Bass:** OP 1 Sine -> Filter 1 (LP Vanilla) with 20ms Attack.
- **Soft Flute:** Single operator Sine with 10% Noise, slow Pitch Vibrato.
- **Analog Organ:** 4-operator additive engine mimicking drawbars.
- **Smooth Vibe:** Sub-only operator layered with a filtered Triangle.

## 5. Vibey (Lush & Wide)
- **Cloud 9:** 9-voice Unison, Max Pan, 3 Delay lines active.
- **Satin Additive:** 256-harmonic pad, Unison Phase 100%, slow Volume Swell.
- **R&B E-Piano:** Clean FM keys, built-in Chorus (Fat preset), Delay 1/4 note.
- **Silk Lead:** Mono Legato, Unison 2 voices (Tight), Reverb Mix 10%.
- **Late Night Pad:** Dark LP filter, high Unison Detune, Chorus Mix 40%.
