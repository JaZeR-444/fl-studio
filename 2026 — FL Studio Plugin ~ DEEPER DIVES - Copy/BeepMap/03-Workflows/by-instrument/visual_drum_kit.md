# Visual Drum Synthesis

Creating a drum kit visually in BeepMap:

## The Kick (Visual)
- **Shape:** A thick Red/Green circle at the bottom of the image.
- **Sound:** A low-frequency burst with a soft attack (due to the circular shape).

## The Hi-Hat (Visual)
- **Shape:** Tiny scattered dots at the top of the image.
- **Sound:** Short, high-frequency "ticks".

## The Riser (Visual)
- **Shape:** A diagonal line from bottom to top.
- **Sound:** A classic white-noise or sine sweep.
