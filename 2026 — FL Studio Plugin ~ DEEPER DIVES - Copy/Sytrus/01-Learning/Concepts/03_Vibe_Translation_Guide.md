# Vibe Translation Guide: Sytrus

Sytrus translates vibes through **FM Complexity** and **Unison Width**.

## 1. Moody (Dark, Heavy, Distant)
- **Concept:** "The Low-End Growl."
- **Levers:**
  - **Operator 1:** Sine wave.
  - **Matrix:** Self-feedback (Operator 1 modulating itself).
  - **Filter:** 24dB Low Pass @ 400Hz.
- **Listen For:** A thick, slightly distorted low-end that feels "heavy" and nocturnal.
- **Don't Do This:** Don't add high harmonics; keep the focus on the sub-weight.

## 2. Upbeat (Bright, Plucky, Pop)
- **Concept:** "The Digital Bell."
- **Levers:**
  - **Matrix:** FM Operator 2 -> Operator 1 (Ratio 4.0 or 8.0).
  - **Envelope:** Very fast decay on the volume and FM amount.
- **Listen For:** A sharp, metallic "Chime" that cuts through a busy drum pattern.

## 3. Jazzy (Warm, Soulful, Human)
- **Concept:** "The Neo-Soul Keys."
- **Levers:**
  - **Harmonics Editor:** Add only the first 3-4 harmonics.
  - **Unison:** 3-5 voices with low "Sub" detuning.
  - **FX:** Built-in Chorus (Warm settings).
- **Listen For:** A soft, "wooden" resonance that mimics a vintage Rhodes piano.

## 4. Melodic (Lush, Ethereal, Polished)
- **Concept:** "The Wide Cloud."
- **Levers:**
  - **Unison:** 9 voices.
  - **Detune:** 20%.
  - **Envelope:** Slow Attack (2 seconds).
- **Listen For:** A shimmering, expansive pad that fills the stereo field effortlessly.

## 5. Psychedelic (Warped, Liquid, Morphing)
- **Concept:** "The Ring-Mod Warp."
- **Levers:**
  - **Matrix:** Use **RM** (Ring Modulation) instead of FM.
  - **LFO:** Map a slow LFO to the **Modulation X** or **Y** knobs.
- **Listen For:** Strange, non-musical metallic overtones that shift and "gurgle" as the LFO moves. [SRC: REPUTABLE]
