# Goal: Vibe - Upbeat

## Goal Statement
Create a bright, snappy lead that drives an energetic, high-tempo track.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Oscillator:** Osc 1 & 2 (Saw, Warm). Detune Osc 2 by 5 cents.
2. **Filter:** LP Mix 80%, BP Mix 20% (for mid-punch). Cutoff 1kHz.
3. **Envelope:** Filter Env Amount +70%. Decay 400ms.
4. **Resonance:** 30%.
5. **Mode:** Mono Legato with Glide at 100ms.

## What to Listen For
- A "stabbing" attack that cuts through the mix.
- A bright, harmonically rich tone that feels "energetic."
