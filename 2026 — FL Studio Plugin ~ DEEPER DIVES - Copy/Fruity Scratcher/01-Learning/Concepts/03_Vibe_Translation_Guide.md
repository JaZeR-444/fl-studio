# Vibe Translation Guide — Fruity Scratcher

## Production Levers: How to Create Vibes with a Turntable Simulator

This guide translates abstract "vibes" into **concrete production moves** you can execute with Fruity Scratcher. Each vibe includes 6-10 actionable levers, listening checkpoints, and common pitfalls.

---

## 1. MOODY

### Production Definition
Introspective, melancholic, dark, emotional depth, slow-building tension, nostalgic sadness.

### Levers to Pull

#### **1. Slow Tape Stops (Extended Deceleration)**
- **Move:** Automate SPD from 1.0x → 0.0x over 4-8 beats
- **Settings:** ACC 15-25% (very gradual slowdown)
- **What to Listen For:** Emotional weight in the pitch drop; avoid abruptness
- **Sample Choice:** Use emotional vocal phrases, minor key melodies
- [SRC: Production Tutorials]

#### **2. Reverse Vocals with Low ACC**
- **Move:** Enable Reverse, set ACC 20-30%
- **Settings:** Use on vocal phrases; slow attack/release
- **What to Listen For:** Ethereal, haunting backward vocals
- **Sample Choice:** Breathy vocals, pad-like sustains
- [SRC: Experimental Production Techniques]

#### **3. Pitch-Dive Outros**
- **Move:** Automate SPD to negative values (-0.5x) at track end
- **Settings:** ACC 25%, slow curve
- **What to Listen For:** Descending into darkness; cinematic close
- **Sample Choice:** Sustained synth pads, ambient textures
- [SRC: Cinematic Production Guides]

#### **4. Subtle Speed Wobble (LFO-Like Effect)**
- **Move:** Automate SPD with slow sine wave (0.9x ↔ 1.1x)
- **Settings:** ACC 40%, slow modulation rate (1 cycle per 4 bars)
- **What to Listen For:** Warped, unstable feeling; underwater vibe
- **Sample Choice:** Piano, Rhodes, atmospheric samples
- [SRC: Lo-Fi Production Techniques]

#### **5. Long ACC for Dragging Feel**
- **Move:** Set ACC 10-20% on scratches
- **Settings:** Use with downtempo beats (70-90 BPM)
- **What to Listen For:** Lazy, dragging scratch; not snappy
- **Sample Choice:** Dark vocal chops, brooding bass notes
- [SRC: Trip-Hop Production]

#### **6. Melancholic Sample Selection**
- **Move:** Load minor-key vocal phrases, sighs, string sections
- **Settings:** Standard SPD 1.0x, occasional tape stops
- **What to Listen For:** Emotional resonance in sample content itself
- **Sample Choice:** "Goodbye," "Lost," "Alone"—emotionally charged words
- [SRC: Sample Selection Guides]

### **What NOT to Do:**
❌ Fast, aggressive scratches (too energetic for moody)  
❌ Upward spin-ups (creates excitement, not introspection)  

### **Listening Checkpoint:**
Does it sound introspective and melancholic? Could it score a rainy scene in a film?

## 2. UPBEAT

### Production Definition
Energetic, bouncy, positive, rhythmic drive, danceable, high-energy momentum.

### Levers to Pull

#### **1. Rhythmic Scratch Patterns (Chirp/Transform)**
- **Move:** Use Baby/Chirp scratches on 2 and 4 (snare timing)
- **Settings:** ACC 60-80%, SEN 50-70%
- **What to Listen For:** Tight, punchy rhythmic accents
- **Sample Choice:** "Yeah!", "Uh!", "Hey!"—short, energetic vocal stabs
- [SRC: Hip-Hop Production Guides]

#### **2. Spin-Up Risers Before Drops**
- **Move:** Automate SPD 0.0x → 1.5x over 2-4 beats before drop
- **Settings:** ACC 50-70% (energetic ramp)
- **What to Listen For:** Tension building toward release; crowd hype
- **Sample Choice:** Hi-hats, cymbal crescendos, crowd noise
- [SRC: EDM Production Techniques]

#### **3. Bouncy Speed Automation**
- **Move:** Automate SPD in rhythmic patterns (1.0x → 1.2x → 1.0x per beat)
- **Settings:** ACC 60%, synced to tempo grid
- **What to Listen For:** Bouncy, playful energy; groove enhancement
- **Sample Choice:** Percussion loops, vocal chops, synth stabs
- [SRC: Bounce/Funk Production]

#### **4. High-Energy Scratches on Vocal Hooks**
- **Move:** Scratch hook phrase ("Get up!", "Let's go!") every 8 bars
- **Settings:** ACC 70%, fast drag speed
- **What to Listen For:** Call-and-response energy; crowd engagement
- **Sample Choice:** Hype vocals, energetic shouts
- [SRC: Party Music Production]

#### **5. Stutter Scratches on Hi-Hats**
- **Move:** Use reverse stutter technique on open hi-hat samples
- **Settings:** ACC 80%, tight timing
- **What to Listen For:** Glitchy, exciting hi-hat variation
- **Sample Choice:** Crisp hi-hats, shakers
- [SRC: Electronic Music Production]

#### **6. Positive Sample Selection**
- **Move:** Load uplifting vocal phrases, major-key melodies
- **Settings:** Standard SPD 1.0x, rhythmic scratching
- **What to Listen For:** Positive emotional tone
- **Sample Choice:** "Feel good!", "Dance!", "Party!"
- [SRC: Sample Selection Guides]

### **What NOT to Do:**
❌ Slow tape stops (kills energy)  
❌ Low ACC values (feels sluggish, not bouncy)  

### **Listening Checkpoint:**
Does it make you want to move? Would it work in a club or festival?

## 3. PSYCHEDELIC

### Production Definition
Trippy, warped, experimental, disorienting, lysergic textures, mind-bending, unconventional.

### Levers to Pull

#### **1. Extreme Speed Modulation**
- **Move:** Automate SPD wildly (-2.0x ↔ +2.0x) in random patterns
- **Settings:** ACC 90% (instant changes)
- **What to Listen For:** Disorienting pitch shifts; reality warping
- **Sample Choice:** Synth drones, spoken word, abstract sounds
- [SRC: Experimental Production]

#### **2. Glitch Reverse Stutters**
- **Move:** Rapid Reverse toggling (automate on/off at 1/16 note intervals)
- **Settings:** ACC 95%, short samples
- **What to Listen For:** Broken, stuttering texture; digital chaos
- **Sample Choice:** Vocal syllables, snare hits, synth plucks
- [SRC: Glitch Production Techniques]

#### **3. ACC Chaos (0% → 100% Automation)**
- **Move:** Automate ACC from 0% to 100% rapidly during scratches
- **Settings:** Creates unpredictable scratch physics
- **What to Listen For:** Unstable, morphing scratch character
- **Sample Choice:** Any harmonic source for maximum weirdness
- [SRC: Experimental Techniques, [UNVERIFIED]]

#### **4. Comb-Filtered Harmonic Scratches**
- **Move:** Scratch sustained vowel sounds or synth pads slowly
- **Settings:** ACC 40%, emphasize pitch bends
- **What to Listen For:** Formant-like resonances; alien vocal quality
- **Sample Choice:** "Ahh", "Ooh", synth pads with rich harmonics
- [SRC: Sound Design Techniques]

#### **5. Feedback Loop Scratches** (Advanced)
- **Move:** Route Scratcher output back to input via send/return
- **Settings:** Low feedback level (10-20%), ACC 60%
- **What to Listen For:** Self-oscillating, evolving textures
- **WARNING:** Can get loud fast—monitor carefully
- [SRC: Experimental Audio Processing, [UNVERIFIED: Requires careful setup]]

#### **6. Non-Linear Automation Curves**
- **Move:** Use bizarre automation shapes (sawtooth, random LFO)
- **Settings:** Apply to SPD, SEN, or ACC
- **What to Listen For:** Unpredictable, disorienting movement
- **Sample Choice:** Abstract soundscapes, field recordings
- [SRC: Creative Automation Techniques]

### **What NOT to Do:**
❌ Predictable rhythmic scratches (too conventional)  
❌ Natural-sounding tape stops (too familiar)  

### **Listening Checkpoint:**
Does it sound alien and disorienting? Would it fit in a psych-rock or experimental electronic track?

## 4. JAZZY

### Production Definition
Swung timing, sophisticated harmony, live feel, instrumental interplay, improvisational quality.

### Levers to Pull

#### **1. Swing-Timed Scratches**
- **Move:** Scratch on swing grid (not straight 16ths)
- **Settings:** ACC 50%, match swing % of track (typically 60-70%)
- **What to Listen For:** Laid-back, groovy scratch timing
- **Sample Choice:** Jazz vocal scat, brass stabs, upright bass notes
- [SRC: Jazz Production Guides]

#### **2. Instrumental Scratch Solos**
- **Move:** Scratch saxophone, trumpet, or piano samples as "solos"
- **Settings:** ACC 40-60%, expressive speed variations
- **What to Listen For:** Human, improvisational quality
- **Sample Choice:** Single-note jazz instruments, clean recordings
- [SRC: Jazz Sampling Techniques]

#### **3. Dynamic Sensitivity for Expression**
- **Move:** Vary scratch pressure (harder drags = faster scratches)
- **Settings:** SEN 40-60% for nuanced control
- **What to Listen For:** Musical phrasing, not mechanical patterns
- **Sample Choice:** Melodic sources—keys, horns, vibes
- [SRC: Expressive Performance Techniques]

#### **4. Brush-Like Scratch Technique**
- **Move:** Use slow, smooth platter drags (like brush on snare)
- **Settings:** ACC 30%, low SPD variation
- **What to Listen For:** Subtle, textured scratch; not aggressive
- **Sample Choice:** Soft percussion, ambient room sounds
- [SRC: Jazz Drum Techniques Applied to Scratching]

#### **5. Call-and-Response Scratching**
- **Move:** Scratch answering melodic phrases in the track
- **Settings:** ACC 50%, match phrasing rhythm
- **What to Listen For:** Conversational interplay with other instruments
- **Sample Choice:** Vocal phrases, melodic riffs
- [SRC: Jazz Composition Techniques]

#### **6. Chromatic Pitch Scratches**
- **Move:** Vary sample pitch (+/- semitones) while scratching for melodic lines
- **Settings:** Use Pitch knob or load differently-tuned samples
- **What to Listen For:** Melodic scratch solos, not just rhythmic
- **Sample Choice:** Single-note instruments at different pitches
- [SRC: Melodic Scratching Techniques]

### **What NOT to Do:**
❌ Straight-grid timing (not swung enough)  
❌ Aggressive, hard scratches (too harsh for jazz vibe)  

### **Listening Checkpoint:**
Does it sound sophisticated and swung? Would it fit in a neo-soul or jazz-hop track?

## 5. VIBEY

### Production Definition
Chill, atmospheric, textural, laid-back, immersive, ambient undertones, "in the zone" feeling.

### Levers to Pull

#### **1. Ambient Scratch Textures**
- **Move:** Scratch pad/ambient samples very slowly
- **Settings:** ACC 20-30%, SPD 0.5x-0.8x
- **What to Listen For:** Evolving, atmospheric texture; not rhythmic
- **Sample Choice:** Synth pads, field recordings, nature sounds
- [SRC: Ambient Production Techniques]

#### **2. Subtle Speed Drift**
- **Move:** Automate SPD very slowly (0.95x ↔ 1.05x over 16 bars)
- **Settings:** ACC 25%, gentle curves
- **What to Listen For:** Warped, tape-like instability; lo-fi vibe
- **Sample Choice:** Lo-fi samples, vinyl crackle, warm synths
- [SRC: Lo-Fi Production]

#### **3. Reverb + Delay on Scratches**
- **Move:** Send scratch channel to heavy reverb/delay
- **Settings:** ACC 40%, long scratch movements for space trails
- **What to Listen For:** Immersive, spacious scratch atmosphere
- **Sample Choice:** Single-note sounds, vocal "ahhs"
- [SRC: Spatial Effects Production]

#### **4. Non-Rhythmic Scratch Gestures**
- **Move:** Scratch without grid alignment—freeform, improvisational
- **Settings:** ACC 35%, no quantization
- **What to Listen For:** Human, organic feel; not machine-locked
- **Sample Choice:** Abstract sounds, textural elements
- [SRC: Experimental Performance]

#### **5. Low-Pass Filtered Scratches**
- **Move:** Filter scratch sample at 2-4kHz before scratching
- **Settings:** ACC 40%, mellow scratch movements
- **What to Listen For:** Warm, muted scratch; not bright/harsh
- **Sample Choice:** Already-warm samples, Rhodes, muted guitar
- [SRC: Lo-Fi/Chill Production]

#### **6. Background Scratch Layers**
- **Move:** Place scratches low in the mix, as texture not lead
- **Settings:** ACC 30%, blend into track atmosphere
- **What to Listen For:** Subconscious texture addition; not focal point
- **Sample Choice:** Ambient pads, soft percussion
- [SRC: Mix Layering Techniques]

### **What NOT to Do:**
❌ Aggressive, loud scratches (breaks chill vibe)  
❌ Tight, quantized rhythms (too rigid for vibey feel)  

### **Listening Checkpoint:**
Does it enhance the atmosphere without drawing focus? Would it fit in a late-night chill playlist?

## Cross-Vibe Applications

Some settings work across multiple vibes:

| **Parameter** | **Moody** | **Upbeat** | **Psychedelic** | **Jazzy** | **Vibey** |
|---------------|-----------|------------|-----------------|-----------|-----------|
| **ACC** | 15-30% | 60-80% | 0-100% (wild) | 40-60% | 20-40% |
| **Sample Type** | Minor vocals, pads | Hype vocals, stabs | Abstract, synths | Jazz instruments | Ambient, lo-fi |
| **Timing** | Slow, deliberate | Rhythmic, tight | Chaotic, random | Swung, expressive | Freeform, drifting |
| **Automation** | Slow tape stops | Spin-ups, bounces | Extreme modulation | Subtle phrasing | Gentle drift |
