# Goal: Vibe - Jazzy

## Goal Statement
Balance a live drum break to sound professional and "clean" while maintaining its organic warmth.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Instrument:** A vintage drum break (sampled from soul/jazz).
2. **Attack:** Boost by +1.5dB (Subtle stick definition).
3. **Release:** Cut by -2dB. This removes the "low-mid buildup" from the old recording room.
4. **Split Freq:** Set to 350Hz. Keep the "thump" of the kick perfectly natural.
5. **Mix:** 50% (Parallel).

## What to Listen For
- A "polishing" effect.
- The break should sound like it was recorded in a better studio, but without losing its vintage "swing."
