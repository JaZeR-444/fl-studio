# SynthMaker vs. Patcher: The Modular Choice

## SynthMaker (The Low-Level Lab)
- **Level:** Component (Add, Multiply, Code).
- **Pros:** Total control. Can build things that don't exist yet. HID controller support.
- **Cons:** **32-bit only.** High learning curve. Hard to share with modern FL users.
- **The "Vibe":** Scientific, experimental, and limitless.

## Patcher (The High-Level Studio)
- **Level:** Plugin (Sytrus, Fruity Delay, Maximus).
- **Pros:** 64-bit native. Use your favorite plugins as modules. Fast workflow. Massive community support.
- **Cons:** Limited to what existing plugins can do. No low-level DSP math.
- **The "Vibe":** Creative, fast, and professional.

## Why use SynthMaker in 2026?
1. **Custom Controllers:** Patcher's Dashboard is great, but SynthMaker allows for significantly more complex "interactive" GUIs.
2. **Learning DSP:** There is no better way to understand how a synthesizer works than by building one in SynthMaker.
3. **Legacy Hardware:** If you need to control an old synthesizer via obscure MIDI SysEx commands, SynthMaker's MIDI primitives are often easier to configure. [SRC: IL-MAN-08]
