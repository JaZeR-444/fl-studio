# Vibe Workflow: UPBEAT - Fruity Spectroman

## Upbeat Vibe Spectral Signature

**Visual Target:**
- Extended highs (content to 15+ kHz)
- Clean low-mids (not muddy)
- Strong presence zone (2-5 kHz forward)
- Bright, open top-end

**Quick Spectrum Check:**
```
40-80 Hz:    ████ (controlled bass)
200-500 Hz:  ██ (clean, not heavy)
2k-5k Hz:    ████████ (forward presence)
5k-10k Hz:   ██████ (brightness)
10k-20k Hz:  ████ (extended air)
```

**Spectroman Settings:**
- FFT: 2048-4096
- Decay: Medium
- Peak Hold: ON (catch energetic transients)
- Averaging: Light

---

## Step-by-Step Upbeat Workflow

### 1. Clean Low-Mids (3 min)
**Action:** Cut 300-400 Hz (-1 to -3 dB)  
**Check:** Less red in mud zone, cleaner overall

### 2. Boost Presence (3 min)
**Action:** +2 to +3 dB at 3-5 kHz  
**Check:** More red/yellow in presence, vocals forward

### 3. Add Air (2 min)
**Action:** High-shelf +2 dB at 10-12 kHz  
**Check:** Extended yellow/green in highs

### 4. Widen Stereo (2 min)
**Action:** Stereo enhancement on supporting elements  
**Check:** Vectorscope shows wider oval
