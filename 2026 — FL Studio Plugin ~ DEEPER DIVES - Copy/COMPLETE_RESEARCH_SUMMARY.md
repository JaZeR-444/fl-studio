## Organization Structure

### Workflow by Instrument
Organized plugin usage by specific instruments:
- Drums (808s, snares, kicks)
- Synths (leads, pads, plucks)
- Bass (sub-bass, mid-bass)
- Keys (piano, organ, Rhodes)
- Strings (violins, cellos, pads)
- Vocals (harmony, lead, ad-libs)

### Workflow by Goal
Organized plugin usage by specific production objectives:
- Mixing (glue, separation, enhancement)
- Sound Design (creation, manipulation, synthesis)
- Mastering (final polish, loudness, clarity)
- Genre-Specific (hip-hop, R&B subgenres)
- Creative Effects (atmosphere, texture, movement)

### Data Presets
Cataloged and organized plugin settings and configurations:
- Genre-specific presets (moody, upbeat, psychedelic, etc.)
- Instrument-specific presets (drums, bass, keys, etc.)
- Effect-specific presets (compression, saturation, filtering, etc.)
- Artist signature presets (Metro Boomin, Zaytoven, etc.)

## Genre Coverage

### Hip-Hop/Rap Subgenres
- **Moody**: Dark, atmospheric, introspective sounds
- **Upbeat**: Energetic, positive, danceable elements
- **Psychedelic**: Experimental, trippy, spatial effects
- **Electronic Bass**: Modern, synthesized low-end
- **Jazzy**: Jazz-influenced harmonies and instruments
- **Funky**: Groove-heavy, rhythmic elements

### R&B Subgenres
- **Moody**: Intimate, emotional, ballad-focused
- **Upbeat**: Energetic, danceable, contemporary
- **Psychedelic**: Experimental, atmospheric, ethereal
- **Electronic Bass**: Modern synthesized bass elements
- **Jazzy**: Jazz-influenced, neo-soul, sophisticated
- **Funky**: Groove-heavy, rhythmic, funk-influenced
- **Soulful**: Classic soul, emotional, warm
- **Smooth**: Polished, radio-friendly, refined
