# Pads Analysis with Fruity Spectroman

## Pad Role in Mix

**Function:** Fill spectrum, create atmosphere  
**Frequency Range:** Usually 200 Hz - 12 kHz  
**Stereo:** Often wide for spaciousness  
**Balance:** Support, don't dominate

---

## Pad Mixing Workflow

### Step 1: High-Pass for Clarity (1 min)
1. Solo pads
2. Check low-end in Spectroman
3. High-pass at 200-300 Hz
4. Leaves room for bass/kick

**Check:** Minimal content below 200 Hz

---

### Step 2: Fill Gaps (3 min)
1. Play full mix without pads
2. Note gaps in spectrum (usually 500 Hz - 5 kHz)
3. Bring in pads
4. Should fill gaps without dominating

**Check:** Fuller spectrum, pads complement

---

### Step 3: Stereo Width (2 min)
1. Check vectorscope with pads
2. Pads should show width (oval pattern)
3. Adds spaciousness to mix

**Check:** Wider vectorscope with pads

---

### Step 4: Level Balance (2 min)
1. Pads should be felt more than heard
2. Should NOT be most prominent element
3. Check spectrum: Pads fill, don't lead

**Check:** Supporting role in spectrum

---

## Pad Checklist
- [ ] High-passed (200-300 Hz)
- [ ] Fills spectral gaps
- [ ] Wide stereo field
- [ ] Supports, doesn't dominate
