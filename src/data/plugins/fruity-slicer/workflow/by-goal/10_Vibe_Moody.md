# Workflow: Moody Vibe Sample Flip with Fruity Slicer

## Goal
Create dark, introspective sample flips with smooth vocal/loop chops.

**Vibe Reference:** Drake "Marvins Room," The Weeknd chopped vocals, SZA atmospheric textures

## Routing Context

**Channel Rack:** Fruity Slicer loaded with vocal/ambient sample  
**Mixer:** Slicer routed to mixer track → Reverb send → Delay send  
**FX Chain:** Slicer → EQ (HPF) → Reverb (80% wet) → Delay (1/4 dotted)

---

## Step-by-Step Setup

### Step 1: Sample Selection (Critical)
**Choose:**
- Isolated vocal phrases (R&B, Soul, old records)
- Ambient pads or synth textures
- Slow melodic loops (guitar, keys)

**Avoid:**
- Fast drum breaks (too energetic)
- Bright, aggressive samples

**Where to Find:**
- Splice/Loopcloud (search "ambient vocal")
- YouTube (isolated vocals, creative commons)
- Old vinyl records (sample legally)

---

### Step 2: Load into Slicer
1. Drag sample into Channel Rack
2. Select "Fruity Slicer" when prompted
3. Slicer loads with waveform visible

---

### Step 3: Slice Settings (Moody-Specific)
**Threshold:** 65-70% (fewer, longer slices)  
**Why:** Moody needs smooth phrases, not choppy hits

**Crossfade:** 25-30 ms (very smooth)  
**Why:** Eliminates clicks for reverb-heavy processing

**Time Stretch:** 0.85x to 0.95x (slow it down)  
**Why:** Slower = more introspective

**Pitch:** -3 to -5 semitones (darker tone)  
**Why:** Lower pitch = moody character

---

### Step 4: Manual Slice Adjustment
1. After Auto-detect, **visually inspect** slices
2. Delete slices that cut words mid-syllable
3. Keep only phrase-level slices (whole words/phrases)
4. **Aim for:** 4-8 slices max (long phrases)

---

### Step 5: MIDI Pattern Creation
**Pattern Style:** Sparse, spacious
- **Note Length:** 1/2 notes to whole notes (long)
- **Velocity:** 60-80 (gentle)
- **Rhythm:** Leave gaps (don't fill every bar)

**Example Pattern (4 bars):**
```
Bar 1: Slice 1 (whole note)
Bar 2: Rest
Bar 3: Slice 2 (half note), Slice 3 (half note)
Bar 4: Rest
```

---

### Step 6: FX Chain (Moody Essential)
**EQ:**
- HPF at 150-200 Hz (remove low mud)
- Gentle dip at 400-500 Hz (warmth)

**Reverb:**
- Large hall or chamber
- Decay: 3-4 seconds
- Pre-Delay: 30-50 ms
- Mix: 60-80% (very wet)

**Delay:**
- 1/4 note dotted
- Feedback: 30-40%
- Mix: 25-35%

---

## What to Listen For

### Moody Validation Checklist
- [ ] Chops feel "pulled back," not in-your-face
- [ ] Reverb tail integrates smoothly (not harsh)
- [ ] Transitions are seamless (no clicks)
- [ ] Overall tone is dark and introspective
- [ ] Pattern has space (not overcrowded)

---

## Variations

### Variation 1: Extreme Moody (Ultra-Sparse)
**Settings:**
- Threshold: 75% (only 2-4 slices)
- Pitch: -7 semitones (very dark)
- Pattern: 1 slice every 2 bars
- Reverb: 90% wet (nearly ambient)

**Result:** Extremely minimal, atmospheric

---

### Variation 2: Moody with Movement (Gentle Automation)
**Automate:**
- Time Stretch: 0.9x → 0.85x (gradual slow-down)
- Pitch: -3 → -5 semitones (darker over time)

**Result:** Evolving, dynamic moody texture

---

## Pitfalls & Fixes

### Pitfall 1: "Chops sound clicky"
**Cause:** Crossfade too low  
**Fix:** Increase to 25-30 ms

### Pitfall 2: "Reverb sounds harsh"
**Cause:** Slices cutting mid-syllable  
**Fix:** Delete bad slices, keep whole phrases

### Pitfall 3: "Pattern feels too busy"
**Cause:** Too many slices triggered  
**Fix:** Remove notes, leave more space

---

## Automation Ideas

### Automation 1: Reverb Swell
- Bar 1-2: Reverb 60% wet
- Bar 3-4: Reverb 90% wet (swell into next section)

### Automation 2: Pitch Drift
- Intro: Pitch -3 semitones
- Verse: Pitch -4 semitones (gradual ramp)
- Chorus: Pitch -5 semitones

## Genre-Specific Moody Rules

### Dark R&B (Drake, The Weeknd)
- Vocal source: Isolated R&B vocals
- Pitch: -3 to -4 semitones
- Reverb: 70-80% wet
- BPM: 65-75

### Introspective Hip-Hop (Earl Sweatshirt)
- Source: Ambient loops, field recordings
- Pitch: -5 to -7 semitones (very dark)
- Lo-Fi: Add vinyl crackle, bit-crushing
- BPM: 70-80

### Moody Trap (Sad Boys)
- Source: Melodic synth loops
- Pitch: -2 to -3 semitones
- 808: Clean, sub-heavy (not distorted)
- BPM: 130-140 (half-time feel)

## Summary: Moody Vibe Essentials

> **Moody Sample Flip = Slow, Smooth, Spacious:**
> - Source: Vocal phrases or ambient loops
> - Threshold: 65-70% (fewer slices)
> - Crossfade: 25-30 ms (smooth)
> - Pitch: -3 to -5 semitones (dark)
> - Pattern: Sparse (lots of space)
> - FX: Heavy reverb (70-80%), gentle delay
>
> **Key:** Emotion over energy. Let slices breathe.

**DO:** Long phrases, heavy reverb, space between notes  
**DON'T:** Fast chops, dense patterns, bright tones
