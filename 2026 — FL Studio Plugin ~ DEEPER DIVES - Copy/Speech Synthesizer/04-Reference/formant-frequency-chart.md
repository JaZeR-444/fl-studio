# Formant Frequency Chart: Voice Personality Research

Speech synthesis works by manipulating **Formants**—the resonant frequencies of the virtual vocal tract. Each "Voice" in the synthesizer has a fixed formant profile. [SRC: IL-MAN-06]

## Why Formants Matter:
If you pitch-shift a vocal normally, it sounds like a chipmunk. If you shift only the pitch but keep the formants, it sounds like the *same person* singing a different note.

## Personality Breakdown:

### 1. Classic (The 90s Robot)
- **Formant Focus:** Mid-range (800Hz - 2kHz).
- **Vibe:** Neutral, clear, and "digital."

### 2. Giant (The Dark Trap Voice)
- **Formant Focus:** Low-mid (200Hz - 500Hz).
- **Vibe:** Massive, authoritative, and scary.
- **Production Note:** Great for "Moody" intros when paired with high reverb.

### 3. Child (The High-Energy Vocals)
- **Formant Focus:** High-mid (3kHz - 5kHz).
- **Vibe:** Sharp, thin, and energetic.
- **Production Note:** Excellent for high-pitched producer tags.

### 4. Robot (Metallic Buzz)
- **Formant Focus:** Scattered, non-harmonic resonances.
- **Vibe:** Sci-fi, "Psychedelic," and textured.

## Technical Research Tip:
Use **Fruity Parametric EQ 2** to look at the spectrum of each voice. You will see 2 or 3 distinct "hills." These are the formants. Moving the **Pitch** slider in the synth shifts the notes *within* these hills, which is why the characters stay recognizable.
