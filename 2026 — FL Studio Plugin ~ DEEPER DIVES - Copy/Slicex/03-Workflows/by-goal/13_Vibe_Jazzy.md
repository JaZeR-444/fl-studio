# Goal: Vibe - Jazzy

## Goal Statement
Chop a soul break while maintaining the "swing" and "human" feel of the original performance.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Manual Markers:** Hit `M` at the start of every hit. **DO NOT** use Auto-slice.
2. **Envelope:** In Articulator 1, set **Attack** to 10ms (Soften the strike) and **Release** to 100ms (Let the "air" ring out).
3. **Swing:** Do not quantize your MIDI notes to the grid. Keep them where Slicex "dumped" them originally.
4. **EQ:** Apply a subtle **Low-Mid Boost** (300Hz) to add "warmth" to the snare and kick.
5. **Saturation (External):** Add a "Tape" saturation plugin to the mixer track.

## What to Listen For
- A "relaxed" and "organic" groove.
- You should hear the "ghost notes" and "room noise" between the main hits.
