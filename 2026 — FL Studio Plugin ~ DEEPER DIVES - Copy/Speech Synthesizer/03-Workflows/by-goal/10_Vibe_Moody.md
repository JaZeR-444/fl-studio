# Goal: Vibe - Moody

## Goal Statement
Create an intimate, "secretive" whispered vocal for late-night R&B or dark lo-fi.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Text:** "Only you know the truth."
2. **Mode:** Select **Whispered**.
3. **Rate:** 50% (Slow and breathy).
4. **Render:** Hit OK.
5. **Mixer FX:**
   - **Fruity Chorus:** Set to 50% to widen the whisper.
   - **Fruity Delay 2:** Very subtle delay to add "depth."
   - **Fruity PanOMatic:** Automate the panning to make the whisper "travel" from left to right ear.

## What to Listen For
- A non-tonal, airy texture that adds "mystery" without needing a melody.
- The "closeness" of the whisper—it should feel like it's right in the listener's ear.
