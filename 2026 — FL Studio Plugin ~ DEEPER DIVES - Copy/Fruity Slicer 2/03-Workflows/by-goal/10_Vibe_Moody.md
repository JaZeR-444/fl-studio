## Step-by-Step Setup

### Step 1: Vocal Sample Selection (Moody-Specific)
**Choose:**
- Sustained vowel phrases ("Ahh", "Ooh", "Mmm")
- Female or male vocals (lower register preferred)
- Emotional, soulful character (not bright/happy)
- Long sustain (2+ seconds ideal)

**Where to Find:**
- Splice: Search "RnB vocal", "soul vocal", "dark vocal"
- Acapella packs (isolate sustained sections)
- Record your own (hum/sing sustained notes)

**What to Listen For:** Vocal already has melancholic character

---

### Step 2: Auto-Slice + Select Darkest Vowel
1. Load vocal into Slicer 2
2. Click "Auto" (auto-slice)
3. **Preview each slice** (click on waveform)
4. **Select:** Longest, darkest vowel ("Ooh" or "Ahh")

**Moody Criteria:**
- Sustained (not short/percussive)
- Dark tonality (low harmonics)
- Minimal vibrato (smooth, not shaky)
- Clean (no clicks, background noise)

---

### Step 3: Enable Chromatic Mode
1. Select chosen slice
2. Enable **"Chromatic"** toggle (ON)
3. Enable **"Play to End"** (full slice plays)

**Result:** Vocal slice now playable across keyboard

---

### Step 4: Moody Pitch Settings
**Global Pitch:** -3 to -7 semitones (darker character)
- **Why:** Lower pitch = darker tonality = moody
- **Range:** -3 (subtle dark) to -7 (very dark)

**Keyboard Range:** C2 to C4 (low-mid register)
- **Why:** High notes = bright (anti-moody)
- **Play:** Stay in low octaves for darkness

**What to Listen For:** Vocal feels heavy, dark (not bright)

---

### Step 5: Slow Attack Envelope
**Attack:** 50-150 ms (slow fade-in)
- **Why:** Slow attack = pads (not stabs)
- **Result:** Vocal swells in (atmospheric)

**Release:** 500-1000 ms (long tail)
- **Why:** Sustain after note release (pad-like)

**What to Listen For:** Vocal fades in and out smoothly (no harsh transients)

---

### Step 6: Filter Envelope (Evolving Darkness)
**Filter Type:** Low-Pass  
**Cutoff:** Start at 1-2 kHz (dark)  
**Envelope:** Slow LPF sweep (1 kHz → 4 kHz over 2-4 seconds)

**Why:** Evolving filter = movement (not static)

**Result:** Vocal starts dark, brightens slightly (still moody)

---

### Step 7: FX Chain (Moody Depth)

**Reverb (Spacious, Dark):**
- Decay: 3-5 seconds (long, atmospheric)
- Pre-Delay: 20-30 ms (spacious)
- Mix: 50-70% (very wet, drowning in space)
- Type: Hall or Cathedral (large, dark space)
- **LPF in Reverb:** Cut above 6-8 kHz (dark reverb)

**Tape Saturation (Warmth):**
- Drive: 20-30% (subtle warmth, not distortion)
- Tape Type: Vintage (warm character)
- Wow/Flutter: Subtle (0.1-0.2 Hz, adds instability)

**EQ (Darkness):**
- HPF: 80-100 Hz (remove rumble)
- LPF: 6-8 kHz (remove brightness, very dark)
- Boost 200-400 Hz: +2-3 dB (body, warmth)

**Chorus (Optional - Subtle Width):**
- Rate: 0.2 Hz (very slow)
- Depth: 15-20% (subtle)
- Mix: 20-30%
- **Why:** Adds subtle movement (not static)

**What to Listen For:** Vocal sits FAR back in mix (reverberant, atmospheric)

---

### Step 8: Moody Chord Voicings
**Pattern Style:** Slow, minor chords, low register

**Example Pattern (8 bars):**
```
Bar 1-2: C3 + Eb3 + G3 (C minor, whole notes)
Bar 3-4: Ab3 + C4 + Eb4 (Ab major, whole notes)
Bar 5-6: G2 + Bb3 + D4 (G minor, whole notes)
Bar 7-8: F3 + Ab3 + C4 (F minor, whole notes)
```

**Key Principles:**
- **Minor chords** (dark harmony)
- **Whole notes** or slow rhythm (breathing, not busy)
- **Low register** (C2-C4, not higher)
- **Voice leading** (smooth movement between chords)

**What to Listen For:** Chord progression feels melancholic (not happy/uplifting)

## Variations

### Variation 1: Extreme Moody (The Weeknd-Style)
**Settings:**
- Pitch: -7 semitones (very dark)
- LPF: 5 kHz (extreme darkness)
- Reverb: 5 sec decay, 80% mix (drowning)
- Chord: Sustain single minor chord (8 bars, no change)

**Result:** Extremely dark, atmospheric pad (minimal movement)

---

### Variation 2: Moody with Movement (SZA-Style)
**Settings:**
- Pitch: -3 semitones (moderate dark)
- Filter Envelope: Active sweep (1 kHz → 6 kHz)
- Chorus: 30% mix (more movement)
- Chord Progression: Changes every 2 bars (more harmonic movement)

**Result:** Moody but evolving (not static)

---

## Pitfalls & Fixes

### Pitfall 1: "Sounds too bright, not moody"
**Cause:** Pitch too high, LPF too high  
**Fix:** 
- Lower pitch to -5 to -7 semitones
- LPF at 6 kHz or lower

### Pitfall 2: "Too dry, not atmospheric"
**Cause:** Insufficient reverb  
**Fix:** Reverb 60%+ mix, 4+ sec decay

### Pitfall 3: "Sounds like a stab, not a pad"
**Cause:** Attack too fast  
**Fix:** Increase Attack to 100+ ms

### Pitfall 4: "Chords sound happy"
**Cause:** Major chords  
**Fix:** Use minor chords only (Cm, Fm, Gm)

## Automation Ideas

### Automation 1: Filter Darkness Build
- Intro: LPF at 2 kHz (very dark)
- Verse: LPF at 4 kHz (moderate dark)
- Chorus: LPF at 6 kHz (less dark, more presence)
- Outro: LPF sweep back to 2 kHz (fades to darkness)

### Automation 2: Reverb Depth
- Verse: Reverb 50% mix (moderate space)
- Chorus: Reverb 70% mix (drowning in space)
- Bridge: Reverb 40% mix (more intimate)

## Advanced Technique: Layering for Depth
**Goal:** Rich, multi-dimensional moody pad

**Workflow:**
1. **Layer 1 (Main Pad):** Settings above
2. **Layer 2 (Sub Pad):** Same vocal, pitch -12 semitones (one octave lower), 40% volume
3. **Layer 3 (High Shimmer):** Same vocal, pitch +7 semitones, LPF 3 kHz, 20% volume, heavy reverb

**Pan:**
- Layer 1: Center
- Layer 2: Center (sub)
- Layer 3: 30% L/R alternating

**Result:** Impossibly rich moody pad (professional depth)

## Genre-Specific Moody Settings

### Dark R&B (The Weeknd)
- Pitch: -5 to -7 semitones
- LPF: 5-6 kHz (very dark)
- Reverb: 60-70% mix, Cathedral
- Tempo: 70-85 BPM
- Chords: Minor, sus2/sus4 (ambiguous)

### Alt-R&B (SZA, FKA Twigs)
- Pitch: -3 to -5 semitones (moderate dark)
- LPF: 6-8 kHz (less extreme)
- Reverb: 50-60% mix, Hall
- Tempo: 75-95 BPM
- Chords: Minor 7th, add9 (jazzy extensions)

### Trap-Influenced Moody (Travis Scott)
- Pitch: -4 semitones
- LPF: 6 kHz
- Reverb: 50% mix
- Tempo: 140-150 BPM (Trap tempo, but moody character)
- Add: Bit-crushing (degradation adds darkness)

## Summary: Moody Chromatic Vocal Pad Essentials

> **Moody Chromatic Vocal = Dark, Spacious, Slow:**
> - Source: Sustained vowel ("Ahh", "Ooh")
> - Chromatic: ON (playable pad)
> - Pitch: -3 to -7 semitones (dark)
> - Attack: 50-150 ms (slow swell)
> - Filter: LPF 5-8 kHz (darkness)
> - Reverb: 50-70% mix, 3-5 sec decay (spacious)
> - Chords: Minor, low register (C2-C4)
>
> **Key:** Darkness and space. Let it breathe.

**DO:** Low pitch, long reverb, slow attack, minor chords  
**DON'T:** Bright pitch, dry mix, fast attack, major chords
