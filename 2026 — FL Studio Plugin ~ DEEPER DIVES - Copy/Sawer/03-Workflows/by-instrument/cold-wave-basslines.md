# Instrument: Cold Wave Basslines

## Best Use Cases
- 80s inspired Hip-Hop (think 21 Savage / Metro Boomin "dark" style).
- Post-punk rap crossovers.

## Mini-Recipe: "Bunker Thud"
1. **Filter:** LP 24dB, Cutoff 100Hz.
2. **Env:** Amp Decay 400ms.
3. **Osc:** Sub Max, Saw 20%.
4. **Mode:** Mono.
- *Vibe Mapping:* Moody/Upbeat.

## Mini-Recipe: "Drive Bass"
1. **Sync:** ON.
2. **Filter:** HP mode, Cutoff 500Hz.
3. **Unison:** 4 Voices.
- *Vibe Mapping:* Upbeat/Psychedelic. This bass "cuts" rather than "thumps."
