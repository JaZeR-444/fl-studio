# Practical Use Cases

## 1. The "Tuned" 808
**Goal:** A long, booming sub-bass kick tuned to C.
-   **Main Osc:**
    -   Peak: ~100Hz.
    -   Base: ~32.7Hz (C1).
    -   Slide: Slow/Long.
-   **Global:** Duration MAX.
-   **Click:** Off.
-   **Result:** A massive synthesized 808 that stays perfectly in pitch at the tail.

## 2. The "Layered" House Kick
**Goal:** A kick with a pro "top" and a clean "bottom".
-   **Main Osc:** Sine wave, fast slide (Punch).
-   **Sample:** Load a "Hi-Hat" or "Acoustic Kick" sample.
-   **Processing:**
    -   Sample Filter: High Pass (remove low end from sample).
    -   Main Osc: Provides the low end.
-   **Result:** The texture of a real drum with the weight of a synth.

## 3. The "Gabber/Hardstyle" Crunch
**Goal:** Aggressive, distorted industrial kick.
-   **Sampling Ratio:** Turn LEFT (approx 20-30%). Listen for the metallic ringing aliasing.
-   **Slave Osc:** Turn Level UP. Tune Offset to +12 semitones or specific dissonant intervals.
-   **Drive:** Use external distortion, but the Sampling Ratio provides the core digital grit.

## 4. The "Clicky" Metal Kick
**Goal:** A kick that cuts through a wall of guitars.
-   **Click Section:** Level High.
-   **Click Cut:** Set to ~2kHz.
-   **Main Osc:** Fast slide for immediate impact.
-   **Why:** The dedicated Click generator is sharper and shorter than most samples.
