# Goal: Cleaning Up Wide 808s

## Goal Statement
Fix a messy, wide 808 that is causing phase issues in the low-end while maintaining the aggressive mid-range width.

## Routing Context
1. **808 Channel:** Raw distorted 808.
2. **Spreader:** Inserted after distortion.
3. **Fruity Limiter:** Final sidechain to Kick.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Bypass:** Set the **Low Bypass Freq** to 150Hz.
2. **Mono:** Engage the **Mono** switch. Watch the vectorscope—the very bottom of the cloud should become a vertical line.
3. **Width:** Set **Width** to 70%.
4. **Separation:** Adjust **Separation** until the high-mid "buzz" of the 808 feels wide and surrounding.
5. **Check:** Switch FL Master to Mono. The "thump" of the 808 should stay identical.

## Starting Settings
- **Width:** 70%
- **Bypass Freq:** 150Hz
- **Mono Toggle:** ON

## Benefits
- Solid, mono sub-bass for club compatibility.
- Aggressive, wide mid-range for headphone impact.
- Zero phase cancellation on the important low frequencies.
