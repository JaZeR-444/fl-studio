# Goal Workflow: Bitmap Sound Design (Custom Textures)

*Goal: Designing a unique, evolving atmospheric background for an R&B track using custom-made bitmaps.*

## 🎛️ Routing Context
- **Routing**: BeepMap -> Mixer Track.
- **Mixer Chain**: Fruity Parametric EQ 2 (Low Pass) -> LuxeVerb.

## 🚶 Step-by-Step Setup
1. **The Canvas (External)**:
   - Open MS Paint or Photoshop. Create a **256 x 128** black canvas.
2. **Drawing the "Music"**:
   - Use a soft white brush. Draw 3-4 gentle, wavy lines across the bottom half of the canvas.
   - *Logic*: Wavy lines create "pulsing" frequencies. Bottom half ensures they are warm and bassy.
3. **The Import**:
   - Save as `texture_seed_01.bmp` and drag into BeepMap.
4. **The Scan**:
   - Set **Length** to 5000ms. Enable **Loop**.
   - Set **Scale** to **Log**.
5. **The Refining (In BeepMap)**:
   - Tweak **Freq (Hz)** until the wavy lines sound like deep, resonant choir hums.
6. **The Immersion**:
   - In the Mixer, turn the Cutoff in the EQ to 1kHz (Muffled).
   - Add **LuxeVerb** with a 4-second decay.
   - *Result*: A background atmosphere that moves and breathes exactly how you drew it.

## 🔄 Variations
- **The "High Glitch"**: Draw tiny white dots at the top of the canvas and set Scan Length to 200ms.
- **The "Logo Sound"**: Draw your producer logo in white and hear what your brand "sounds" like.

## ⚠️ Pitfalls & Fixes
- **Problem**: Sound is too harsh.
- **Fix**: You have white pixels at the top of your canvas. Erase them or blur them in your photo editor.
