## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Research framework provides structured approach
The research framework SHALL provide a structured approach for users to understand and explore plugin capabilities systematically.

#### Scenario: Systematic exploration
- **WHEN** a user wants to understand a plugin's capabilities
- **THEN** they MUST follow the structured research framework to explore systematically

### Requirement: Research framework includes learning phases
The research framework SHALL include defined phases or steps that guide users through progressive understanding of the plugin.

#### Scenario: Progressive learning
- **WHEN** a user begins researching a plugin
- **THEN** they MUST follow the defined phases in the research framework to build understanding progressively

### Requirement: Research framework defines success metrics
The research framework SHALL include measurable success metrics that allow users to validate their understanding of the plugin.

#### Scenario: Understanding validation
- **WHEN** a user completes the research framework
- **THEN** they MUST be able to validate their understanding using the defined success metrics

### Requirement: Research framework suggests practical exercises
The research framework SHALL suggest practical exercises or tasks that reinforce understanding of plugin capabilities.

#### Scenario: Practical application
- **WHEN** a user is learning through the research framework
- **THEN** they MUST have practical exercises to apply their knowledge and reinforce learning
