# Goal: Creating Thick Strings

## Goal Statement
Design a massive, cinematic string pad that uses the "Warm" mode to fill the entire frequency spectrum.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Oscillators:** Osc 1, 2, and 3 all set to **Saw**.
2. **Warm Mode:** Enable **Warm** on all three oscillators (Total of 6 oscillators).
3. **Detuning:** Fine-tune Osc 2 to +10 cents and Osc 3 to -10 cents.
4. **Filter:** LP Mix 100%. Cutoff at 1kHz.
5. **Envelopes:** Amp Attack 1.5s, Release 2.0s.
6. **Chorus:** Enable built-in Chorus. Set Mix to 50%.

## What to Listen For
- A "thick" wall of sound that doesn't sound thin or digital.
- A slow, majestic swell that feels organic.

## Automation Ideas
- **Filter Swell:** Automate the **Cutoff** from 500Hz to 2kHz over 8 bars.
- **Chorus Depth:** Increase the Chorus mix during the chorus of your song.
