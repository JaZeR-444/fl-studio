# Fruity Slicer 2: Quickstart (5 Minutes to Chromatic Vocal Instrument)

## Goal
Create a playable vocal instrument from a vocal sample in 5 minutes.

**Result:** Vocal chop → Lead melody instrument (Trap/Pop style)

[SRC: IL-MAN]

---

## 5-Minute Workflow

### Step 1: Load Sample (30 seconds)
1. Drag vocal sample into Channel Rack
2. Select **"Fruity Slicer 2"** from sampler menu
3. Waveform appears in Slicer 2 window

**Sample Recommendation:** Vocal phrase with sustained vowels ("Ahh", "Ooh", "Yeah")

---

### Step 2: Auto-Slice (30 seconds)
1. Click **"Auto"** button (detects slices automatically)
2. Waveform now has slice markers (vertical lines)
3. **Verify:** Slices should align with vocal syllables

**Typical Result:** 8-16 slices for 2-bar vocal loop

---

### Step 3: Select Best Slice (1 minute)
1. **Click each slice** on waveform (preview plays)
2. **Listen for:** Sustained vowel sound ("Ahh", "Ooh")
3. **Select:** Slice with best sustain (longest, cleanest)

**What to Look For:**
- Long, sustained vowel (not short consonant)
- Clean tone (no clicks, pops, artifacts)
- Interesting character (vibrato, warmth)

**Example:** If vocal says "Yeah-yeah-ooh", select "Ooh" slice (sustained)

---

### Step 4: Enable Chromatic Mode (30 seconds)
1. With slice selected, find **"Chromatic" toggle**
2. Click **"Chromatic" ON** (checkbox checked)
3. Also enable **"Play to End"** (plays full slice, not just loop start)

**Critical:** Chromatic mode = slice plays across entire keyboard (pitched correctly)

---

### Step 5: Play Melody (1 minute)
1. Open Piano Roll (F7)
2. **Play notes:** C4, E4, G4 (C major chord)
3. **Hear:** Selected vocal slice at different pitches

**Result:** Vocal "Ahh" = playable instrument (like a synth lead)

**Test:** Play a simple melody (C4 → D4 → E4 → G4)

---

### Step 6: Adjust Attack (Optional, 1 minute)
1. In Slicer 2, find **"Attack" envelope** (per-slice)
2. **Lower Attack** to 5-10 ms (faster transient, less fade-in)
3. **Result:** Vocal responds faster (more synth-like)

**Why:** Default attack can be slow (mushy). Fast attack = crisp.

---

### Step 7: Add FX (Optional, 1 minute)
**Quick FX Chain:**
1. Route to Mixer track
2. Add **Fruity Reverb 2:** Decay 2 sec, 30% mix (space)
3. Add **Fruity Compressor:** 4:1, attack 10 ms (punch)

**Result:** Polished vocal instrument (radio-ready)

## Common First-Time Issues

### Issue 1: "It plays the whole loop, not one slice"
**Cause:** "Play to End" disabled or wrong slice selected  
**Fix:** 
1. Select specific slice (click on waveform)
2. Enable "Play to End"
3. Enable "Chromatic"

---

### Issue 2: "Sounds like chipmunk at high notes"
**Cause:** Normal behavior (pitch shifting)  
**Fix:** 
- Play in comfortable range (C3-C5 for vocals)
- OR lower global pitch (-3 semitones)

---

### Issue 3: "Attack is slow/mushy"
**Cause:** Default attack envelope  
**Fix:** Lower Attack to 5-10 ms (faster response)

---

### Issue 4: "Can't find Chromatic toggle"
**Cause:** Not looking in right section  
**Fix:** Look in **main Slicer 2 panel** (top section, near "Play to End")

---

### Issue 5: "Slices in wrong places"
**Cause:** Auto-detect imperfect  
**Fix:** 
1. Manually add/move/delete slices (right-click waveform)
2. OR adjust Auto-detect sensitivity (Threshold slider)

## Troubleshooting Decision Tree

```
Can't hear anything?
├─ Is slice selected? (Click waveform slice)
│  └─ Yes → Is "Chromatic" ON?
│     └─ Yes → Is "Play to End" ON?
│        └─ Yes → Check MIDI routing
└─ No → Select a slice first

Sounds wrong pitch?
├─ Chromatic enabled?
│  └─ Yes → Normal (plays at note pitch)
└─ No → Enable Chromatic

Whole loop plays?
└─ Enable "Play to End" + Select single slice
```
