# SF2 Format Basics: The Legacy Sampler

## What is an SF2?
A SoundFont (.sf2) is a file format that bundles one or more multi-sampled instruments into a single file. Unlike a `.wav` file which is just one sound, a `.sf2` contains a "map" of which sample plays at which MIDI note and velocity. [SRC: IL-MAN-05]

## Anatomy of a SoundFont
1. **Samples:** The raw audio recordings.
2. **Instruments:** Collections of samples mapped to regions of the keyboard.
3. **Presets:** The final "patches" you see in the plugin, which can combine multiple instruments and add internal envelopes/filters.

## Why use SF2 today?
- **Speed:** SFP loads 500MB banks in milliseconds.
- **Variety:** There are 25+ years of free SoundFonts available online, covering everything from Nintendo 64 sounds to $50,000 Grand Pianos.
- **CPU:** SFP is one of the most CPU-efficient ways to get high-quality acoustic sounds in FL Studio.

## Recommended Free Banks for Hip-Hop/R&B
- **SGM-V2.0:** The "Gold Standard" for General MIDI (Pianos, Guitars, Drums).
- **Crisis General MIDI:** Extremely high-quality orchestral sounds.
- **GeneralUser GS:** A versatile, all-purpose bank with a "warm" vintage character.
- **N64/SNES SoundFonts:** Found on sites like Musical Artifacts, perfect for "Retro" or "Video Game Rap."
