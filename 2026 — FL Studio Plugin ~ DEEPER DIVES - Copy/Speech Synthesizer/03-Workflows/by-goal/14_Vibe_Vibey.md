# Goal: Vibe - Vibey

## Goal Statement
Create a smooth, wide vocal-synth "pad" using melodic speech.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Text:** Use the syntax `(C5)Vibe (G5)Lush`.
2. **Style:** **Monotone/Sing**.
3. **Rate:** 30% (Very slow, stretching the vowels).
4. **Render:** Hit OK.
5. **Mixer FX:**
   - **Fruity Chorus:** 50% Mix.
   - **Fruity Delay 3:** Large Hall Delay.
   - **Fruity Love Philter:** Simple LFO LP filter.

## Mini-Recipe: "The Silk Robot"
- Rate at 20%.
- Monotone style.
- Whisper mode.
- Layered with a real vocal.
- *Result:* Adds a "synthetic breath" layer to your vocal that makes it sound modern and unique.
