# Goal: Vibe - Vibey

## Goal Statement
Create a lush, wide R&B "cloud" pad that feels expansive and expensive.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Oscillator:** Saw 60%, Sub 30%.
2. **Filter:** LP 12dB. Cutoff 35%.
3. **Unison:** 8 Voices. Detune 12%. Pan 100% (Full width).
4. **Envelopes:** Attack 1s, Release 2s.
5. **Acoustics:** Reverb Size 100%, Mix 25%. Delay Mix 15%.
6. **Matrix:** LFO -> Filter (10%) for subtle movement.

## Mini-Recipe: "Satin Pad"
- Filter LP 24dB.
- Cutoff at 300Hz.
- Chorus ON (Mix 50%).
- Unison 4 voices.
- *Result:* A thick, "expensive" sounding pad that sits perfectly behind vocals.
