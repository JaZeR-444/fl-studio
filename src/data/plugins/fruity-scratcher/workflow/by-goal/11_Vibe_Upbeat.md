# Fruity Scratcher: Vibe Workflow – UPBEAT

## Overview
This workflow creates **energetic, bouncy, positive scratch patterns** for upbeat hip-hop/pop-rap productions. Focus: fast rhythmic scratches, chirps, tight grid alignment.

---

## Routing Context

**INSERT:** Scratcher on FX/scratch track  
**SEND:** 20% to short plate reverb (Fruity Reeverb 2)  
**PARALLEL:** Optional—parallel with chorus for width  
**SAMPLING ROUTING:** Load bright, percussive vocal samples

---

## Step-by-Step Setup

### 1. Sample Selection
- **Load bright vocal sample:**
  - High-energy ad-libs: "yeah!", "let's go!", "uh-huh!", "hey!"
  - Major key melodic phrases
  - Percussive consonants (sharp "k", "t", "ch" sounds)
- **Where to find:** Splice (search "hype vocal"), Prime Loops, or record yourself

### 2. Parameter Starting Points
```
ACC (Acceleration): 85%
SEN (Sensitivity): 75%
SPD (Speed): 100% (will automate up for risers)
Pan: Center (will pan scratches for bounce)
Volume: 0dB (prominent in mix)
```

### 3. Scratch Technique
- **Chirp scratch** (forward-back-forward with crossfader)
  - 4 scratches per bar (on 16th-note grid)
  - Sharp, quick movements (no sustain)
- **Baby scratch** (fast back-and-forth)
  - Double-time feel (8 scratches per bar)

### 4. Automation Setup
- **Automate SPD knob:**
  - Bars 1-6: 100% (locked groove)
  - Bars 7-8: 100% → 130% (riser effect into drop)
- **Automate Pan:**
  - Scratch 1: Center
  - Scratch 2: Hard Left
  - Scratch 3: Hard Right
  - Scratch 4: Center
  - (Creates stereo bounce pattern)
- **What to listen for:** Scratches should drive energy forward

### 5. Post-FX Chain
1. **High-Pass Filter** (Fruity Love Philter)
   - Cutoff: 200Hz (keep scratches bright)
   - Resonance: 5%
2. **Short Plate Reverb** (Fruity Reeverb 2)
   - Size: 40%
   - Decay: 1.2 seconds
   - Mix: 20%
3. **Stereo Widener** (Fruity Stereo Enhancer)
   - Stereo Separation: +30%

## Variations

### Variation 1: Double-Time Chirps
- Set FL Studio grid to 32nd notes
- Perform rapid chirp scratches (8 per bar)
- ACC: 95% (maximum snap)
- **What to listen for:** Scratches should be machine-gun fast

### Variation 2: Call-and-Response
- Bar 1: 4 scratches (call)
- Bar 2: Silence (response from other elements)
- Bar 3-4: Repeat
- Creates interactive groove with drums

---

## Pitfalls + Fixes

### ❌ PITFALL: "Scratches sound sluggish"
**FIX:** Increase ACC to 90-100%, increase SEN to 80%+, scratch faster physically

### ❌ PITFALL: "Scratches get lost in the mix"
**FIX:** High-pass at 300Hz, increase volume, reduce reverb mix to 15%

### ❌ PITFALL: "Riser sounds abrupt"
**FIX:** Extend SPD automation to 4 bars (smoother transition), add filter sweep

---

## Automation Ideas

### Automation 1: Stutter Riser
- Automate SPD in steps: 100% → 110% → 120% → 130% (every 2 bars)
- Creates stuttering riser effect
- **What to listen for:** Should feel like building energy in stages

### Automation 2: Sensitivity Modulation
- Automate SEN: 70% → 90% → 70% (4-bar cycle)
- Creates dynamic responsiveness
- **What to listen for:** Scratches should vary in intensity

---

## What To Listen For (Quality Checks)

✅ **Scratches are sharp and snappy** (not dragging)  
✅ **Riser builds tension** (smooth upward movement)  
✅ **Stereo bounce is clear** (L/R alternation audible)  
✅ **Scratches sit on top of mix** (prominent, not buried)  
✅ **Overall vibe feels energetic** (not laid-back)

---

## Mono Compatibility Check
- Collapse to mono → stereo bounce will collapse to center
- Ensure scratches still audible (increase volume if needed)
- If scratches disappear, reduce stereo width to 20%

---

## Commit Workflow
1. Record scratch performance into Piano Roll (MIDI automation)
2. Render to audio for consistency
3. Slice rendered audio into one-shots for additional sequencing

---

## Genre Application: Upbeat Hip-Hop/Pop-Rap

### Use Cases:
- **Hook:** Fast chirp scratches on "yeah!" sample (every 2 bars)
- **Intro:** Call-and-response scratch pattern (8 bars)
- **Pre-Drop:** Riser automation into chorus (last 4 bars of verse)
