# Fruity Soft Clipper: Vibe Preset Shortlist

## Moody Vibe Presets

### Moody Master - Minimal Glue
**Settings:**
- Threshold: -0.3 dB
- Post Gain: 0 dB

**Use When:** Dark R&B, introspective Hip-Hop, Drake-style tracks  
**What It Does:** Barely-there glue without aggression  
**Listen For:** Mix feels cohesive but not "pushed"

---

### Moody Vocal - Ultra Gentle
**Settings:**
- Threshold: -0.5 dB
- Post Gain: 0 dB

**Use When:** Lead vocals in moody tracks  
**What It Does:** Smooths peaks for clean reverb tail  
**Listen For:** Vocals sit "inside" reverb naturally

**Settings:**
- Threshold: -8.0 dB
- Post Gain: +3.0 dB

## How to Use This Shortlist

### Step 1: Identify Your Vibe
- Is the track moody? upbeat? psychedelic? jazzy? vibey?

### Step 2: Load Corresponding Master Preset
- Moody → "Moody Master - Minimal Glue"
- Upbeat → "Upbeat Master - Competitive"
- Etc.

### Step 3: Load Supporting Presets
- Drums: Load vibe-appropriate drum preset
- Bass: Load vibe-appropriate bass preset
- Reverb: Load vibe-appropriate reverb preset (if applicable)

### Step 4: Adjust by Ear
- Presets are starting points, not final settings
- Fine-tune Threshold ±0.5 dB based on your mix
