# Goal: Vibe - Vibey

## Goal Statement
Create a lush, wide, and smooth R&B "cloud" pad that feels expensive and immersive.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Source:** OP 1 set to a complex additive wave (Osc tab -> "Analysis" of a choir sample).
2. **Unison:** 9 Voices, 10% Detune, 30% Sub-level.
3. **Width:** Set **Unison Pan** to 100%.
4. **Envelope:** Slow 2s Attack, 3s Release.
5. **FX:** Delay 1 (1/4), Delay 2 (1/8), Delay 3 (1/16). All at 10% Mix.
6. **Result:** A massive "wash" of sound that fills the speakers effortlessly.

## Mini-Recipe: "Silk Texture"
- OP 1 Sine wave.
- OP 2 Sine wave (Ratio 2.000).
- Unison 9 voices (Tight detune 2%).
- Chorus ON (Mix 50%).
- *Result:* A high-end, smooth "sheen" pad for modern R&B.
