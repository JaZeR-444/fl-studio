# Goal: Vibe - Upbeat

## Goal Statement
Create a bright, high-definition FM bell that drives the melody of an energetic trap track.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Carrier (OP 1):** Sine wave, Ratio 1.000.
2. **Modulator (OP 2):** Sine wave, Ratio 4.000.
3. **Matrix:** Route **OP 2 -> OP 1** at 60%.
4. **Envelope:**
   - OP 1 Vol: 0ms Attack, 800ms Decay.
   - OP 2 Vol: 0ms Attack, 150ms Decay.
5. **Oversampling:** Set to **16x** for maximum high-frequency clarity.
6. **FX:** Add a short **Reverb** (Size 20%, Mix 15%).

## What to Listen For
- A "crystal clear" digital strike.
- The high ratio (4.000) provides that "chime" or "bell" edge that cuts through 808s.
