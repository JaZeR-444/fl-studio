# Goal: Quick Result - Fruity Spectroman

## Objective

Get immediate visual feedback on your mix's frequency balance and stereo field to make fast, confident mixing decisions.

**Time Investment:** 2-5 minutes  
**Skill Level:** Beginner-friendly  
**Result:** Clear understanding of frequency imbalances and stereo imaging

---

## 60-Second Setup

### Step 1: Load Spectroman (10 seconds)
1. Mixer → Master channel
2. Add effect → Fruity Spectroman
3. Position window where visible

### Step 2: Quick Settings (10 seconds)
- FFT Size: 2048 (default is usually fine)
- Decay: Medium
- Peak Hold: ON
- Range: Full (20 Hz - 20 kHz)

### Step 3: Play Your Mix (40 seconds)
- Play chorus or busiest section
- Observe spectrum for 30-40 seconds
- Note overall shape

---

## The 3-Point Quick Check

### Check 1: Low-End Balance (Look Left Side)

**What to See:**
```
✅ GOOD: Moderate red/yellow at 50-100 Hz
❌ BAD: Heavy red below 30 Hz (rumble/waste)
❌ BAD: All blue at 40-80 Hz (no bass weight)
```

**Quick Fix:**
- Too much sub (below 30 Hz): Add high-pass filter
- Weak bass: Check bass/kick levels, consider boost at 60-80 Hz

---

### Check 2: Clarity Zone (Look at 2-5 kHz)

**What to See:**
```
✅ GOOD: Yellow/green presence at 2-5 kHz
❌ BAD: All blue (lacks clarity/presence)
❌ BAD: Bright red spikes (harsh/fatiguing)
```

**Quick Fix:**
- Lacks clarity: Boost 2.5-4 kHz on vocals/leads
- Too harsh: Cut 3-5 kHz (-2 to -3 dB)

---

### Check 3: Stereo Field (Look at Vectorscope)

**What to See:**
```
✅ GOOD: Oval/circular pattern, strong vertical component
❌ BAD: Horizontal line (over-widened, phase issues)
❌ BAD: Pure vertical (too mono, no width)
```

**Quick Fix:**
- Too wide: Reduce stereo enhancement
- Too narrow: Add width to supporting elements (not bass!)

---

## Fast Visual Diagnosis

### Problem: Mix Sounds Muddy

**Spectroman Shows:**
- Heavy red at 200-500 Hz
- Dark at 2-5 kHz (lacks clarity)

**Quick Action:**
1. Cut 300-400 Hz (-2 to -4 dB) with EQ
2. Boost 3 kHz (+1 to +2 dB) for clarity
3. Check Spectroman: Should see less red in mud zone

---

### Problem: Mix Sounds Harsh

**Spectroman Shows:**
- Bright red spikes at 3-5 kHz
- May extend to 6-8 kHz

**Quick Action:**
1. Identify exact peak frequency
2. Cut with narrow Q (-2 to -4 dB)
3. Check Spectroman: Spike should reduce

---

### Problem: Mix Sounds Dull

**Spectroman Shows:**
- All blue above 5 kHz
- Weak 2-4 kHz zone

**Quick Action:**
1. Boost 3-4 kHz (+2 dB) for presence
2. Add air shelf at 10 kHz (+1 to +2 dB)
3. Check Spectroman: Should see more content in highs

---

### Problem: Bass is Weak

**Spectroman Shows:**
- Blue/cyan at 40-100 Hz
- Lacking low-end presence

**Quick Action:**
1. Check bass/kick levels (may just be quiet)
2. Boost 60-80 Hz (+2 to +3 dB)
3. Check Spectroman: Should see red/yellow increase

---

## 2-Minute Reference Comparison

### Fast A/B Method

**Setup (30 seconds):**
1. Load reference track in FL Studio
2. Roughly match playback volume

**Compare (90 seconds):**
1. Play reference for 30 seconds → observe spectrum
2. Mental snapshot of overall shape
3. Play your mix for 30 seconds → compare
4. Note biggest difference
5. Make one targeted adjustment

**What to Compare:**
- Overall tilt (bright vs. warm)
- Low-end extension (how much bass?)
- Presence zone (2-5 kHz energy)
- High-end air (above 10 kHz)

**Don't:**
- Try to match exactly
- Make multiple changes at once
- Compare at different volumes

## Quick Win Workflows

### Workflow 1: "5-Minute Mix Check"

**Purpose:** Fast verification of mix balance

1. Load Spectroman on Master (30 sec)
2. Play mix, do 3-point check (2 min)
3. Identify biggest issue (30 sec)
4. Make one EQ adjustment (1 min)
5. Verify in Spectroman (1 min)

**Result:** One significant improvement in 5 minutes

---

### Workflow 2: "Instant Bass Verification"

**Purpose:** Confirm low-end is correct

1. Load Spectroman, zoom to 20-200 Hz (20 sec)
2. Play section with bass/kick (30 sec)
3. Check for:
   - Kick at 50-80 Hz (red/yellow)
   - Bass at 60-120 Hz
   - No rumble below 30 Hz
4. Adjust if needed (1-2 min)

**Result:** Confident low-end in 3 minutes

---

### Workflow 3: "Stereo Width Quick Check"

**Purpose:** Verify stereo imaging

1. Load Spectroman (20 sec)
2. Watch vectorscope while playing (1 min)
3. Check pattern:
   - Bass centered (vertical)
   - Width on supporting elements (oval)
4. Test mono (switch to mono, listen) (30 sec)
5. Adjust width if needed (1 min)

**Result:** Proper stereo field in 3 minutes

## Quick Visual Targets

### Balanced Mix (Quick Reference)

**Quick Look:**
```
LEFT (bass)    : Moderate red (not too heavy)
MIDDLE (mids)  : Yellow/green (present, not excessive)
RIGHT (highs)  : Green/cyan gradually fading (not all blue)
```

**Vectorscope:**
```
Shape: Oval/circular (width + center)
Bass: Strong vertical component (mono)
```

## Fastest Route to Better Mix

**If you have only 2 minutes:**

1. **Open Spectroman** (10 sec)
2. **Check for red below 30 Hz** → High-pass if present (30 sec)
3. **Check for excess red at 200-400 Hz** → Cut if muddy (30 sec)
4. **Check if 2-5 kHz is visible** → Boost if dull (30 sec)
5. **Done** - Three quick wins

---

## When to Stop

**You've achieved quick result when:**
- ✅ No excessive rumble (below 30 Hz)
- ✅ Controlled low-mids (200-500 Hz not dominating)
- ✅ Visible presence zone (2-5 kHz has content)
- ✅ Stereo field shows width with centered bass

**Don't keep tweaking if:**
- Mix sounds good to your ears
- Visual roughly matches reference
- You're making tiny adjustments with no audible improvement

---

## Common Quick-Fix Traps

### ❌ Trap 1: Chasing Perfect Visual
**Issue:** Trying to make spectrum look "ideal"  
**Reality:** If it sounds good, it is good

### ❌ Trap 2: Making Too Many Changes
**Issue:** Adjusting multiple things at once  
**Reality:** One targeted fix at a time

### ❌ Trap 3: Comparing Loud to Quiet
**Issue:** Reference is louder, looks better  
**Reality:** Match levels first

---

## The Bottom Line

**Fruity Spectroman gives you visual confirmation in seconds.** Load it on Master, do the 3-point check (low-end, clarity, stereo), identify the biggest issue, make one targeted fix, and verify. You don't need perfect spectrum - you need to know if there's excessive rumble, muddy low-mids, lack of presence, or phase issues. Spectroman shows you these problems instantly so you can fix them fast and move on.

**Your 60-Second Checklist:**
1. Low-end: Red at 50-100 Hz, not below 30 Hz ✓
2. Clarity: Content visible at 2-5 kHz ✓
3. Stereo: Oval vectorscope, bass centered ✓

**If all three check out, you're good to go.**
