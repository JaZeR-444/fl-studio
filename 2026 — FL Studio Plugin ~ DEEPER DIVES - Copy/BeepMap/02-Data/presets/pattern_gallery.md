# BeepMap Pattern Gallery

Instead of traditional presets, BeepMap relies on "Source Images":

## 1. The "Frequency Sweep" (Diagonal Line)
- A simple line from bottom-left to top-right.
- Results in a pure, rising sine wave sweep.

## 2. The "White Noise" (Static)
- An image of random black and white noise (like TV static).
- Results in a rich, textured white/pink noise burst.

## 3. The "Harmonic Series" (Horizontal Lines)
- Multiple horizontal lines spaced apart.
- Results in a static, organ-like chord or complex harmonic tone.

## 4. The "Rhythmic Pulse" (Vertical Bars)
- Vertical white bars on a black background.
- Results in a rhythmic, clicking "Geiger counter" effect.
