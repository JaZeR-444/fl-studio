# Psychoacoustics of the Kick

## The Haas Effect in Layering
If your sample transient and synth transient hit slightly apart (1-5ms), the brain perceives them as a single, thicker sound.
- **BassDrum Strategy:** Use the **Sample Delay** to slightly offset the sample click. This can make the kick feel "wider" or "larger" without adding actual stereo width.

## Fundamental Dominance
The human ear prioritizes the lowest stable frequency.
- **Rule:** If your kick tail (Base Freq) is changing pitch (vibrato or too much slide), the listener will perceive the entire track as "out of tune."
- Always ensure the **Base** frequency is a static, pure tone at the end of the kick's duration.
