# Genre Style Board: Sytrus

## Hip-Hop
- **Technique:** "Audible FM Sub." Use two Sine operators in a 1:1 ratio. Slightly modulate the carrier with the modulator (10% FM). This adds harmonic "bite" that makes the sub-bass audible on laptop and phone speakers.
- **Reference Idea:** Think of the deep, punchy sub-bass in modern Pierre Bourne or Pierre-influenced productions.

## Rap
- **Technique:** "Cold Trap Bells." Use a high FM Ratio (e.g., 4.000 or 8.000) for the modulator. Use a very short volume envelope on the modulator to create a metallic "ping."
- **Reference Idea:** The "icy" bell melodies in early 2010s trap (Lex Luger, 808 Mafia).

## R&B / Pop-Hip-Hop
- **Technique:** "The 9-Voice Cloud Pad." Use the additive engine to build a soft wave. Set Unison to 9 voices with tight detuning. Enable the internal Chorus and 3 Delay lines.
- **Reference Idea:** The lush, "expensive" sounding background pads in modern R&B like Summer Walker or SZA.
