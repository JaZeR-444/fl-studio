# Goal: Vintage Computer Narration

## Goal Statement
Recreate the classic 80s/90s PC "text-to-speech" sound for lo-fi interludes or technological-themed beats.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **The Text:** Type a technical-sounding sentence.
2. **Voice:** Select **Classic**.
3. **Style:** Select **Natural**.
4. **Rate:** Set to 110% (Slightly rushed).
5. **Pitch:** Leave at 0.
6. **Render:** Hit OK.
7. **Mixer FX:**
   - **Fruity Parametric EQ 2:** Use a "Radio" preset (High-Pass at 500Hz, Low-Pass at 3kHz).
   - **iZotope Vinyl (External):** Add some "Year: 1980" grit.

## What to Listen For
- The characteristic "clipping" and "nasal" quality of early computer speech.
- The "robotic" but intelligible cadence of the Natural style.
