# 00: Quickstart - 5 Minutes to Your First Sound

## Step-by-Step "Vintage Bass"
1. **Reset:** Go to the plugin menu and select "Reset."
2. **Oscillator:** Turn the **Saw** to 80% and the **Sub Level** to 100%.
3. **Phase:** Set **Sub Phase** to 180° for a thick, solid foundation.
4. **Filter:** Select **LP mode** and **24dB slope**.
5. **Cutoff:** Turn **Cutoff** down to 200Hz.
6. **Snap:** Turn the **Filter Env Amount** to +50% and set **Filter Decay** to 500ms.
7. **Verify:** Play a low note (C1–C3). You should hear a thick, snappy analog bass.

## Best "First Result" Recipe: Lo-Fi Keys
- **Voices:** Set to Poly.
- **Unison:** 2 voices, Detune 10%.
- **Filter:** LP 12dB, Cutoff 50%.
- **FX:** Chorus Mix 30%, Reverb Mix 15%.
- **Movement:** In the Matrix, route LFO to Pitch with Amount 1%.
- *Result:* Ethereal, unstable keys perfect for R&B or Lo-Fi.
