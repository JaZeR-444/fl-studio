# Newtone - Start Here

Welcome to the **Newtone** Deeper Dive. Newtone is FL Studio's advanced pitch correction and time manipulation plugin.

## What is Newtone?

Newtone provides:
- Pitch correction
- Time stretching
- Pitch shifting
- Formant control
