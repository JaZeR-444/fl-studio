## Step-by-Step Jazzy Workflow

### 1. Enhance Warmth (3 min)
**Action:** +2 to +3 dB at 250-500 Hz

### 2. Natural Presence (2 min)
**Action:** +1 to +2 dB at 2-4 kHz (smooth)  
**Check:** Gentle rise, not spike

### 3. Natural Roll-Off (2 min)
**Action:** Slight cut above 12 kHz  
**Check:** Gradual fade to blue

### 4. Preserve Dynamics (ongoing)
**Action:** Light compression only  
**Check:** Spectrum stays dynamic
