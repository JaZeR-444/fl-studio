# 01: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Symptom | The Fix | How to Verify |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Too much Unison on Bass** | Bass sounds weak, phasing, or "hollow." | Reduce Unison to 1 voice or 0% detune. | Check the Master meter for low-end consistency. |
| **Resonance too high on sub** | "Whistling" sound masks the actual bass note. | Lower the `Resonance` or `Cutoff`. | You should hear the "thump" more than the "whistle." |
| **Free-running LFO** | Rhythmic modulation is different every time. | Enable `Retrig` on the LFO section. | Every note should have the exact same "wobble." |
| **Poly mode on Leads** | Notes overlap and sound messy. | Switch `Voices Mode` to `Mono` or `Legato`. | Only one note should play at a time. |
| **Sub Phase misalignment** | The sub-bass sounds "quiet" when the Saw is loud. | Adjust the `Sub Phase` knob until it sounds full. | The waveform should look "stable" in an oscilloscope. |
