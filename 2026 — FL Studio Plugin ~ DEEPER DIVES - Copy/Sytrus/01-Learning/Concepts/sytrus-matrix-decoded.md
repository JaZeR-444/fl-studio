# Sytrus Matrix Decoded: The Synthesis Hub

## What is the Matrix?
The Sytrus Matrix is where you define the architecture of your patch. It is a 9x9 grid that handles all signal routing. [SRC: IL-MAN-09]

## How to Read the Grid:

### 1. The Rows (Sources)
- **OP 1-6:** The six operators.
- **F1-F3:** The outputs of the three filter units.

### 2. The Columns (Destinations)
- **OP 1-6:** Where you send signals for **FM** (Frequency Modulation) or **RM** (Ring Modulation).
- **F1-F3:** Where you send signals to be filtered.
- **OUT:** Where you send signals to the Master Output.
- **PAN:** Where you send signals to the Pan engine.

## Common Routing Patterns:

### A. Simple Subtractive (Traditional Synth)
- **Matrix:** Turn up **OP 1 -> F1** and **F1 -> OUT**.
- **Vibe:** Classic, warm, and easy to control.

### B. FM Carrier/Modulator (Bells/EPs)
- **Matrix:** Turn up **OP 2 -> OP 1** and **OP 1 -> OUT**.
- **Vibe:** Metallic, digital, and sharp. OP 2 is "modulating" the frequency of OP 1.

### C. Ring Modulation (Robotic/Scary)
- **Matrix:** Left-click the knob at the intersection of **OP 2 -> OP 1** until the ring turns blue (indicating RM mode).
- **Vibe:** Distorted, non-harmonic, and futuristic.

### D. Parallel Filtering (Lush Layers)
- **Matrix:** **OP 1 -> F1**, **OP 2 -> F2**, **F1 -> OUT**, **F2 -> OUT**.
- **Vibe:** Allows you to have a bright lead and a dark sub-layer in the same patch.

## Secret Shortcut:
- **Right-Click Matrix Knobs:** Quickly toggle between 0%, 50%, and 100%.
- **Middle-Click:** Resets the knob to its default value.
