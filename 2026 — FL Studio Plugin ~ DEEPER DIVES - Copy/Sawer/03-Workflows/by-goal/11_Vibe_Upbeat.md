# Goal: Vibe - Upbeat

## Goal Statement
Create a snappy, high-energy analog stab for "poppy" trap or upbeat hip-hop.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Oscillator:** Saw 100%, Sub Level 50%.
2. **Filter:** LP 24dB. Cutoff 20%. Resonance 35%.
3. **Envelope:** Filter Env Amount 80%. Decay 300ms.
4. **Unison:** 2 Voices (Tight). Detune 5%.
5. **Effects:** Chorus Mix 20% for "pop" sheen.

## What to Listen For
- A "chirpy" transient that hits at the same time as the snare.
- A clean, solid analog weight that doesn't linger too long.
