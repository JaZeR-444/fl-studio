## 🌑 MOODY

### 1. Dark Vocal Pad (★★★★★)
**File**: `Chromatic_Moody_VocalPad_Minus12.fsc`  
**Best For**: Intro/verse background, emotional hooks  
**Quick Settings**:
- Chromatic: ON
- Pitch: -12 semitones
- Attack: 100ms
- Release: 800ms
- Filter: Low-pass 1.2kHz
- Time-Stretch: Elastique Pro

**Sample Requirements**: Sustained vocal phrase with clear vowel (alto/baritone vocals work best)  
**Mix Context**: Layer under main vocals, -8dB volume, add long reverb (3-5s decay)  
**Vibe Keywords**: Melancholic, heavy, introspective, Drake-style

---

### 2. Reverse Texture Swell (★★★★☆)
**File**: `Rhythmic_Moody_ReverseTexture_LongRelease.fsc`  
**Best For**: Build-ups, transitions, eerie atmosphere  
**Quick Settings**:
- Chromatic: OFF
- Reverse: ON (2-3 slices)
- Attack: 0ms (preserve reverse swell)
- Release: 400ms
- Filter: Band-pass 500Hz-2kHz
- Pan: Wide L/R spread

**Sample Requirements**: Drum loop or percussion break  
**Mix Context**: Place on off-beats, 30-40% volume of main drums, add ping-pong delay  
**Vibe Keywords**: Nostalgic, eerie, disorienting, lo-fi

---

### 3. Stretched Minor Chord (★★★★☆)
**File**: `Chromatic_Moody_MinorChord_Stretch200.fsc`  
**Best For**: Verse bass, bridge pad, dragging melodic foundation  
**Quick Settings**:
- Chromatic: ON
- Pitch: -7 semitones
- Time-Stretch: 200% (Elastique Pro)
- Attack: 50ms
- Release: 1000ms
- Filter: Low-pass 800Hz-1.2kHz

**Sample Requirements**: Single bass note or synth chord from sample  
**Mix Context**: Play whole notes in Dm/Am/Fm, boost 200-400Hz, add hall reverb  
**Vibe Keywords**: Dragging, heavy, cello-like, emotional weight

---

## ⚡ UPBEAT

### 1. Bounce Stutter (★★★★★)
**File**: `Rhythmic_Upbeat_VocalStutter_Plus5.fsc`  
**Best For**: Drops, ad-lib fills, energy boosts  
**Quick Settings**:
- Chromatic: OFF
- Pitch: +5 semitones
- Attack: 0ms
- Release: 80ms
- Filter: High-pass 300Hz, boost 2-5kHz
- Trigger: 1/16 note stutters

**Sample Requirements**: Short vocal syllables ("yeah," "uh," "hey")  
**Mix Context**: Loud (100-127 velocity), tight quantization, add 1/16 dotted delay  
**Vibe Keywords**: Bouncy, bright, energetic, Carti-style

---

### 2. Rapid Vocal Stabs (★★★★☆)
**File**: `Chromatic_Upbeat_VocalStabs_Plus3.fsc`  
**Best For**: Melodic hooks, rapid-fire chops  
**Quick Settings**:
- Chromatic: ON
- Pitch: +3 semitones
- Attack: 0ms
- Release: 100ms
- Filter: Slight high-shelf boost
- Trigger: 1/8 or 1/16 melodic runs

**Sample Requirements**: Clear vocal syllables with pitch  
**Mix Context**: Play in major keys (C, G, D), lock to grid, compress hard  
**Vibe Keywords**: Tight, bright, forward, pop-trap

---

### 3. Bright Percussion Chops (★★★★☆)
**File**: `Rhythmic_Upbeat_PercussionChops_HighEnergy.fsc`  
**Best For**: Hi-hat chops, percussion fills  
**Quick Settings**:
- Chromatic: OFF
- Pitch: +2 semitones
- Attack: 0ms
- Release: 50ms
- Filter: High-pass 1kHz
- Trigger: Rapid 1/32 rolls

**Sample Requirements**: Hi-hat loops, shaker loops, bright percussion  
**Mix Context**: Layer over main drums, pan L/R alternation, high in mix  
**Vibe Keywords**: Crisp, tight, energetic, trap-style

## 🌀 PSYCHEDELIC

### 1. Warp Zone (★★★★★)
**File**: `Chromatic_Psychedelic_WarpZone_Stretch400.fsc`  
**Best For**: Build-ups, breakdowns, experimental sections  
**Quick Settings**:
- Chromatic: ON
- Pitch: Automate -12 to +12
- Time-Stretch: 400% (Elastique Vintage for artifacts)
- Attack: Random per slice (0-200ms)
- Release: 800ms
- Filter: High resonance, automate cutoff

**Sample Requirements**: Any sample (vocal, synth, drum)  
**Mix Context**: Play atonal intervals, add shimmer reverb 100% wet, heavy flanger  
**Vibe Keywords**: Trippy, disorienting, Travis Scott-style, acid rap

---

### 2. Glitch Random Pan (★★★★☆)
**File**: `Rhythmic_Psychedelic_GlitchPan_OddDivisions.fsc`  
**Best For**: Glitch stutters, chaotic fills  
**Quick Settings**:
- Chromatic: OFF
- Pitch: 0 (or automate randomly)
- Attack: 0ms
- Release: 100-500ms (random per slice)
- Pan: Random hard L/R per slice
- Trigger: Odd divisions (1/7, 1/13)

**Sample Requirements**: Short percussive or vocal slices  
**Mix Context**: Off-grid triggering, high feedback delay (60%+), reverb wash  
**Vibe Keywords**: Chaotic, glitchy, unpredictable, experimental

---

### 3. Reverse + Forward Mix (★★★★☆)
**File**: `Hybrid_Psychedelic_ReverseMix_Disorienting.fsc`  
**Best For**: Transitions, disorienting textures  
**Quick Settings**:
- Chromatic: OFF
- Reverse: ON for 50% of slices
- Attack: 0ms
- Release: 300ms
- Filter: Automate cutoff wildly
- Trigger: Mix forward/reverse slices randomly

**Sample Requirements**: Any rhythmic or melodic sample  
**Mix Context**: Layer under main elements, phaser/flanger FX, stereo width  
**Vibe Keywords**: Reality-bending, drunk timing, Asap Rocky-style

## 🎷 JAZZY

### 1. Rhodes Swing 58% (★★★★★)
**File**: `Chromatic_Jazzy_RhodesSwing58.fsc`  
**Best For**: Jazz-rap chords, lo-fi hip-hop progressions  
**Quick Settings**:
- Chromatic: ON
- Pitch: 0 semitones
- Attack: 10ms
- Release: 500ms

**Mix Context**: Play Dm7-G7-Cmaj7-Am7, slight stereo width, small room reverb  
**Vibe Keywords**: Musical, swung, organic, Tribe Called Quest-style

## How to Use This Shortlist

1. **Identify your vibe** (moody, upbeat, psychedelic, jazzy, vibey)
2. **Load the #1 go-to preset** for that vibe
3. **Load appropriate sample** (check "Sample Requirements")
4. **Test in mix context** (don't solo—hear it with other elements)
5. **Tweak if needed** (adjust pitch, filter, or envelopes)
6. **Save as new variant** if you make significant changes
