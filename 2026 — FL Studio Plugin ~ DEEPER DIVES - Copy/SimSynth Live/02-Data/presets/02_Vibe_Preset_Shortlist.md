# Vibe Preset Shortlist

## 1. Moody (Dark & Emotional)
- **Deep Triangle:** Osc 1 (Triangle, Warm), LP 100%, Cutoff 200Hz.
- **Dusty Organ:** Osc 1 & 2 (Sine), BP 50%, HP 20%, Chorus 30%.
- **Lonely Brass:** Saw (Warm), slow Attack, LP 100% at 400Hz.
- **Tired Synth:** Osc 1 (Square), LFO -> Pitch (1%), high Damping.
- **Submerged Pad:** Triangle + Noise, Filter Cutoff at 150Hz.

## 2. Upbeat (Punchy & 80s)
- **Classic 80s Brass:** Osc 1 & 2 (Saw, Warm), Filter Env +70%, Decay 1s.
- **Snappy Bass:** Osc 1 (Square), Osc 3 (Sine Sub), short Decay.
- **Laser Lead:** Saw (Warm), High Resonance, Fast Pitch LFO.
- **Pop Synth:** Square (PWM modulated), Chorus 20%.
- **High-Pass Pluck:** HP 50%, LP 30%, fast Decay.

## 3. Psychedelic (Trippy & Moving)
- **Ring Mod Bells:** Sine Ring Mod, LFO -> Filter (Fast).
- **Hollow Sweep:** LP 50%, HP 50% (Notch), LFO -> Cutoff.
- **Phase Blur Pad:** Max Chorus, high Unison Detune, LFO -> Pan.
- **Metallic Growl:** Saw Ring Mod, High Resonance LP filter.
- **Warped Space:** LFO -> Pitch (5% depth), Max Delay feedback.

## 4. Jazzy (Warm & Sophisticated)
- **Smooth Analog Keys:** Square + Sine, LP 12dB, Attack 50ms.
- **Hollow Body Bass:** Triangle + Square, LP 100% at 300Hz.
- **Jazz Organ:** Sine Drawbar (Osc 1 & 2), BP Mix 30%.
- **Soft Brass Pad:** Saw (No Warm), Slow Attack, LP 40%.
- **Clean Vibe:** Sine Ring Mod (Subtle), high Release.

## 5. Vibey (Lush & Modern)
- **R&B Cloud:** 3 Saws, All Warm, Max Chorus, Reverb (External).
- **Satin Lead:** Saw (Warm), Mono Legato, High Glide.
- **Lush Pulse Pad:** Pulse (PWM), Chorus 50%, High Release.
- **Modern Keys:** Sine + Square, Detune 5 cents, Reverb Size 100%.
- **Wide 808 Lead:** Sine (Warm), Unison Spread, LP 20%.
