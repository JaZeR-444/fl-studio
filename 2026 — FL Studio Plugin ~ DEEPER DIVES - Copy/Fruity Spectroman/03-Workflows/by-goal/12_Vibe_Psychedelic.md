# Vibe Workflow: PSYCHEDELIC - Fruity Spectroman

## Psychedelic Vibe Spectral Signature

**Visual Target:**
- MOVING, dynamic spectrum
- Rich harmonic complexity
- Wide stereo field
- Full-range presence

**Quick Spectrum Check:**
- Spectrum should CHANGE visibly (sweeping, pulsing)
- Multiple frequency peaks simultaneously
- Extended highs with reverb tails
- Wide, active vectorscope

**Spectroman Settings:**
- FFT: 2048 (responsive to movement)
- Decay: Fast-Medium (see movement)
- Peak Hold: OFF (preserve dynamics)
- Averaging: OFF (show real-time changes)

---

## Step-by-Step Psychedelic Workflow

### 1. Add Modulation Effects (5 min)
**Action:** Phaser, flanger, chorus on synths  
**Check:** Spectrum sweeps and moves visibly

### 2. Widen Stereo Field (3 min)
**Action:** Stereo enhancement, wide reverb  
**Check:** Vectorscope expands to wide oval

### 3. Extend Harmonics (3 min)
**Action:** Saturation, distortion adds overtones  
**Check:** Additional frequency peaks appear

### 4. Add Reverb/Delay (2 min)
**Action:** Long reverb tails  
**Check:** Extended high-frequency content

---

## Psychedelic Checklist
- [ ] Spectrum is moving (not static) ✓
- [ ] Wide vectorscope ✓
- [ ] Multiple frequency peaks ✓
