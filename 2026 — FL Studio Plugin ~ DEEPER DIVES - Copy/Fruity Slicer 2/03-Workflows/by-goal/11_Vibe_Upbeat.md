# Vibe: Upbeat

## Fruity Slicer 2 Upbeat Sample Flip

### Vibe Definition
**Upbeat = Fast + Bright + Bouncy + Energetic**

---

## Settings for Upbeat

**Core Settings:**
- **Chromatic Mode:** ON (for melodic chops)
- **Pitch:** +2 to +4 semitones (brighter)
- **Time-Stretch:** Transient mode (clean, punchy)
- **Slice Mode:** 1/8 Beat (tight, rapid slices)
- **Envelope:** Fast attack (0-10ms), short release (100-200ms)

---

## Step-by-Step

1. **Load energetic sample** (vocal run, brass stab, synth arp)
2. **Auto-slice at 1/8 beat** for tight chops
3. **Enable Chromatic mode** on best slice
4. **Pitch up +3 semitones** for brightness
5. **Set fast attack envelope** (5ms) for snappy response
6. **Create 16th note pattern** in Piano Roll
7. **Add brightness:** EQ boost 8-12kHz

## Post-Processing

- Fruity Parametric EQ: +2dB shelf 10kHz
- Light compression: Fast attack, 3:1 ratio
- Subtle stereo width (10-15%)

---

## What To Listen For

**✅ Success:**
- Bright, energetic tone
- Tight, punchy transients
- Bouncy rhythm

**❌ Avoid:**
- Muddy low-end
- Slow attack (kills bounce)
- Sparse patterns

---

## Genre Context

**Modern Trap:** Vocal chop melodies, bouncy 808s
**Pop-Hip-Hop:** Clean vocal instruments, bright mix
**Dance/EDM:** Rapid fire vocal chops, build-up energy

## Summary

Upbeat Slicer 2 flips use **Chromatic mode + pitch up + fast envelopes + 16th patterns**. Keep it tight, bright, and bouncy.
