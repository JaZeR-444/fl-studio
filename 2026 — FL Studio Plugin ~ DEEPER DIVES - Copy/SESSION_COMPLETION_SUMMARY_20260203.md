## Vibe Translation Framework Applied

Each vibe workflow translates abstract terms into concrete actions:

**Moody:**
- Pitch down (-5 to -7 semitones)
- Low time-stretch (20-40% for artifacts)
- Slow chop patterns
- Reverb for space
- Low-pass filtering

**Upbeat:**
- Pitch up (+2 to +5 semitones)
- High time-stretch (70-100% for clarity)
- Fast 16th note patterns
- Brightness EQ boost
- Tight compression

**Psychedelic:**
- Extreme pitch automation
- Time-stretch mode morphing
- Random/chaotic patterns
- Heavy modulation FX
- Embrace artifacts

**Jazzy:**
- Subtle pitch (-2 to +1)
- Moderate time-stretch (40-60%)
- Swing quantize
- Warmth/saturation
- Organic feel

**Vibey:**
- Moderate pitch down (-3 to 0)
- Smooth time-stretch (60-80%)
- Slow evolving patterns
- Reverb/delay tails
- Filtered highs
