# Fruity Scratcher — by-instrument: Samples (Loops & Textures)

## Sample Loop Scratching

Scratching full loops and texture samples creates background atmospheres and lo-fi character.

---

## Mini-Recipe 1: Jazz Loop Warble (Lo-Fi)

### Parameters
- SPD: 0.92x ↔ 1.08x (slow sine automation)  
- ACC: 35%  
- Sample: Jazz/soul loop (4-8 bars)

### Steps
1. Load jazz or soul sample loop
2. Automate SPD with slow sine wave
3. Add Effector (vinyl mode)
4. Layer low in mix

### What to Listen For
- [ ] Subtle pitch wobble (vinyl warble)
- [ ] Warm, vintage character
- [ ] Blends as background texture

### Vibe Mapping
- **Jazzy:** Perfect fit
- **Vibey:** Slower wobble (0.95x ↔ 1.05x)

---

## Mini-Recipe 2: Ambient Pad Reverse (Psychedelic/Vibey)

### Parameters
- SPD: -0.6x to -0.8x  
- ACC: 45%  
- Sample: Ambient pad, vocal hum (5-10 sec)

### Steps
1. Load sustained pad or vocal
2. Set SPD to reverse playback
3. Add heavy reverb (60% mix)
4. Low in mix as atmosphere

### Vibe Mapping
- **Vibey:** Sustained reverse texture
- **Psychedelic:** Pair with delay and phaser

---

## Mini-Recipe 3: Sample Loop Tape Stop (Transition)

### Parameters
- SPD: 1x → 0.7x (lazy stop, doesn't reach 0x)  
- ACC: 30%  
- Sample: Full loop (4-8 bars)

### Steps
1. Load sample loop
2. Automate SPD from 1x to 0.7x
3. Don't reach full stop (maintains groove)
4. Use for transitions

### Vibe Mapping
- **Jazzy:** Lazy stop (1x → 0.7x)
- **Moody:** Full stop (1x → 0x)

---

## Mini-Recipe 4: Texture Scratch (Experimental)

### Parameters
- SPD: 0.8x to 1.2x (varied)  
- ACC: 40-60%  
- Sample: Any sustained texture

### Steps
1. Load ambient or noise texture
2. Manually scratch slowly
3. Create gentle pitch movement
4. Experimental background element

### Vibe Mapping
- **Psychedelic:** Wide SPD range, heavy FX
- **Vibey:** Minimal movement
