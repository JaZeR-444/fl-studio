# Instrument Workflow: Ambient Glitch Textures (Pixel Dust)

*Goal: Using pixel art and high grain settings to create digital ear-candy for Trap beats.*

## 🎛️ Routing Context
- **Setup**: BeepMap -> Mixer Track -> Fruity PanOMatic.

## 🚶 Step-by-Step Setup
1. **The Source**:
   - Create a small 64x64 black image. Place 5-6 random white and red pixels around the top and middle.
2. **The Import**:
   - Load into BeepMap.
3. **The Texture (Grain)**:
   - Turn the **Grain** knob all the way to the right (Maximum).
   - *Result*: Instead of smooth lines, you will hear "stepping" and digital artifacts for every pixel.
4. **The Rhythm (Length)**:
   - Set **Length** to 150ms. Enable **Loop**.
   - *Result*: A rapid-fire digital glitch pattern.
5. **The Movement**:
   - In the Mixer, use **Fruity PanOMatic** with a fast Sine LFO on the Pan knob.
   - *Result*: The glitches will dance around the listener's head.
6. **The Result**: High-frequency digital "dust" that adds complexity to your hi-hat patterns.

## 🔄 Variations
- **The "Data Stream"**: Use a screenshot of computer code to generate a "hacker" style background noise.
- **The "Star Field"**: Use a photo of the night sky for a random, twinkling melodic texture.

## ⚠️ Pitfalls & Fixes
- **Problem**: Glitches are too loud and piercing.
- **Fix**: Use a **Fruity Limiter** with a high ceiling to catch the peaks, and use a High-Shelf EQ to lower everything above 10kHz.
