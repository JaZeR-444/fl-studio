# Phase Alignment in Kick Synthesis

Phase alignment is the most important concept when layering synthesized kicks.

## The Problem: Hollow Kicks
If two oscillators (Main and Slave) or an oscillator and a sample are 180 degrees out of phase, their frequencies cancel each other out. The kick will sound "thin" or "weak" even if the volume is high.

## The Solution: Phase & Delay
- **Slave Phase:** This knob shifts the starting position of the Slave oscillator's waveform.
- **Workflow:**
  1. Set Main and Slave levels to be audible.
  2. Slowly sweep the **Phase** knob while playing a 4/4 kick pattern.
  3. Listen for the moment the low-end "blooms" or becomes solid.
- **Sample Delay:** Use this to move the attack transient of your loaded sample. If the sample click and the synth click hit at the same time and are out of phase, you lose the "punch". Shifting the delay by just a few milliseconds can fix this.
