# Fruity Scratcher — by-instrument: Leads (Melodic Scratching)

## Lead & Synth Scratching

Less common in hip-hop/R&B, but useful for creative melodic manipulation.

---

## Mini-Recipe 1: Synth Lead Pitch Bend

### Parameters
- SPD: 0.8x → 1.3x (automate)  
- ACC: 55%  
- Sample: Synth lead phrase (2-4 sec)

### Steps
1. Load synth lead sample
2. Automate SPD for pitch bends
3. Creates warbly, psychedelic lead
4. Pair with reverb + delay

### Vibe Mapping
- **Psychedelic:** Wide SPD range (0.5x ↔ 1.8x)
- **Vibey:** Subtle drift (0.95x ↔ 1.05x)

---

## Mini-Recipe 2: Melody Loop Scratch (Experimental)

### Parameters
- SPD: 1x  
- ACC: 70%  
- SEN: 60%  
- Sample: Melodic phrase (1-3 sec)

### Steps
1. Load short melody
2. Scratch in time with beat
3. Creates stuttered melody effect
4. Experimental technique

### Vibe Mapping
- **Upbeat:** Rapid melodic scratches
- **Jazzy:** Swung timing

---

## Mini-Recipe 3: Lead Tape Stop (Transition)

### Parameters
- SPD: 1x → 0x  
- ACC: 40%  
- Sample: Lead or melody (2-4 sec)

### Steps
1. Load lead phrase
2. Tape stop before section change
3. Creates musical transition

### Vibe Mapping
- **Moody:** Gradual stop
- **Upbeat:** Quick stop

---

## Mini-Recipe 4: Reverse Lead Swell

### Parameters
- SPD: 0x → -1x  
- ACC: 45%  
- Sample: Lead ending (2-4 sec)

### Steps
1. Load lead phrase ending
2. Reverse swell into new section
3. Pair with reverb

### Vibe Mapping
- **Moody:** Slow, haunting reverse
- **Psychedelic:** Heavy FX chain
