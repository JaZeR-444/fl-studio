# Gabber Kick Distortion Chain

**Context:** Hardcore, Gabber, and Industrial Techno.

### The "BassDrum" Source
BassDrum is legendary for this because of its **Sampling Ratio** knob.

### Workflow
1.  **The Source:**
    -   Main Osc: Peak 150Hz, Base 50Hz.
    -   **Sampling Ratio:** Turn LEFT to about 9 o'clock. The sound should turn into a bit-crushed, ringing mess.
2.  **The Distortion (Internal):**
    -   Crank the Main Osc Level to max.
    -   Use the **Slave Osc** at +12 semitones (1 octave up) to add a screaming layer.
3.  **The Distortion (External):**
    -   Route to Mixer.
    -   Add **Fruity Waveshaper**. Draw a steep S-curve.
    -   Add **Fruity Parametric EQ 2**. Boost the "ringing" frequency (usually around 400-600Hz) created by the Sampling Ratio alias.
4.  **Result:** A kick that sounds like a sheet of metal being hit with a hammer.
