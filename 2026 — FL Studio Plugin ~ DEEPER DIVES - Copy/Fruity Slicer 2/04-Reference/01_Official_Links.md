## Related FL Studio Tools

### Complementary Slicing/Sampling Plugins

**Slicex**
- Manual: https://www.image-line.com/fl-studio-learning/fl-studio-online-manual/html/plugins/Slicex.htm
- Usage: Advanced slicing with auto-mapping, more features than Slicer 2
- Workflow: Use Slicex for complex projects, Slicer 2 for quick work

**DirectWave**
- Manual: https://www.image-line.com/fl-studio-learning/fl-studio-online-manual/html/plugins/DirectWave.htm
- Usage: Multi-sample instruments, advanced sampling
- Workflow: Export Slicer 2 slices to DirectWave for layering

**Edison**
- Manual: https://www.image-line.com/fl-studio-learning/fl-studio-online-manual/html/plugins/Edison.htm
- Usage: Audio editing, sample preparation before slicing
- Workflow: Edit/normalize in Edison, then slice in Fruity Slicer 2
