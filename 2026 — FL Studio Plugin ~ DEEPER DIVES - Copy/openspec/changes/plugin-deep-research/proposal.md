## Why

To deepen the understanding of specific FL Studio plugins by conducting rigorous research into their usage, pairings, and factual data, going beyond basic documentation to support advanced workflows and plugin development.

## What Changes

- Conduct deep research for the following plugins:
  - 3x Osc
  - Drumaxx
  - Edison
  - FL Keys
  - Morphine
  - Patcher
  - Soundgoodizer
  - Toxic Biohazard
  - Wave Candy
- Update documentation with factual data, advanced usage techniques, and workflow pairings for each plugin.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `plugin-deep-dives`: Comprehensive research and documentation of features, usage patterns, and pairings for selected FL Studio plugins.
