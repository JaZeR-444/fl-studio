# Core Techniques And Best Practices: BassDrum

## 1. The "Perfect" Sub Tuning
- **Technique**: In the **BASE** tab, set the "End" frequency to match your song's key.
- **Move**: Use a Frequency-to-Note chart (e.g., 41.2Hz for E1).
- **Benefit**: This ensures the tail of your kick drum resonates with your bassline, creating a solid "foundation" for the track.

## 2. Transient Layering (The "Click" Trick)
Make your kick audible on iPhone speakers.
- **Technique**: Go to the **SAMPLE** tab.
- **Move**: Select a sharp "Noise" sample. Turn the **High Pass Filter** to 5kHz.
- **Goal**: This adds a dedicated high-frequency "snap" that tells the listener a kick has hit, even if their speaker can't play the sub-frequencies.

## 3. The "Vacuum" Pull (Offset)
- **Technique**: Use the **Offset** knob in the SAMPLE tab.
- **Move**: Set a very small positive offset (e.g., 5ms).
- **Listen For**: The synthesized "thump" hits first, then the "click" hits.
- **Effect**: This creates a "sucking" or "breathing" sensation that can add a unique groove to R&B kicks.

## 4. Drive Stage "Glue"
- **Technique**: Set **Drive** to "Soft" or "Tube."
- **Move**: Increase the Amount to 30%.
- **Result**: This compresses the Base and Sample layers together, making them feel like one single instrument rather than two layers.

## 5. Mono Consistency Verification
- **Protocol**: Synthesized kicks are 100% mono by default.
- **Verification**: Switch your Master track to Mono.

## Common Pitfalls + Fixes
| Pitfall | The Fix |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"Buzzy" Bass** | Your **Start** frequency is too high or the **Pitch Decay** is too slow. |
| **Weak Attack** | Increase the **Volume** of the Sample layer. |
| **Over-clipping** | Lower the **Post-Gain** in the Drive tab after adding distortion. |
