# Bass Analysis with Fruity Spectroman

## Bass Frequency Zones

**Fundamental:** 40-120 Hz (note-dependent)  
**Harmonics (warmth):** 120-250 Hz (even harmonics)  
**Harmonics (brightness):** 300-600 Hz (odd harmonics)  
**Presence:** 1-3 kHz (string/pick attack)

---

## Bass Analysis Workflow

### Step 1: Identify Fundamental (2 min)
1. Solo bass
2. Zoom Spectroman to 20-200 Hz
3. Play bass line, note where peaks occur
4. Typical fundamentals:
   - E1 = 41 Hz
   - A1 = 55 Hz
   - D1 = 37 Hz
   - (Note-dependent)

**Check:** Clear red peak at fundamental frequency

---

### Step 2: Bass vs. Kick Relationship (5 min)
1. Solo bass + kick
2. Check 40-100 Hz zone
3. Identify overlap:
   - Kick at 60 Hz, bass at 80 Hz = good separation
   - Both at 60 Hz = masking issue

**Solutions:**
- Sidechain bass to kick
- Tune kick/bass to different frequencies
- EQ cut bass slightly at kick fundamental

**Spectroman Check:** Should see distinct peaks for each

---

### Step 3: Harmonic Balance (3 min)
1. Check 120-600 Hz zone
2. Too much = muddy bass
3. Too little = thin bass

**Adjustments:**
- Boost 120-250 Hz for warmth
- Cut 300-400 Hz if muddy
- Check Spectroman for balance

---

### Step 4: 808 Sub-Bass (Hip-Hop/Trap)

**Special Considerations:**
- Fundamental at 40-60 Hz (very deep)
- Should DOMINATE low-end visually
- Must be mono (vectorscope vertical)

**808 Workflow:**
1. Tune 808 to key of track
2. Boost 40-60 Hz if needed
3. High-pass at 30 Hz (clean rumble)
4. **Check:** Heaviest zone in spectrum

---

## Common Bass Issues

**Weak Bass:**
- Fundamental not prominent (weak red at 40-120 Hz)
- **Fix:** Boost fundamental, check level

**Muddy Bass:**
- Too much 200-500 Hz
- **Fix:** Cut 300-400 Hz

**Bass Disappears in Mono:**
- Stereo processing on bass
- **Fix:** Make bass mono, check vectorscope

**Bass Competes with Kick:**
- Both at same frequency
- **Fix:** Sidechain or tune differently

---

## Bass Checklist
- [ ] Fundamental clear and strong
- [ ] Separated from kick frequency
- [ ] Harmonics balanced (not muddy)
- [ ] Mono (vectorscope vertical)
- [ ] Strong in mono playback
