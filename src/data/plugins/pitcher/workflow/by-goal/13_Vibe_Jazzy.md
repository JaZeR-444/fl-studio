# Vibe Workflow: JAZZY (Extensions)

**Context:** Adding color notes to a vocal.

1.  **Mode:** MIDI.
2.  **Chord:** Play a Major 9th chord on MIDI (Root, 3, 5, 7, 9).
3.  **Balance:**
    *   Pan Root: Center.
    *   Pan 3rd/7th: Left.
    *   Pan 9th: Right.
4.  **Result:** A lush, wide jazz choir that follows your lead voice.
