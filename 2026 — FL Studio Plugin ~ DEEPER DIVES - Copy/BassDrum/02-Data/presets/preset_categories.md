# BassDrum Preset Categories

BassDrum presets are typically grouped by their intended genre:

- **Electronic:** Pure sine-based kicks for House/Techno.
- **Acoustic:** Hybrid patches that use the Sample layer heavily to mimic a real kick drum.
- **Hard:** Overdriven, aliased kicks for Hardstyle/Gabber.
- **Long/808:** Sustained sub-kicks for Trap and Hip-Hop.

*Note: Most producers prefer starting from the "Default" patch and tuning the Base frequency to their song's key immediately.*
