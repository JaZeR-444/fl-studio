# Fruity Scratcher — by-instrument: FX (Transitions & Risers)

## Scratching FX Samples

Use Scratcher on risers, crashes, and FX for dynamic transitions.

---

## Mini-Recipe 1: Riser Pitch Bend (Build-Up)

### Parameters
- SPD: 0.8x → 1.5x (automate)  
- ACC: 70%  
- Sample: Riser or synth swell (4-8 beats)

### Steps
1. Load riser sample
2. Automate SPD from 0.8x to 1.5x
3. Creates rising pitch effect
4. Use before drops

### What to Listen For
- [ ] Smooth pitch rise
- [ ] Builds energy and tension
- [ ] Peaks at drop downbeat

### Vibe Mapping
- **Upbeat:** Fast rise (2 beats)
- **Moody:** Slow rise (6-8 beats)

---

## Mini-Recipe 2: Crash Reverse Swell

### Parameters
- SPD: 0x → -1x  
- ACC: 40%  
- Sample: Cymbal crash (2-4 sec)

### Steps
1. Load crash or cymbal sample
2. Automate SPD from 0x to -1x (reverse swell)
3. Use as tension builder

### Vibe Mapping
- **Moody:** Slow swell (4-6 beats)
- **Psychedelic:** Pair with reverb + delay

## Mini-Recipe 3: White Noise Tape Stop

### Parameters
- SPD: 1x → 0x  
- ACC: 85-95%  
- Sample: White noise or texture (1-2 sec)

### Steps
1. Load noise/texture sample
2. Quick tape stop (1 beat)
3. Use for dramatic transitions

### Vibe Mapping
- **Upbeat:** Quick, aggressive stop
- **Trap:** Pre-drop brake effect
