# Goal: Scary Demon Voice

## Goal Statement
Create a terrifying, deep, and breathing vocal for dark trap or cinematic horror intros.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **The Text:** Type a short, threatening phrase (e.g., "Welcome to your nightmare").
2. **Voice:** Select **Giant**.
3. **Mode:** Select **Breathy**.
4. **Pitch:** Turn the Pitch slider all the way DOWN (-2 Octaves).
5. **Rate:** Set to 40% (Very slow and deliberate).
6. **Render:** Hit OK.
7. **Mixer FX:**
   - **Fruity Blood Overdrive:** Increase the "Pre-Amp" for grit.
   - **Fruity Reeverb 2:** Large cathedral setting, 40% Wet.
   - **Fruity Delay 3:** Ping-Pong delay to make it "spin" in the head.

## What to Listen For
- A deep, guttural resonance that vibrates the sub-woofer.
- The "breathy" texture adds a sense of "presence" that a standard pitch-shifted vocal lacks.
