# Goal: Vibe - Vibey

## Goal Statement
Soften sharp digital "pokes" to create a smooth, silk-like R&B mix.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Placement:** On a bright synth lead or vocal ad-lib.
2. **Attack:** Turn **Attack** to -4dB.
3. **Release:** Boost **Release** to +2dB.
4. **Mix:** 100%.
5. **Result:** Removes the "digital edge" and makes the sound feel "expensive" and "worn-in."

## Mini-Recipe: "Silk Texture"
- Attack at -3dB.
- Release at 0dB.
- Mix at 50%.
- *Result:* The instrument still has its rhythm, but it doesn't "hurt" the ear in a bright mix.
