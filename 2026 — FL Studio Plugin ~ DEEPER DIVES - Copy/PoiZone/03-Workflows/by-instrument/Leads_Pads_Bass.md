# Workflow: Instrument Recipes

## 1. Leads (The "Hook")
*   **Osc:** Saw + Pulse.
*   **Glide:** ON.
*   **Delay:** Ping Pong.

## 2. Bass (The "Drive")
*   **Osc:** Saw.
*   **Filter:** LP, Res 20%.
*   **Env:** Short Decay.
*   **Mode:** Mono.

## 3. Pads (The "Wash")
*   **Osc:** Saw.
*   **Unison:** 4 Voices.
*   **Attack:** Slow (1-2 seconds).
*   **Release:** Long.
