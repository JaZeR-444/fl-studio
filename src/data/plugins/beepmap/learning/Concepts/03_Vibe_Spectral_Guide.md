# Vibe Spectral Guide: BeepMap

Translating pixel data into specific Hip-Hop and R&B aesthetic targets.

---

## 1. MOODY
*Darkness, weight, and "heavy" blurred atmospheres.*
- **The Lever**: **Low Contrast Grayscale + Long Scan Length + Log Scale**.
- **Move**: Load a photo of a dark forest or a blurred gray gradient. Set Length to 4000ms.
- **What to Listen For**: A muffled, "breathing" drone that slowly shifts. It provides a heavy backdrop that feels organic yet synthetic.
- **Don't Do This**: High-contrast white lines (too "alert").

## 2. UPBEAT
*Energy, high-pitched "pings," and rapid movement.*
- **The Lever**: **High Contrast Pixel Art + Fast Scan + High Base Hz**.
- **Move**: Use a black image with small white dots. Set Length to 200ms.
- **What to Listen For**: Sharp "chirps" and digital "blips" that mimic old video game sound effects. It adds an energetic, playful layer to upbeat Trap.
- **Don't Do This**: Long, slow scans (kills the energy).

## 3. PSYCHEDELIC
*Disorientation, shifting scales, and "melting" vision.*
- **The Lever**: **Full Color Fractals + Blue Channel Active + Looped Scan**.
- **Move**: Load a trippy, colorful fractal or kaleidoscope image. Enable the "Blue" channel.
- **What to Listen For**: The sound will swirl between speakers and shift in harmonic complexity as it hits different colors. It feels dizzying and intense.
- **Don't Do This**: Grayscale images (too predictable).

## 4. JAZZY
*Warmth, subtle focus, and "soulful" resonance.*
- **The Lever**: **Sepia Tones + Minimal Vertical Data + Log Scale**.
- **Move**: Use an image with only 2-3 horizontal lines near the bottom of the canvas. 
- **What to Listen For**: A warm, resonant tone that mimics a vintage synth or an E-Piano. Because there's only a few lines, it sounds "focused" rather than noisy.
- **Don't Do This**: High-frequency noise at the top of the image.

## 5. VIBEY
*Modern luxury, smooth silk, and "expensive" air.*
- **The Lever**: **Soft Gradients + High Scan Resolution + External Reverb**.
- **Move**: Load a soft peach-to-purple gradient. Set Grain to minimum. Add LuxeVerb.
- **What to Listen For**: A "silky" spectral wash that feels like expensive R&B atmosphere. The soft gradients ensure there are no sharp, ugly transients.
- **Don't Do This**: Sharp edges or pixelated text.
