# Goal: Vibe - Jazzy

## Goal Statement
Create a sophisticated, natural-sounding stereo image for organic instruments.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Width:** 45%.
2. **Separation:** Drag toward "Merged."
3. **Low Bypass:** 250Hz.
4. **Mixer Placement:** Use on a mono Piano or electric Guitar.
5. **Result:** Mimics the sound of two microphones placed in a room, giving the instrument a "real" physical space.

## What to Listen For
- A "classy" and "natural" width.
- No synthetic "sheen" or "phasing."
