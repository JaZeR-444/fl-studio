# Leads/Synths Analysis with Fruity Spectroman

## Lead Synth Frequency Profile

**Fundamental:** 200 Hz - 2 kHz (note-dependent)  
**Harmonics:** 2-10 kHz (brightness, character)  
**Air:** 10-16 kHz (sparkle)

---

## Lead Mixing Workflow

### Step 1: Analyze Harmonic Content (2 min)
1. Solo lead synth
2. Play sustained note
3. Observe harmonic distribution:
   - Saw wave: Full harmonics (many peaks)
   - Square: Odd harmonics only
   - Sine: Fundamental only (single peak)

**Check:** Understand synth's spectral character

---

### Step 2: Carve Space for Lead (3 min)
1. Unsolo, play full mix
2. Check where lead sits (usually 500 Hz - 5 kHz)
3. If buried:
   - Boost lead at its dominant frequency
   - OR cut competing elements in that zone

**Check:** Lead visible in its frequency range

---

### Step 3: Control Harshness (2 min)
1. Check for harsh peaks at 3-6 kHz
2. If harsh: Cut resonant frequency
3. Synths can have harsh resonances

**Check:** No bright red spikes causing fatigue

---

### Step 4: Add Brightness (Optional)
1. If dull: Boost 5-10 kHz
2. Adds edge and presence
3. **Check:** Increased content in high-mids

---

## Pads Analysis

**Frequency Profile:**
- Wide range (200 Hz - 12 kHz)
- Fill spectrum without dominating
- Stereo width adds space

### Pad Workflow
1. Check if pads fill gaps in spectrum
2. Should complement, not compete
3. Often wide in stereo (vectorscope oval)
4. High-pass at 200-300 Hz (leave room for bass)

---

## Common Issues

**Lead Buried:**
- Not visible in spectrum
- Boost presence zone or cut competitors

**Harsh Synth:**
- Red spikes at 3-6 kHz
- Cut resonant frequency

**Pads Too Dominant:**
- Taking up too much spectrum
- Reduce level, EQ to support role

---

## Lead Checklist
- [ ] Harmonics visible and balanced
- [ ] Lead sits clearly in mix
- [ ] No harsh resonances
- [ ] Brightness appropriate
- [ ] Pads fill, don't dominate
