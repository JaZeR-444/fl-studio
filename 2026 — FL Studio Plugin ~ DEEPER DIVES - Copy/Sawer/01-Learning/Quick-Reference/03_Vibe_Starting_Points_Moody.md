# 03: Vibe Starting Points - Moody

## Purpose
To create dark, brooding, and "heavy" atmospheres suitable for dark trap or late-night R&B.

## 5 Starting Moves
1. **The Dark LP:** Set Filter to **LP 24dB**. Cutoff at 10% (around 150Hz).
2. **The "Tape" Noise:** Turn **Noise** to 8%. It fills the silence with analog texture.
3. **The Sub Punch:** Set **Sub Level** to Max. Set **Sub Phase** to 180°.
4. **The Slow Drift:** In the Matrix, map **LFO** to **Master Pitch**. Amount: 1%. Speed: 2Hz.
5. **The Chorus Wash:** Turn on **Chorus**. Set Mix to 40%. It makes the sound "uncertain."

## Automation Idea
- **Cutoff Breath:** Slowly automate the **Filter Cutoff** from 5% to 15% and back over 4 bars. It sounds like the bass is "breathing."

## What-to-Listen-For Checklist
- [ ] Is it dark enough to stay out of the way of the lead?
- [ ] Does the noise add a "vintage" quality?
- [ ] Is the low-end solid and consistent?
