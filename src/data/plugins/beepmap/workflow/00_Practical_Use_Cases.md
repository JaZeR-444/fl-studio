# Practical Use Cases

## 1. The "Hidden Message" (Steganography)
**Goal:** Put your logo in the spectrogram.
-   **Image:** Create a black image with your logo in White text.
-   **BeepMap:** Load the image. Set **Scale** to "Linear" (usually maps better to linear spectrograms).
-   **Playback:** Play a long C note.
-   **Verify:** Put "Wave Candy" on the mixer track. Select "Spectrum" mode. You will see your logo scrolling by as the sound plays.

## 2. Eerie Horror Soundscapes
**Goal:** Unsettling background atmosphere.
-   **Image:** Load a picture of fire, smoke, or a nebula.
-   **Controls:**
    -   Scale: **Linear** (for dissonant, non-musical tones).
    -   Loop: On.
    -   Length: Very Slow.
    -   Widen: Max.
-   **Result:** A constantly shifting, unrepeatable wall of sound.

## 3. "Drawing" Automation/Risers
**Goal:** Create a specific sound effect shape.
-   **Image:** Open MS Paint. Draw a Red line starting at bottom-left and curving to top-right.
-   **BeepMap:** Load image.
-   **Result:** You hear a sine wave sweep up (Riser) that pans hard Left (Red).
-   **Variation:** Draw a Green line going down. Now you have a stereo X-cross effect.

## 4. Glitch Drum Loops
**Goal:** Instant IDM textures.
-   **Image:** Load a barcode or a QR code.
-   **Controls:** Length = Fast. Loop = On.
-   **Result:** Rhythmic, clicking, beeping patterns that sync to the scan speed.
