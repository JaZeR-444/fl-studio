# Oscillator Tuning Guide

To make your kick musical:
1. Identify the key of your song (e.g., F minor).
2. Find the root note frequency (F0 = ~43.65Hz).
3. Set BassDrum's **Base** frequency to that value.
4. **Hint Bar:** FL Studio's hint bar shows the Note and Frequency as you turn the knob.
5. **Result:** Your kick tail will resonate perfectly with your bassline.
