# Goal: Hard-Sync Leads

## Goal Statement
Create a "tearing" or "whining" lead sound that uses oscillator synchronization to create complex, shifting harmonics.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Sync:** Turn the **Sync** button ON.
2. **Oscillator:** Play with the **Sync Frequency** (Slave Osc). You will hear the sound change from a "muffled" tone to a "screaming" tone.
3. **LFO:** In the Matrix, route **LFO** to **Oscillator Pitch (Sync)**.
4. **Modulation:** Set the LFO speed to 1/8 note.
5. **Unison:** Add 4 voices of Unison with 10% Detune for thickness.

## What to Listen For
- A "pinched" or "nasal" quality that shifts as you move the sync frequency.
- Harmonic "tearing" that sounds like a wah-wah pedal but more aggressive.

## Automation Ideas
- **Sync Sweep:** Automate the Sync Frequency over a 4-bar phrase. This is the hallmark of the "West Coast Whine."
- **Velocity to Sync:** Map velocity to Sync Frequency so that hitting the key harder makes the lead "scream" more.
