# Goal: Lo-Fi 90s Setup

## Goal Statement
Recreate the "crunchy," limited-bandwidth sound of 90s digital samplers and video game consoles.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **The Bank:** Load a legacy SoundFont (e.g., 8-bit or 12-bit banks from the SNES era).
2. **Resampling:** Switch the Resampling mode to **Crunchy**.
3. **The Filter:** Enable the Filter override. Set **Cut** to 60%. This removes modern "crispness."
4. **Sample Tuning:** Pitch the instrument UP +12 semitones, then play your melody an octave lower. This forces the engine to interpolate and create more "aliasing."
5. **Effects:** Turn on the internal **Reverb**. The metallic quality of legacy reverb adds to the lo-fi vibe.

## Starting Settings
- **Resampling:** Crunchy
- **Filter Cut:** 60%
- **Pitch:** +1200 cents
- **Reverb:** ON

## What to Listen For
- "Digital grit" or a slight "hiss" when high notes are played.
- A sound that feels like it belongs in a 1995 RPG or a gritty Boombap record.
