# Goal: Instrument Group Immersion

## Goal Statement
Apply a subtle spatial glue to a group of instruments (e.g., Guitars, Pads, or Strings) to make them feel cohesive and immersive.

## Step-by-Step Setup
1. **Bus Routing:** Route all melodic instruments to an "Instrument Bus."
2. **Width:** Set **Width** to 25%.
3. **Separation:** Keep at 50% (Default).
4. **Low Bypass:** Set to 200Hz to keep any low-mid "weight" centered.
5. **Visualization:** Look for a "gentle cloud" on the vectorscope.
6. **Automation:** Slowly automate the **Width** to increase by 10% during the chorus.

## Starting Settings
- **Width:** 25%
- **Bypass:** 200Hz
- **Mono:** ON

## Why this works:
Instead of every instrument having a different panning setting, Spreader creates a "unified" stereo field that makes the group sound like it was recorded in the same room.
